Introduction

This Quality System Manual establishes the Quality Management System (QMS) requirements for DS Home Computing Ltd. Which outline the standard work procedures for the quality control program.

This manual specifies the requirements of the QMS for DS Home Computing Ltd.to demonstrate our ability to consistently provide reliable products, processes or services which meet customer and legal requirements. The QMS, Quality Policy and Manual are written for use by DS Home Computing Ltd.

Through the implementation and integration of this QMS into DS Home Computing Ltd. processes, this Quality Manual is used to familiarize individuals, customers and other external organizations with the controls and processes that have been implemented and to assure them that the reliability of our QMS is maintained and is focused on continual improvement and customer satisfaction.

DS Home Computing Ltd.’s QMS meets the requirements of ISO 9001:2015 and uses the Plan, Do, Check and Act approach(PDCA) to process planning.

This document outlines the project turnover plan and workflow for DS Home Computing Ltd projects from the project team to the owner organization, including the roles and responsibilities of different parties within this process. This process works in coordination with the Documentation Standard Work Process to successfully complete a project.

We believe that all services provided must be the highest level of quality for clients. We understand the importance of providing quotes with a prompt response, exceeding the expectations of our clients by achieving scheduled completion dates. We continually invest in our employees to ensure our standards of quality are excellent.

The scope of the quality management system described in this manual is to deliver defect-free products construction services that meet the requirements of our internal and external clients.

Terms and Definitions

This document does not introduce any new definitions but rather relies on the following:

Terms and definitions given in ISO 9000:2015 apply

Management of Change (MOC)- Is a structured approach in an organization for ensuring that changes are smoothly and successfully implemented to achieve lasting benefits

Non-Conformance (NCR) - Any deviation to the applicable Code, client specification, design standard, special requirements, drawing, established procedure or the applicable QA Manual. (i.e any condition whereby the product or service does not meet DS Home Computing Ltd’ or the client’s requirements.)

Internally Generated NCR- An NCR that is created by a DS Home Computing Ltd employee due to a nonconformance

Externally Generated NCR- An NCR that is created by anyone outside of DS Home Computing Ltd due to a nonconformance (i.e Supplier, manufacturer, project owner, etc)

Non-Conformance Report Log- A record of non-conformance

Corrective Action- Steps that are taken to remove the causes of an existing nonconformity and to render product acceptable.

Preventative Action- Action taken to avoid repetition of similar non-conformances including modification or enforcement of procedures or implementation of additional requirements.

Concession- An allowance made which allows a non-conforming item to be accepted without meeting the originally specified requirements.

Control of Externally Provided Processes and Services

General

DS Home Computing Ltd ensures that externally provided processes and services conform to client requirements. DS Home Computing Ltd determines the control applied to externally provided processes and services when:

Services from external providers are intended for incorporation into DS Home Computing Ltd own services;

Services are provided directly to the customer(s) by external providers on behalf of DS Home Computing Ltd; and

A process, or part of a process, is provided by an external provider as a result of a decision by DS Home Computing Ltd. DS Home Computing Ltd determines and applies criteria for the evaluation, selection, monitoring of performance, and re-evaluation of external providers, based on their ability to provide processes and services in accordance with client requirements. DS Home Computing Ltd retains documented information of these activities and any necessary actions arising from the work performed.

Type of Extent Control

DS Home Computing Ltd ensures that externally provided processes and services do not adversely affect DS Home Computing Ltd’s ability to consistently deliver conforming products and services to its customers.

DS Home Computing Ltd:

Ensures that externally provided processes remain within the control of its quality management system;

Defines both the controls that it intends to apply to an external provider and those it intends to apply to the resulting output.

Takes into consideration:

The potential impact of the externally provided processes and services on DS Home Computing Ltd’s ability to consistently meet customer and applicable regulatory requirements;

The effectiveness of the controls applied by the external provider;

Determines the verification, or other activities, necessary to ensure that the externally provided processes and testing services meet requirements.

Control of Nonconforming Outputs

Nonconforming Installations

DS Home Computing Ltd ensures that outputs that do not conform to their requirements are identified and controlled to prevent their unintended use.

DS Home Computing Ltd takes appropriate action based on the nature of the nonconformity and its effect on the conformity of client products and equipment.

DS Home Computing Ltd deals with nonconforming installations in one or more of the following ways:

Correction;

Segregation, containment, until a decision is made from the client;

Informing the customer; and

Obtaining authorization for acceptance under concession.

Conformity to the requirements are verified when nonconforming outputs are corrected.

Nonconformity and Corrective Action

Step for Nonconformity

When a nonconformity occurs, including any arising from complaints, DS Home Computing Ltd:

Reacts to the nonconformity and, as applicable:

Takes action to control and correct it;

Deals with the consequences;

Evaluates the need for action to eliminate the cause(s) of the nonconformity, in order that it does not recur or occur elsewhere, by:

Reviewing and analyzing the nonconformity;

Determining the causes of the nonconformity;

Determining if similar nonconformities exist, or could potentially occur;

Implements any action needed;

Reviews the effectiveness of any corrective action taken;

Updates risks and opportunities determined during planning, if necessary; and

Makes changes to the quality management system, if necessary.

Corrective actions are appropriate to the effects of the nonconformities encountered.

Documentation

DS Home Computing Ltd retains documented information as evidence of:

The nature of the nonconformities and any subsequent actions taken; and

The results of any corrective action.
