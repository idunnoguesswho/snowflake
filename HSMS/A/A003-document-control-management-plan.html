1. Purpose and Scope

This Document Control Management Plan (DCMP) establishes the framework, policies, procedures, and forms governing the creation, identification, review, approval, distribution, storage, retrieval, retention, and disposition of all documented information produced or maintained by DS Home Computing Ltd. (DSHC).

It satisfies the requirements of ISO 9001:2015 Clause 7.5 (Documented Information) and supports alignment with Canadian federal and Alberta provincial legislation, including but not limited to the Occupational Health and Safety Act (Alberta), the Personal Information Protection and Privacy Act (PIPA), and applicable NEB/CER regulatory frameworks.

1.1 Scope of Application

This plan applies to all documented information regardless of format or medium (paper, electronic, or hybrid), across all functional areas of DSHC:

Operations / Facilities Management (OP)

Safety, Security and Environmental (SA)

Human Resources (HU)

Information Management (IN)

Corporate Governance (CO)

Financial Management (FI)

Legal and Regulatory (LE / RE)

Customer Management (CU)

Business Development and Planning (BU)

Public Relations (PU)

Documents generated under construction, commissioning, and EIM project scopes are captured under this plan and supplemented by the project-specific Document Turnover Program (refer to document D.001).

1.2 Objectives

Ensure all documents are created, reviewed, and approved before use

Prevent use of obsolete or superseded documents

Maintain a single source of truth for all master documents

Support ISO 9001:2015 certification and regulatory compliance

Enable efficient document retrieval and business continuity

Provide clear retention, archival, and disposition procedures aligned to the DS Home Retention Schedule

3. Document Control Policy
Policy Statement

DS Home Computing Ltd. is committed to maintaining accurate, current, and accessible documented information that supports the quality management system and protects the interests of clients, employees, regulators, and the public. All documented information shall be controlled at all times throughout its lifecycle.

3.1 Policy Principles

Every document in active use must be at its current revision level

All documents must be approved by an authorized signatory before issue

Documents must be uniquely identified using the DSHC naming convention

Storage locations must ensure protection from damage, loss, or unauthorized access

Obsolete documents must be removed from points of use immediately upon supersession

Personal information within documents must be managed in accordance with PIPA (Alberta)

Electronic documents in the QMS DMS are the master copies; printed copies are uncontrolled unless stamped CONTROLLED

3.3.3 Physical Filing Rules

Physical (paper) controlled documents are filed in colour-coded binders by primary category

Each binder is labelled with the primary code, category, and revision date

Controlled paper copies bear a CONTROLLED stamp with copy number and distribution date

Physical masters are stored in a locked, fire-resistant cabinet in the Document Control office

Obsolete paper documents are shredded upon supersession unless legal hold applies

3.4 Document Lifecycle and Control Procedures

3.4.1 Creation and Drafting

Document Owner initiates a new document request using Form C.DC-001 (Document Request Form)

Template appropriate to the document type is used (master templates held in QMS DMS under IN > IN01 Forms Control)

Document Owner completes all required fields including scope, references, and revision history table

Draft documents are watermarked DRAFT and are not for operational use

3.4.2 Review

All new or revised documents undergo technical review by a Subject Matter Expert (SME)

SME review is documented in Form C.DC-002 (Document Review Record)

Reviewers confirm: accuracy of content, compliance with applicable regulations, alignment with ISO 9001:2015 requirements, and consistency with other QMS documents

Review cycle must be completed within 10 business days of draft issue

3.4.3 Approval

Documents are approved by the authority level defined in the Document Authority Matrix (see Section 4)

Approved documents are assigned a document number if not already assigned

The Document Controller updates the Document Master Register (Form C.DC-003) within 2 business days of approval

Approval signatures (wet or digital) are retained in the Document History file

3.4.4 Issue and Distribution

Controlled electronic copies are distributed via the DMS with access rights set by the Document Controller

Controlled paper copies are issued with a Distribution Record (Form C.DC-004) and stamped CONTROLLED

Uncontrolled copies may be distributed externally but must bear the UNCONTROLLED IF PRINTED watermark

Distribution list is maintained in the Document Master Register

3.4.5 Revision

Any person may request a document revision using Form C.DC-001

Minor changes (editorial, formatting): revision letter suffix (Rev 0a, 0b); no re-approval required if content unchanged

Major changes (content, scope, procedure): full review and approval cycle; revision number increments

All previous revisions are archived in the DMS under a designated Superseded folder

3.4.6 Obsolescence

Upon approval of a new revision, the Document Controller marks the previous revision as OBSOLETE in the DMS

All controlled copies of the obsolete document are retrieved from distribution points within 5 business days

Obsolete paper documents are shredded and recorded in Form C.DC-005 (Obsolete Document Disposal Record)

Obsolete electronic documents are moved to the Superseded archive and access-restricted

5. Personnel Turnover Practices — Knowledge and Document Continuity

The departure of key personnel creates significant risk of institutional knowledge loss. DSHC implements the following practices to preserve document integrity during personnel transitions, aligned with ISO 9001:2015 Clause 7.5.3 (Control of Documented Information) and best practices for operational continuity.

5.1 Document Ownership Review

Sixty (60) calendar days prior to a planned departure, the departing employee's manager triggers a Document Ownership Review using Form C.DC-006 (Document Ownership Transfer Form)

All documents owned by the departing employee are identified in the Document Master Register

Each document is assigned a new Owner within 30 days of notice

For unplanned departures (termination, incapacity), the manager assumes interim ownership immediately and completes the transfer within 10 business days

5.2 Knowledge Transfer Protocol

Transition Meeting: Departing employee meets with successor to review all owned documents, including their purpose, review cycle, and any pending revisions

Document Audit: Successor verifies that all documents are at current revision and complete

Gap Identification: Any identified gaps (missing records, incomplete versions, informal processes not documented) are reported to the Document Controller and Document Request Forms submitted

Handover Record: Form C.DC-007 (Knowledge Handover Record) is completed and filed in the departing employee's personnel file and the QMS document record

Post-Transfer Review: Within 30 days of transition completion, Document Controller audits the affected documents to confirm continuity

5.3 Critical Document Identification

Documents in the following categories are designated Critical Documents and require enhanced turnover controls:

Quality Management System manuals and procedures

Active construction and commissioning data books

Regulatory submissions and correspondence

Client contracts and service agreements

Safety and Emergency Response Plans

Certification and competency records for regulated roles

Financial records under active audit period

Critical Documents must have at minimum two individuals with access and working knowledge at all times (Primary Owner + Backup).

5.4 Contractor and Third-Party Document Continuity

Contractors operating under DSHC project controls are required to designate a document contact as a condition of engagement

Upon contractor demobilization, all project documents are transferred to DSHC in accordance with the Document Turnover Program (D.001)

Third-party originals are scanned and filed in the DMS; originals are retained per the retention schedule

6.3 Disposition Process

Document Controller identifies records approaching end of retention period (quarterly review)

Document Controller consults Document Owner and Legal counsel before authorizing disposal

Disposal Approval Form (C.DC-008) is completed and approved by Branch Manager

Electronic records are permanently deleted from all systems; paper records are cross-cut shredded or placed in a locked shred bin for a certified destruction vendor

Certificate of Destruction is retained for 6 years after disposal

Disposition is logged in the Document Master Register

Legal Hold Exception: If a document is subject to litigation, regulatory inquiry, or audit, the standard retention schedule is suspended and the document is placed under a Legal Hold order. No disposal may occur under Legal Hold without written authorization from the General Manager.
