B.009 · Policy

Duty to Accommodate

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use

Policy brief & purpose

This human resources policy affects all workers on DS Home Computing Ltd sites.

Scope

This policy applies to all our workers.

Policy elements

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to providing equal opportunity and reasonable accommodations to employees with disabilities. DS Home Computing Ltd complies with the Alberta Human Rights Commission Duty to accommodate legislation and all other applicable federal, provincial, and local laws regarding disability discrimination and accommodation.

Reasonable accommodations are available for the known physical or mental limitations of qualified employees with disabilities. An employee is qualified if they can perform essential job functions with or without reasonable accommodation.

For purposes of this policy, a disability is any physical or mental impairment that substantially limits a major life activity.

A reasonable accommodation is a modification or adjustment of an employee's job or work environment that enables that employee to perform essential job functions or enjoy the same employment benefits and privileges as similarly situated employees without disabilities. Examples of reasonable accommodations include: modifying a workspace to make it wheelchair accessible, providing screen reading software, or adjusting an employee's work schedule to accommodate medical appointments. DS Home Computing Ltd. does not provide accommodations of a personal nature, such as eyeglasses or hearing aids.

DS Home Computing Ltd. is committed to providing accommodations so long as accommodations do not place an undue hardship on business operations or pose a threat to the health or safety of employees in the workplace.

ACCOMMODATION PROCESS

DS Home Computing Ltd. will actively engage in an interactive process with employees who request accommodations to determine what, if any, accommodation can be provided. DS Home Computing Ltd. aims to process requests for accommodations in a prompt and efficient manner.

Employees who request accommodations will be asked to have a physician identify in writing the nature of the accommodations required to suit their identified disability.

Once DS Home Computing Ltd. receives accommodation documentation, DS Home Computing Ltd. makes an initial determination about the employee's eligibility for accommodations. DS Home Computing Ltd. can request additional medical information or have an employee's medical information reviewed by a medical expert to make this initial determination. In addition, DS Home Computing Ltd. can ask employees to provide information about their educational qualifications and work experience if their reassignment to another position is considered as an accommodation.

If DS Home Computing Ltd. finds that an employee is eligible for an accommodation, DS Home Computing Ltd. notifies the employee's supervisor. DS Home Computing Ltd. works with the employee's supervisor to examine the essential functions of the employee's job and find what, if any, accommodation can be provided. Determinations regarding accommodations are made jointly by DS Home Computing Ltd. and the employee's supervisors. Such determinations are

Employees who are denied accommodations are notified of the denial and the basis for the denial. Employees can appeal accommodation determination rulings.

Accommodations are reviewed annually. As part of the review, employees can be asked to provide updated medical information to demonstrate that the need for accommodations is ongoing.

Employees who have questions about the accommodation process should contact Doug Senger.

CONFIDENTIALITY

All information obtained by DS Home Computing Ltd. concerning medical conditions or history of employees, including genetic information, is maintained in separate medical files and treated as confidential records that are disclosed only as permitted by law. HR representatives and supervisors who have knowledge of employees’ medical information are prohibited from sharing such information unless others need to be informed.

ANTI-RETALIATION

Retaliation for requesting or being granted a disability accommodation is prohibited. If an employee believes that they are subject to retaliation based on a disability accommodation(s) or a disability accommodation request, they should inform the Human Resources Department or Doug Senger
