Inclement Weather Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use

Policy brief & purpose

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to worker safety, client service, and operational continuity during severe weather and hazardous travel conditions. This policy provides guidance for reporting, decision-making, pay administration, remote work, and work stoppage during inclement weather.

This policy supports the Health and Safety Management System (HSMS) commitment to clear policies, shared responsibility, employee participation, confidentiality, hazard control, fair treatment, training, communication, and continuous improvement.

Scope

This policy applies to employees and subcontractors working at offices, client sites, project sites, in vehicles, outdoors, remotely, or while travelling for work.

Policy elements

  • Employees must use reasonable judgement and notify their supervisor as soon as practical if weather or road conditions may delay or prevent safe attendance.
  • Supervisors must consider road conditions, site hazards, weather warnings, visibility, temperature, wind, lightning, wildfire smoke, flooding, and client/site restrictions before assigning work.
  • No worker is expected to perform work where there is an undue hazard. Workers may refuse dangerous work in accordance with Alberta OHS requirements.
  • Where practical, management may approve remote work, delayed start, early departure, schedule changes, use of banked time, vacation, unpaid leave, or reassignment.
  • Employees who are already at work must follow emergency, evacuation, driving, working alone, and communication procedures during severe weather.
  • Timekeeping and pay will be administered according to applicable employment standards, employment terms, and Company direction.

Definitions

TermPolicy meaning
Inclement weatherWeather or environmental conditions that may affect safe travel, attendance, or work, including snow, ice, extreme cold or heat, storm, lightning, wind, wildfire smoke, flooding, or poor visibility.
Undue hazardA serious and immediate threat to health and safety as understood under Alberta OHS dangerous work requirements.

Responsibilities

RoleResponsibilities
Managers / OwnersProvide resources, approve reasonable exceptions, enforce expectations, maintain records, and ensure policy decisions comply with Alberta requirements.
Supervisors / Project ManagersCommunicate expectations, monitor compliance, respond to concerns, document issues, and escalate matters that may affect safety, privacy, payroll, or employment status.
Workers / SubcontractorsRead and follow the policy, ask questions when unsure, report hazards or concerns, cooperate with investigations, and protect confidential information.
Human ResourcesSupport consistent application, maintain confidential records, advise on legislation and accommodation, and coordinate training or corrective action where required.

Communication expectations

  • Check messages, phone, email, and supervisor instructions when severe weather is forecast.
  • Report delays or safety concerns before the scheduled start where possible.
  • Do not drive for work if conditions create an unreasonable risk. Contact a supervisor for direction.
  • Document weather-related schedule changes on time records as directed.

Reporting, review, and corrective action

Employees and subcontractors are encouraged to raise concerns early through their supervisor, Project Manager, Human Resources Manager, or owner. Concerns involving a supervisor may be reported directly to Human Resources or ownership. The Company will respond fairly, promptly, and confidentially to the extent reasonably possible.

Breaches of this policy may result in coaching, retraining, written warning, suspension, loss of privileges, removal from site, termination of employment or contract, or other corrective action appropriate to the circumstances. The Company will not tolerate retaliation against a person who raises a good-faith concern or participates in a review or investigation.

Confidentiality and records

Records created under this policy will be kept only as long as required for employment, safety, payroll, accounting, investigation, insurance, legal, or business purposes. Personal employee information will be collected, used, disclosed, stored, and destroyed in a reasonable manner and shared only with persons who have a legitimate need to know or where required by law.

Training, communication, and review

This policy will be communicated during orientation, when assigned duties make it relevant, and when changes occur. Supervisors and managers are responsible for applying the policy consistently. This policy will be reviewed when legislation changes, when an incident or audit shows improvement is needed, or at least every three (3) years as part of HSMS administration.
