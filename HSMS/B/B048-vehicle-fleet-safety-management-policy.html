Vehicle Fleet Safety Management Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use

Policy brief & purpose

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to safe, legal, and responsible operation of Company-owned, leased, rented, client-provided, and personally owned vehicles used for Company business. This policy establishes driver authorization, vehicle inspection, maintenance, reporting, safe driving, and record requirements.

This policy supports the Health and Safety Management System (HSMS) commitment to hazard assessment, hazard control, fit for work, employee participation, shared responsibility, training, confidentiality, and continuous improvement.

Scope

This policy applies to all employees, subcontractors, supervisors, managers, and authorized drivers who operate a vehicle for Company business, travel between jobs, transport people or materials, tow trailers, use rented vehicles, or use personal vehicles for approved work purposes.

Policy elements

  • Only authorized, licensed, fit-for-work, and adequately trained workers may operate vehicles for Company business.
  • Drivers must comply with traffic laws, client rules, site rules, road bans, load securement requirements, hours of service requirements where applicable, and Company policies.
  • Vehicles must be suitable for the task, maintained in safe operating condition, and inspected before use.
  • Seatbelts must be worn by all occupants. Drivers must not permit unsafe passenger conduct, distracted driving, impairment, unsecured loads, or unsafe vehicle operation.
  • Drivers must immediately report collisions, damage, defects, tickets, licence restrictions, suspensions, near misses, unsafe road conditions, or concerns that may affect driving safety.
  • Company vehicles may be used only for approved Company business unless management authorizes another use in writing.
  • Drivers are responsible for basic vehicle security, including locking vehicles, securing tools and equipment, protecting keys, and not leaving confidential documents exposed.

Driver authorization and minimum requirements

  • Maintain a valid driver licence for the class of vehicle being operated.
  • Inform the Company promptly of any licence restriction, suspension, medical restriction, ticket, collision, or condition that may affect driving duties.
  • Complete orientation, vehicle-specific instruction, 360 before driving, and other required training before driving.
  • Do not drive when impaired by fatigue, alcohol, drugs, medication, illness, distraction, emotional distress, or other conditions that may affect safety.

Vehicle inspection and maintenance

  • Complete a pre-use inspection before operating a vehicle and document it when required.
  • Do not operate a vehicle with a defect that makes it unsafe or illegal to drive.
  • Remove unsafe vehicles from service, tag or report them as required, and notify the supervisor immediately.
  • Keep vehicles reasonably clean, fuelled or charged, and ready for safe use.
  • Ensure tools, equipment, materials, cargo, ladders, and documents are secured before travel.

Prohibited conduct

  • Texting, emailing, reading, browsing, programming a device, or handling a phone while driving.
  • Speeding, aggressive driving, stunt driving, racing, or driving in a way that endangers people or property.
  • Transporting unauthorized passengers, unauthorized cargo, firearms, illegal substances, or other prohibited items.
  • Tampering with telematics, dash cameras, safety equipment, seatbelts, inspection records, or maintenance records.
  • Using a Company vehicle for personal purposes without written approval.

Collision or incident response

  • Stop safely and protect people from immediate danger.
  • Call emergency services if anyone may be injured or if hazards require emergency response.
  • Notify the supervisor as soon as safe to do so.
  • Do not admit fault or make statements on behalf of the Company.
  • Exchange legally required information and document facts, photos, witnesses, location, weather, and damage where safe and appropriate.

Reporting, review, and corrective action

Employees and subcontractors must promptly report unsafe conditions, vehicle or equipment defects, fatigue concerns, journey concerns, incidents, near misses, injuries, damage, fines, tickets, collisions, and policy breaches to their supervisor, Project Manager, Human Resources Manager, or owner.

The Company will review reported concerns, implement corrective actions where required, and document outcomes. Corrective action may include coaching, retraining, revised schedules, removal from driving duties, loss of vehicle privileges, written warning, suspension, termination, or contractor removal depending on the circumstances.

Confidentiality and records

Records created under this policy will be kept only as long as required for safety, employment, payroll, accounting, insurance, vehicle maintenance, investigation, legal, or business purposes. Personal employee information will be collected, used, disclosed, stored, and destroyed in a reasonable manner and shared only with persons who have a legitimate need to know or where required by law.

Training, communication, and review

This policy will be communicated during orientation, before a worker is assigned affected duties, when a change in work creates additional risk, and when changes occur. Supervisors and managers are responsible for applying the policy consistently. This policy will be reviewed when legislation changes, after a significant incident or audit finding, when operations change, or at least every three (3) years as part of HSMS administration.
