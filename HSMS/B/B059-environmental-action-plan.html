ENV-PLAN-001Environmental Action Plan

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes that effective environmental performance requires structured planning, measurable actions, and continuous improvement. This Environmental Action Plan supports the commitments outlined in the Environmental Policy by identifying priority focus areas and actions intended to reduce environmental impacts across DS Home Computing Ltd operations.

This plan provides a practical framework to guide environmental initiatives while allowing flexibility to adapt to varying operational contexts, jurisdictions, and evolving best practices as DS Home Computing Ltd expands its geographic footprint.

Purpose

The purpose of this Environmental Action Plan is to establish a structured approach for implementing environmental initiatives that reduce resource consumption, minimize waste, and support sustainable operations in alignment with DS Home Computing Ltd’s Environmental Policy and ESG objectives.

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Approve environmental priorities and initiatives

Allocate resources to support environmental action planning

Review progress and outcomes

Safety / ESG Coordinator

Coordinate environmental initiatives and performance tracking

Support monitoring, reporting, and continuous improvement

Supervisors

Support implementation of applicable environmental actions at the project and operational level

Promote environmentally responsible practices within their teams

Workers

Follow environmental practices and procedures

Participate in training and awareness initiatives

Identify and report environmental concerns or improvement opportunities

Scope

This Environmental Action Plan applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd operations, including office-based and field activities, and to all employees, contractors, and subcontractors in all countries where DS Home Computing Ltd operates.

Terms and Definitions

Environmental Action PlanA structured plan that outlines initiatives and actions designed to reduce environmental impacts and support sustainable operations.

SustainabilityThe practice of meeting current needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

Plan

DS Home Computing Ltd will implement and maintain environmental initiatives in the following focus areas, where applicable and practicable:

Energy Efficiency

Encourage energy-efficient practices in office and field operations

Support the use of renewable or lower-emission energy sources where feasible

Identify opportunities to reduce energy consumption through improved planning and technology

Waste Management

Promote proper segregation, handling, and disposal of waste in accordance with applicable regulations

Encourage reduction, reuse, and recycling practices

Support employee awareness of responsible waste management

Water Conservation

Promote responsible water use in facilities and field activities

Identify opportunities to reduce water consumption and prevent waste

Sustainable Procurement

Encourage the selection of suppliers and materials with reduced environmental impact

Promote the use of recycled, reusable, or environmentally preferable products where available

Transportation and Travel

Encourage fuel-efficient travel practices and route optimization

Promote alternatives to unnecessary travel, including digital collaboration tools

Communication and Reporting

Monitor and communicate progress on environmental initiatives internally

Support external environmental reporting where required or appropriate

Training

DS Home Computing Ltd will provide environmental awareness training and information, as appropriate, to support the effective implementation of this plan.
