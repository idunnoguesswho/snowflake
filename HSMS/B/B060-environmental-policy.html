ENV-POL-001Environmental Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes that environmental stewardship is a fundamental component of responsible business operations and long-term sustainability. As a commissioning-focused organization operating across multiple countries and environments, DS Home Computing Ltd acknowledges that its activities may have direct and indirect impacts on the natural environment.

This Environmental Policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to protecting the environment, preventing pollution, responsibly managing resources, and continually improving environmental performance across all operations, projects, and business relationships.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define DS Home Computing Ltd’s environmental commitments and principles and to provide a consistent framework for environmental responsibility that supports sustainable operations, regulatory compliance, and continual improvement.

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Provide leadership and resources to support environmental objectives

Ensure environmental considerations are integrated into business planning and decision-making

Approve environmental goals, targets, and initiatives

Safety / ESG Coordinator

Support environmental performance monitoring and reporting

Maintain environmental documentation and records

Coordinate environmental reviews and continuous improvement efforts

Supervisors

Implement environmental requirements within operations and projects

Promote environmentally responsible practices at worksites

Report environmental incidents, risks, or non-conformances

Workers

Comply with environmental requirements and procedures

Participate in environmental training as required

Report environmental concerns or incidents promptly

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd operations, including employees, contractors, subcontractors, and business partners, in all countries where DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business.

Terms and Definitions

Environmental SustainabilityResponsible interaction with the environment to avoid depletion or degradation of natural resources and to support long-term ecological balance.

Pollution PreventionThe use of processes, practices, materials, or energy that avoid, reduce, or control pollution at the source.

Scope 1, 2, and 3 EmissionsGreenhouse gas emissions categorized as direct emissions (Scope 1), indirect emissions from purchased energy (Scope 2), and other indirect emissions occurring across the value chain (Scope 3).

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to conducting its operations in a manner that protects the environment and supports sustainable development. This commitment includes:

Environmental Protection and Compliance

Complying with applicable international, national, regional, and local environmental laws and regulations

Adopting recognized environmental best practices where feasible

Preventing pollution and minimizing adverse environmental impacts

Resource Use and Waste Management

Reducing the consumption of natural resources, including energy, water, and materials

Promoting reuse, recycling, and responsible waste disposal

Minimizing waste generation and encouraging environmentally responsible behaviors

Climate and Emissions Responsibility

Monitoring and managing greenhouse gas emissions where applicable

Supporting initiatives to reduce emissions associated with operations, travel, and supply chains

Aligning environmental objectives with long-term emissions reduction goals

Sustainable Operations and Assets

Managing assets and operations in a manner that protects land, air, and water

Supporting biodiversity protection where operations may interact with natural environments

Encouraging environmentally responsible use and maintenance of facilities and equipment

Sustainable Procurement

Encouraging suppliers and business partners to demonstrate environmental responsibility

Considering environmental impacts in procurement decisions where practicable

Awareness and Communication

Communicating environmental commitments to employees, contractors, and relevant stakeholders

Promoting environmental awareness and engagement across the organization

Training

DS Home Computing Ltd will provide environmental awareness and sustainability training where appropriate to support the effective implementation of this policy.
