ENV-POL-002Greenhouse Gas (GHG) Emissions Reduction Targets

Introduction

DS Home Computing Electrical and Instrumentation Corp. (DS Home Computing) recognizes the global importance of mitigating greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and is committed to contributing meaningfully to climate change mitigation through measurable action. As a commissioning company operating primarily within the oil and gas sector in Canada and the United States, DS Home Computing has a responsibility to monitor, manage, and reduce emissions associated with its operations and supply chain.

At this time, DS Home Computing does not own or control any of its office locations or worksites. The company operates out of leased facilities in Edmonton, Alberta, and Houston, Texas, and provides commissioning services at client-controlled and temporary project sites. Because of this, DS Home Computing has limited ability to influence building structure, base energy systems, or infrastructure-related emissions. However, the company is fully committed to taking practical, achievable, and verifiable actions within its direct operational control to reduce emissions related to office activities, transportation, procurement, waste, and energy use.

This policy establishes quantitative, time-bound, and verifiable targets to reduce Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 GHG emissions, along with a corporate intensity reduction target. These targets align with the Paris Agreement and support DS Home Computing’ long-term goal of achieving net-zero emissions by 2050.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish measurable, time-bound, and transparent greenhouse gas (GHG) reduction targets across all DS Home Computing operations. It defines corporate-level quantitative goals that guide the company’s approach to reducing emissions in Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 categories, as well as monitoring overall emissions intensity.

DS Home Computing aims to strengthen its commitment to environmental responsibility, support its sustainability objectives, and meet both client and regulatory expectations for climate accountability. Progress toward these goals will be monitored, verified, and reported to ensure transparency and continual improvement.

Because DS Home Computing operates from leased facilities and client-controlled sites, the company does not have the authority to make structural or infrastructure-related changes to buildings or energy systems. As a result, DS Home Computing will focus on achievable and measurable actions within its operational control—such as optimizing energy efficiency in leased spaces, reducing vehicle emissions, minimizing business travel, and collaborating with clients and landlords to encourage lower-emission practices.

Roles & Responsibilities

Management/Supervisors

Approve emission reduction targets and ensure sufficient resourcing for implementation.

Review annual performance and integrate emission reduction objectives into company strategy.

Sustainability Lead

Develop and maintain the corporate GHG inventory and monitor progress.

Track progress, verify results, and publish findings in ESG or Sustainability Reports.

Ensure accuracy and compliance with recognized standards.

Supervisors and Leads

Promote field level initiatives to minimize emissions

Workers

Participate in reduction initiatives (carpooling, hybrid work, waste reduction).

Follow sustainable practices in day-to-day operations.

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing operations in Canada and the USA, including:

Corporate offices in Edmonton, Alberta and Houston, Texas.

Company-owned vehicles, portable equipment, and generators.

Business travel, employee commuting, procurement, and waste generation.

Temporary client-controlled project sites where DS Home Computing maintains influence or tracking capability.

Because DS Home Computing does not own or manage its physical office buildings or client work sites, this policy focuses on areas where emissions are within DS Home Computing’ direct or indirect operational control.

Terms and Definitions

Scope 1 Emissions: Direct GHG emissions from sources owned or controlled by DS Home Computing (e.g., company vehicles, generators, natural gas heating).

Scope 2 Emissions: Indirect GHG emissions from the generation of purchased or acquired electricity, heating, cooling, or steam used in DS Home Computing’ offices.

Scope 3 Emissions: Other indirect GHG emissions from value chain activities (e.g., purchased goods and services, business travel, commuting, waste).

tCO₂e (Tonnes of Carbon Dioxide Equivalent): Standard unit used to express the global warming potential of GHGs.

Absolute Reduction Target: A fixed reduction in total emissions irrespective of company growth.

Intensity Reduction Target: Reduction in emissions per unit of activity (e.g., per labour hour or dollar of revenue).

Baseline Year: The benchmark year for measuring reductions — 2025 is DS Home Computing’ baseline year.

Procedures

Scope 1 – Direct Emissions Target (Absolute)

Target:

Reduce total Scope 1 emissions by 15%, from 165 tCO₂e in 2025 to 140 tCO₂e by 2030.

Sources Covered:

Fuel consumption from company-owned vehicles, generators, and portable equipment.

Actions:

Implement anti-idling procedures and driver efficiency training.

Track and report vehicle fuel use through centralized GHG logs.

Optimize travel routes to reduce mileage and emissions.

Verification:

Fuel usage will be converted to CO₂e using Environment and Climate Change Canada and U.S. EPA emission factors.

Scope 2 – Purchased Energy Target (Absolute)

Target:

Reduce total Scope 2 emissions by 25%, from 210 tCO₂e in 2025 to 158 tCO₂e by 2030.

Sources Covered:

Electricity and natural gas consumption in leased DS Home Computing offices.

Actions:

Conduct annual energy audits to identify reduction opportunities.

Install or request high-efficiency lighting and smart energy controls within leased spaces, where permitted.

Transition to 100% renewable electricity procurement by 2029 through available utility programs or renewable energy certificates.

Promote hybrid work arrangements and remote meetings to reduce office energy consumption.

Clarification:

DS Home Computing does not own or control the buildings in which its offices operate and therefore cannot make structural modifications such as upgrading HVAC systems, insulation, or building envelopes. The company will, however, collaborate with landlords to encourage feasible improvements and focus on operational measures within its control.

Verification:

Energy usage will be tracked via utility bills and converted to tCO₂e using regional emission factors.

Scope 3 – Indirect Value Chain Emissions Target (Absolute)

Target:

Reduce total Scope 3 emissions by 10%, from 690 tCO₂e in 2025 to 620 tCO₂e by 2030.

Sources Covered:

Business travel, commuting, procurement, waste generation, and third-party logistics.

Actions:

Minimize non-essential travel through virtual collaboration tools.

Engage suppliers and subcontractors with documented sustainability practices.

Achieve 90% waste diversion at leased office locations by 2028.

Encourage employee carpooling and the use of lower-emission transport options.

Verification:

Scope 3 emissions will be calculated annually using the GHG Protocol’s Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard.

Corporate Intensity Reduction Target

Target:

Reduce overall GHG intensity by 20%, from 0.045 tCO₂e per labour hour in 2025 to 0.036 tCO₂e per labour hour by 2030.

Scope:

Covers total emissions across Scopes 1, 2, and 3, normalized by total labour hours.

Actions:

Integrate emissions tracking into project reporting through Salus and corporate systems.

Incorporate sustainability metrics into project closeout and internal performance reviews.

Encourage efficiency through travel reduction, improved planning, and responsible procurement.

Verification:

Calculated annually as total emissions divided by labour hours, verified through payroll and project data.

Performance Tracking and Reporting

Annual performance will be summarized in DS Home Computing’ ESG or Sustainability Report and may be subject to third-party verification.

All supporting documentation, including fuel logs, energy data, and supplier reports, will be maintained in the ESG document management system.

Training

At this time, company-wide training specific to GHG reduction is not mandatory for all personnel, as most DS Home Computing employees and contractors are engaged on a project-to-project basis and may not have direct involvement in emissions reporting or management activities.

General awareness of DS Home Computing’ ESG commitments and environmental practices will be incorporated into onboarding and safety orientation sessions where applicable. Personnel directly involved in data collection, fleet management, procurement, and ESG reporting will receive focused instruction relevant to their responsibilities.

Training materials and frequency will evolve as the company’s emissions reduction program matures and additional opportunities for engagement are identified.

2024-2026 Regulatory Update - Federal GHG Cap Regulations

The Government of Canada published proposed Oil and Gas Sector Greenhouse Gas Emissions Cap Regulations in the Canada Gazette (Part I, November 9, 2024). These proposed regulations establish a federal cap-and-trade system for the oil and gas sector, layered on top of existing provincial systems (Alberta TIER, federal OBPS). DS Home Computing must monitor finalization and assess obligations for any client sites where DS Home Computing has operational influence over GHG-emitting activities.

Federal GHG Cap Key Thresholds: Operators producing 30,000 or more barrels of oil equivalent per month from January 2024 onward must report emissions and production for 2026 by June 1, 2027. Applies to onshore/offshore oil and gas production, oil sands, natural gas processing, and LNG. These requirements are in addition to Alberta TIER and federal OBPS obligations.

Federal Facility GHG Reporting Program 2024/2025: Federal GHG reporting requirements for 2024/2025 data were updated (Canada Gazette, December 9, 2023 Notice). Facilities emitting 50,000 tCO2e or more per year must report. DS Home Computing corporate Scope 1 and 2 emissions from office operations are below this threshold. Scope 3 emissions from client commissioning activities should be tracked for ESG reporting transparency and client disclosure requests.
