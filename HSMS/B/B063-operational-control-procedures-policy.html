ENV-PROC-002Operational Control Procedures Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes that effective operational controls are essential to managing significant environmental aspects and ensuring compliance with applicable environmental requirements. Operational controls provide the practical link between environmental commitments and day-to-day activities, helping to prevent environmental harm and promote consistent, responsible practices across all operations.

This policy establishes expectations for implementing and maintaining operational control procedures to manage environmental risks related to waste, emissions, and resource use.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define DS Home Computing Ltd’s approach to operational environmental controls by:

Managing significant environmental aspects

Supporting compliance with environmental regulations

Promoting consistent and sustainable operational practices

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Ensure operational control procedures are implemented and maintained

Provide resources to support effective environmental controls

Support continuous improvement initiatives

Safety / ESG Coordinator

Provide guidance on operational control requirements

Support monitoring, documentation, and review activities

Evaluate trends and improvement opportunities

Supervisors

Oversee the implementation of operational control procedures at sites and projects

Ensure personnel are informed of applicable requirements

Address non-conformances and implement corrective actions

Workers

Follow established operational control procedures

Participate in training and awareness initiatives

Report environmental hazards or control failures

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and business partners across all operations, sites, and projects in all countries where DS Home Computing Ltd operates.

Terms and Definitions

Operational ControlA documented process or practice implemented to manage a significant environmental aspect and reduce associated risks.

Significant Environmental AspectAn element of an organization’s activities, products, or services that has, or can have, a significant impact on the environment.

EmissionsReleases of substances, including greenhouse gases or air contaminants, into the atmosphere as a result of operational activities.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd shall establish and maintain operational control procedures appropriate to the nature and level of environmental risk.

Waste Management Controls

Waste shall be segregated at the point of generation where feasible

Waste shall be stored in designated, clearly labeled containers

Hazardous waste shall be stored and handled in accordance with regulatory requirements

Licensed waste carriers and authorized disposal facilities shall be used where required

Waste handling and disposal activities shall be documented

Emissions Control

Emissions associated with operations shall be monitored where applicable

Measures shall be implemented to reduce emissions where feasible

Emissions data shall be recorded and reported in accordance with regulatory and client requirements

Resource Conservation

Energy-efficient practices and technologies shall be encouraged

Water use shall be managed responsibly and leaks addressed promptly

Material use shall be optimized to minimize waste

Recycling practices shall be supported where feasible

Training

Operational control requirements shall be communicated through applicable orientation, training, and awareness programs for employees and contractors.
