ENV-PROC-004Environmental Inspections and Audits and Record Management

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes that regular environmental inspections, audits, and effective record management are essential to ensuring regulatory compliance, identifying environmental risks, and supporting continual improvement. These activities provide assurance that environmental controls are effective and that environmental commitments are being implemented consistently across operations.

This procedure establishes a structured approach for conducting environmental inspections and audits and for managing environmental records across all DS Home Computing Ltd locations.

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to define requirements for:

Conducting environmental inspections and audits

Identifying and addressing environmental hazards and non-conformances

Managing and retaining environmental records

Supporting compliance, transparency, and continuous improvement

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Ensure environmental inspections and audits are supported and resourced

Review significant findings and corrective actions

Safety / ESG Coordinator

Coordinate environmental inspection and audit processes

Maintain audit protocols and guidance

Track findings, corrective actions, and trends

Support record retention and document control

Supervisors

Ensure environmental inspections are conducted as required

Address findings and implement corrective actions

Maintain site-level environmental records

Workers

Cooperate with inspections and audits

Follow environmental requirements and controls

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd operations, projects, and facilities, including office and field activities, in all countries where DS Home Computing Ltd operates.

Terms and Definitions

Environmental InspectionA routine, systematic examination of a worksite or operation to identify environmental hazards, risks, or non-compliance.

Environmental AuditA formal, documented evaluation of environmental management practices and compliance against defined criteria, standards, or legal requirements.

Environmental RecordDocumented evidence demonstrating environmental activities, compliance, inspections, audits, incidents, training, or corrective actions.

Procedure

Environmental Inspections and Audits

Environmental inspections shall be conducted at a frequency appropriate to the nature and risk of the operation and in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.

Inspections may be conducted in conjunction with broader HSE inspections where appropriate.

Environmental audits may be conducted periodically to assess compliance with environmental policies, procedures, and applicable requirements.

Findings from inspections and audits shall be documented and corrective actions identified and tracked to completion.

Environmental Records Management

DS Home Computing Ltd shall maintain environmental records as evidence of compliance and environmental performance. Records may include, but are not limited to:

Permits and approvals

Training and competency records

Inspection and audit records

Environmental incident and spill reports

Waste and hazardous materials records

Regulatory correspondence and reports

Site-specific environmental documentation

Environmental records shall be retained in accordance with applicable legal, regulatory, client, and DS Home Computing Ltd document control requirements. Where no specific retention period is defined, records shall be retained for a minimum of three (3) years.

Review and Follow-Up

Inspection and audit findings shall be reviewed to identify trends and systemic issues.

Corrective actions shall be implemented and monitored for effectiveness.

Lessons learned may be communicated to support continuous improvement.

Training

Environmental inspection, audit, and record management requirements will be included in applicable training and awareness programs.
