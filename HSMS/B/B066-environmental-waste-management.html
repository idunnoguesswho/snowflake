ENV-PROC-005Environmental Waste Management

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to responsible waste management as part of its broader environmental stewardship and sustainability objectives. Although the nature of DS Home Computing Ltd’s work typically generates limited waste, improper handling or disposal can result in environmental harm, regulatory non-compliance, and reputational risk.

This Environmental Waste Management Policy establishes clear expectations for minimizing waste generation, managing waste responsibly, and supporting sustainable practices across all DS Home Computing Ltd operations and locations.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to responsible waste management practices that:

Minimize environmental impact

Support regulatory compliance

Promote waste reduction, reuse, and recycling

Align with applicable client, contractor, and regulatory requirements

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Ensure waste management practices align with regulatory and client requirements

Provide resources and support for effective waste management

Support continuous improvement initiatives

Safety / ESG Coordinator

Provide guidance on waste management requirements

Support monitoring, documentation, and continuous improvement

Review waste-related performance trends

Supervisors

Oversee waste management practices at worksites

Ensure waste segregation, storage, and disposal requirements are followed

Provide guidance and support to workers

Workers

Follow waste management requirements and procedures

Participate in applicable training

Support waste minimization and recycling efforts

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and business partners across all operations and projects in all countries where DS Home Computing Ltd operates. It applies to all waste types, including hazardous and non-hazardous waste.

Terms and Definitions

Waste MinimizationActions taken to reduce the quantity of waste generated through efficient use of materials and resources.

Waste SegregationThe separation of waste into distinct categories (e.g., recyclable, non-recyclable, hazardous) at the point of generation.

Hazardous WasteWaste that poses actual or potential risks to human health or the environment due to its chemical, physical, or biological properties.

Licensed Waste CarrierAn authorized organization permitted to transport waste in accordance with applicable regulatory requirements.

Authorized Waste Disposal FacilityA facility approved by regulatory authorities to treat, recycle, or dispose of waste materials.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to managing waste responsibly and sustainably across its operations.

Waste Minimization and Reduction

DS Home Computing Ltd will:

Promote efficient use of materials

Encourage reuse and recycling where feasible

Support continuous improvement in waste management practices

Where practicable, DS Home Computing Ltd may establish environmental objectives related to waste reduction and emissions associated with waste management activities.

Waste Segregation and Storage

Waste shall be segregated at the point of generation where feasible

Designated, clearly labeled containers shall be used

Hazardous waste shall be stored in accordance with regulatory requirements

Waste Transportation

Licensed waste carriers shall be used where required

Waste shall be secured during transport to prevent spillage or leakage

Waste Disposal

Waste shall be disposed of in accordance with applicable local, regional, and national regulations

Authorized disposal facilities shall be used

Waste disposal documentation shall be retained as required

Compliance and Documentation

Compliance with applicable waste management regulations is mandatory

Records related to waste handling, transport, and disposal shall be maintained

Continuous Improvement

Waste management practices shall be reviewed periodically

Lessons learned and feedback shall be incorporated into ongoing improvements

Training

Waste management awareness and requirements shall be included in applicable onboarding and training programs for employees and contractors.
