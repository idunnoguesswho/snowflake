B.068 · POLICY

Business Continuity Plan

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-GOV-PLAN-001Business Continuity Plan

Purpose

The purpose of this Business Continuity Plan (BCP) is to ensure DS Home Computing Ltd can continue or rapidly resume critical business operations following a disruption, while protecting employees, clients, assets, and reputation.

Scope

This plan applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd operations, offices, projects, and employees across all countries of operation.

Continuity Objectives

Protect employee health and safety

Maintain critical business functions

Minimize service disruption to clients

Safeguard assets, information, and reputation

Enable orderly recovery and resumption of operations

Critical Business Functions

The following functions are considered critical and prioritized during disruptions:

Health, Safety, and Emergency Response

Client communications and project support

Payroll and financial operations

IT systems and data access

Supply chain coordination

Regulatory and contractual compliance

Roles & Responsibilities

Senior Management

Declare business continuity events

Approve recovery priorities and resource allocation

Business Continuity Lead (BC Lead)

Coordinate continuity response

Maintain this plan and supporting documentation

Liaise with incident response and recovery teams

Department Leads

Identify department-specific critical activities

Implement continuity strategies within their functions

Workers and Contractors

Follow continuity instructions

Maintain communication with supervisors

Business Continuity Strategies

Continuity strategies may include:

Remote work arrangements

Temporary relocation of work locations

Reallocation of personnel and resources

Use of alternate suppliers or service providers

Manual workarounds for disrupted systems

Activation Criteria

This plan may be activated when:

A disruption significantly impacts operations for more than 24 hours

Critical systems or facilities are unavailable

A significant safety, environmental, cyber, or natural event occurs

Review

This plan shall be reviewed every three (3) years, or sooner if there are major incidents, audits, or organizational changes.
