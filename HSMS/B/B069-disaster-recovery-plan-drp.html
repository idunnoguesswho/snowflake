Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-GOV-PLAN-002Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)

Purpose

The Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) outlines how DS Home Computing Ltd will restore IT systems, data, and digital infrastructure following a disruption.

Scope

This plan applies to:

Corporate and project IT systems

Cloud services and data storage

Communication platforms

Digital records and documentation

Recovery Objectives

Recovery Time Objective (RTO): Restore critical systems within acceptable timeframes

Recovery Point Objective (RPO): Minimize data loss through backups and redundancy

Roles & Responsibilities

IT / Systems Lead

Coordinate system restoration

Verify data integrity and security

Business Continuity Lead

Align IT recovery with business priorities

Third-Party Providers

Support system recovery per contractual arrangements

Recovery Strategies

Regular system backups (cloud and/or offsite)

Secure backup verification and testing

Redundant systems where feasible

Alternative communication platforms

Disaster Recovery Phases

Assessment

Identify impacted systems and data

Containment

Prevent further damage or data loss

Restoration

Restore systems from verified backups

Validation

Confirm functionality and data integrity

Return to Normal Operations

Resume standard system use

Testing and Review

Backup restoration tests conducted periodically

Plan reviewed every 3 years or after major incidents
