ESG-GOV-POL-001General ESG Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to integrating Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) principles into its business strategy, operations, and decision-making processes. ESG considerations support responsible growth, long-term value creation, and accountability to employees, clients, communities, and other stakeholders.

This General ESG Policy establishes the overarching framework that guides DS Home Computing Ltd’s approach to environmental stewardship, social responsibility, and ethical governance across all jurisdictions in which the organization operates.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to define DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to ESG principles and to provide a consistent governance framework that:

Supports sustainable business practices

Promotes responsible environmental and social performance

Upholds ethical conduct, transparency, and accountability

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Provide leadership and resources to support ESG initiatives

Approve ESG objectives and monitor progress

Ensure ESG considerations are integrated into strategic and operational decisions

Safety / ESG Coordinator

Coordinate ESG programs and reporting

Monitor ESG performance and emerging risks

Support audits, assessments, and continuous improvement

Supervisors

Support ESG initiatives at the operational and project level

Promote responsible and sustainable practices within their teams

Ensure applicable ESG requirements are communicated and followed

Workers

Comply with applicable ESG policies and procedures

Participate in required training and awareness initiatives

Contribute to continuous improvement of ESG performance

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and business partners across all operations, projects, and geographic locations in which DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business.

Artificial Intelligence systems shall not replace competent person authority in safety-critical decision-making.

Terms and Definitions

Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG)A framework used to evaluate an organization’s impact on the environment, society, and governance practices.

Sustainable DevelopmentDevelopment that meets present needs without compromising the ability of future generations to meet their own needs.

Net-ZeroAchieving a balance between greenhouse gas emissions produced and those removed from the atmosphere.

Diversity and InclusionThe practice of fostering a workplace that values individual differences and promotes equal opportunity and respect.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to conducting business responsibly and sustainably through the following ESG pillars:

Environmental Stewardship

DS Home Computing Ltd will:

Promote responsible resource use and environmental protection

Support initiatives to reduce emissions, waste, and environmental impacts

Comply with applicable environmental laws and regulations

Continually improve environmental performance

Social Responsibility

DS Home Computing Ltd will:

Respect internationally recognized human rights

Promote diversity, equity, and inclusion

Support safe and healthy workplaces

Engage respectfully with communities and Indigenous peoples

Governance and Ethics

DS Home Computing Ltd will:

Uphold ethical business conduct and compliance with laws

Maintain transparent governance and accountability structures

Protect information and manage cyber and data risks

Prevent corruption, bribery, and unethical practices

Training

DS Home Computing Ltd will provide ESG-related training and awareness programs, as appropriate, to support compliance with this policy. Training may include environmental awareness, human rights, diversity and inclusion, ethics, cybersecurity, and governance topics.
