Ethics Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-GOV-POL-002Ethics Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to conducting all business activities with honesty, integrity, fairness, and accountability. Ethical conduct is fundamental to maintaining trust with employees, clients, business partners, and communities, and to protecting DS Home Computing Ltd’s reputation and long-term success.

This Ethics Policy establishes the standards of conduct expected of all individuals acting on behalf of DS Home Computing Ltd and provides guidance for ethical decision-making across all jurisdictions in which the organization operates.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Define standards of ethical behaviour for DS Home Computing Ltd

Promote a respectful, inclusive, and transparent workplace

Support compliance with applicable laws and regulations

Provide mechanisms for reporting and addressing ethical concerns

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Lead by example and promote ethical conduct

Ensure ethical concerns are addressed promptly and fairly

Support a culture of openness and accountability

Human Resources

Support ethics awareness, reporting, and investigations

Maintain records related to ethics concerns and outcomes

Monitor trends and continuous improvement opportunities

Supervisors

Communicate ethical expectations to their teams

Address ethical concerns raised at the operational level

Escalate issues when required

Workers

Act ethically and in accordance with this policy

Report suspected unethical behaviour or violations

Participate in required ethics and compliance training

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and business partners across all operations, projects, and countries where DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business.

Terms and Definitions

Ethical ConductBehaviour that aligns with principles of honesty, integrity, fairness, and respect.

Conflict of InterestA situation in which personal, financial, or other interests may compromise, or appear to compromise, an individual’s ability to act in the best interest of DS Home Computing Ltd.

Business CourtesyAny gift, gratuity, meal, entertainment, or other benefit offered or received in the course of business.

Confidential InformationNon-public information related to DS Home Computing Ltd, clients, employees, or business partners that must be protected from unauthorized disclosure.

RetaliationAny adverse action taken against an individual for reporting concerns or participating in an investigation in good faith.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd expects all individuals acting on its behalf to uphold the highest ethical standards.

Respectful and Inclusive Workplace

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to:

Providing a workplace free from discrimination, harassment, and abusive behaviour

Promoting dignity, respect, and inclusion

Encouraging open dialogue and diverse perspectives

Discrimination, harassment, or offensive behaviour will not be tolerated.

Legal Compliance

DS Home Computing Ltd will comply with all applicable laws, regulations, and contractual obligations in the jurisdictions in which it operates. Individuals are expected to understand and comply with requirements relevant to their roles.

Conflicts of Interest

Individuals must avoid situations where personal interests conflict, or appear to conflict, with the interests of DS Home Computing Ltd. Actual or potential conflicts must be disclosed promptly and managed appropriately.

Gifts, Gratuities, and Business Courtesies

DS Home Computing Ltd prohibits offering or accepting business courtesies that could improperly influence, or appear to influence, business decisions. Modest, infrequent courtesies that align with industry norms may be permitted, subject to management approval where required.

Fair Competition

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to ethical and lawful competition. The organization will not engage in anti-competitive practices, bribery, or improper influence.

Confidentiality and Information Protection

Confidential and proprietary information must be protected and used only for legitimate business purposes. Unauthorized disclosure is prohibited.

Use of Company Resources

Company resources shall be used responsibly and primarily for business purposes. Misuse of company resources is prohibited.

Reporting Ethical Concerns

Versys Group encourages individuals to report ethical concerns without fear of retaliation. Reports may be made through:

Supervisors or management

Human Resources

Designated ethics or whistleblower reporting channels

All reports will be investigated promptly and confidentially where possible.

Accountability

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contractual relationships.

Training

Ethics and conduct awareness will be included in onboarding and periodic training programs, as appropriate.

References

ISO 37001 – Anti-Bribery Management Systems

ISO 37301 – Compliance Management Systems

OECD Guidelines for Multinational Enterprises

United Nations Global Compact Principles

Applicable local, national, and international laws

Review

This policy shall be reviewed every three (3) years, or sooner if required due to regulatory changes, organizational changes, or emerging ethical risks.
