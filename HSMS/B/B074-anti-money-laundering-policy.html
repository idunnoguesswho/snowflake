Anti-Money Laundering Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-GOV-POL-005Anti-Money Laundering Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to conducting business with integrity, transparency, and in compliance with applicable anti-money laundering (AML) and counter-terrorist financing (CTF) laws. Money laundering and related financial crimes undermine economic stability, support illegal activities, and pose significant legal and reputational risks.

This policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to preventing the misuse of its operations, financial systems, and business relationships for money laundering or terrorist financing purposes.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Prevent money laundering, terrorist financing, and related financial crimes

Ensure compliance with applicable AML and CTF laws and regulations

Establish expectations for identifying, reporting, and managing AML risks

Protect DS Home Computing Ltd, its employees, and stakeholders from legal and reputational harm

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Promote a culture of compliance and ethical financial conduct

Ensure appropriate AML controls and oversight are in place

Support corrective action where risks or violations are identified

Finance/ESG Coordinator

Support AML awareness, monitoring, and reporting

Maintain records and documentation related to AML compliance

Coordinate investigations and corrective actions

Supervisors

Monitor activities for potential AML risks or red flags

Ensure transactions and business relationships align with this policy

Escalate concerns promptly

Workers

Comply with this policy and applicable AML requirements

Remain alert to suspicious activities or transactions

Report concerns in good faith without fear of retaliation

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, agents, and business partners in all countries where DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business.

Terms and Definitions

Money LaunderingThe process of concealing or disguising the origins of illegally obtained money or assets to make them appear legitimate.

Terrorist FinancingThe provision or collection of funds with the intention that they be used to carry out terrorist activities.

Criminal PropertyProperty derived directly or indirectly from criminal activity.

Red FlagA warning sign or indicator that a transaction or activity may involve money laundering or terrorist financing.

Beneficial OwnerThe individual(s) who ultimately own or control a customer, supplier, or transaction.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd maintains zero tolerance for money laundering, terrorist financing, or related financial crimes.

Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited:

Concealing or disguising the origin, ownership, or control of criminal property

Conducting transactions involving known or suspected criminal proceeds

Assisting in evading legal or regulatory requirements

Structuring transactions to avoid reporting thresholds

Facilitating or promoting unlawful activity

Business Relationships

DS Home Computing Ltd will only conduct business with customers, suppliers, and partners engaged in legitimate activities

Funds must originate from lawful and transparent sources

Unusual or unexplained transactions must be reviewed and escalated

Red Flags

Examples of AML red flags include, but are not limited to:

Reluctance to provide complete or accurate business information

Requests for cash payments or unusual payment methods

Payments from unrelated third parties

Transactions involving high-risk jurisdictions or tax havens

Overpayments followed by refund requests to third parties

Unusual transaction patterns inconsistent with normal business activity

Reporting and Non-Retaliation

Suspected or actual AML concerns must be reported promptly

Reports may be made through management, Finance, ESG, or whistleblower channels

Retaliation against individuals reporting concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited

Training

AML awareness training shall be provided:

During onboarding for relevant roles

Periodically thereafter, based on risk and responsibility
