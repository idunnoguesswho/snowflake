B.075 · POLICY

Anti Bribery and Corruption Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

GOV-POL-006 Anti Bribery Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to conducting business ethically, transparently, and in full compliance with applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws. Bribery and corruption undermine fair competition, expose organizations to significant legal and reputational risks, and conflict with DS Home Computing Ltd’s values and ESG commitments.

This policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s zero-tolerance approach to bribery and corruption and defines expectations for ethical conduct across all jurisdictions in which the organization operates.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Prohibit bribery, corruption, and other improper payments in any form

Ensure compliance with applicable international, national, and local anti-corruption laws

Define responsibilities for preventing, identifying, and reporting bribery and corruption risks

Promote a culture of integrity, transparency, and accountability

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Demonstrate leadership and commitment to ethical conduct

Ensure appropriate controls, resources, and oversight are in place

Take action where violations are identified

Human Resources/ ESG Coordinator

Support training, awareness, and policy implementation

Coordinate investigations and corrective actions

Maintain records and monitor trends

Supervisors

Promote awareness of anti-bribery expectations

Monitor activities for potential bribery or corruption risks

Escalate concerns promptly

Workers

Comply with this policy and applicable laws

Avoid offering, giving, soliciting, or accepting bribes or improper benefits

Report suspected or actual violations without fear of retaliation

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, agents, intermediaries, and business partners in all countries where DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business.

Terms and Definitions

BriberyThe offering, promising, giving, receiving, or soliciting of anything of value to improperly influence a business or governmental decision.

CorruptionThe abuse of entrusted power for private gain.

Anything of ValueIncludes cash, gifts, entertainment, travel, meals, employment opportunities, charitable donations, political contributions, or any other benefit.

Foreign Public OfficialAny individual who holds a legislative, administrative, or judicial position in a foreign government, or who performs a public function, including employees of state-owned enterprises.

Facilitation PaymentA payment made to expedite or secure routine governmental actions; such payments are prohibited under this policy.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd maintains zero tolerance for bribery and corruption in any form.

Prohibition of Bribery and Improper Payments

Bribes, kickbacks, facilitation payments, or other improper benefits are strictly prohibited

This prohibition applies to interactions with both public officials and private entities

Violations may result in disciplinary action, termination, and legal consequences

Gifts, Entertainment, and Business Courtesies

Modest, infrequent, and reasonable business courtesies may be permitted where lawful and appropriate

Courtesies must never be intended to improperly influence a decision

Excessive or inappropriate gifts or entertainment are prohibited

Third Parties and Intermediaries

DS Home Computing Ltd may be held liable for bribery committed by third parties acting on its behalf

Appropriate due diligence, contractual safeguards, and monitoring must be applied

Red flags or suspicious circumstances must be reported and addressed

Books, Records, and Internal Controls

Accurate and complete records must be maintained

Off-the-books accounts, false invoices, or misleading entries are prohibited

Transactions must be properly authorized and documented

Reporting and Non-Retaliation

Suspected or actual violations must be reported promptly

Reports may be made through supervisors, Human Resources, ESG channels, or whistleblower mechanisms

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited

Accountability

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contracts, and may expose individuals and DS Home Computing Ltd to civil or criminal penalties.

Training

Anti-bribery and corruption training shall be provided:

During onboarding for new employees

Periodically thereafter, based on role and risk

Employees and relevant third parties may be required to formally acknowledge compliance with this policy.
