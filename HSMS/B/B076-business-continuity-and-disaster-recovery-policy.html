Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-GOV-POL-007Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes the importance of maintaining operational resilience in the face of unexpected disruptions. Business interruptions—whether caused by natural disasters, technological failures, cyber incidents, or other unforeseen events—can significantly impact employees, clients, and business continuity.

This Business Continuity and Disaster Recovery (BCDR) Policy establishes a framework to support preparedness, response, and recovery efforts, ensuring that critical business functions can continue and be restored in a timely and coordinated manner.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Support the continuity of critical business operations during disruptions

Minimize the impact of incidents on people, assets, and services

Establish governance expectations for business continuity and disaster recovery planning

Protect DS Home Computing Ltd’s reputation, financial stability, and stakeholder interests

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Provide leadership, oversight, and resources for business continuity and disaster recovery

Ensure continuity risks are addressed as part of enterprise risk management

Coordinate business continuity and disaster recovery planning activities

Support risk assessments, testing, and plan maintenance

Facilitate response and recovery coordination during disruptions

Maintain operational readiness and continuity strategies

Coordinate with third-party vendors and suppliers during disruptions

Human Resources/Safety/Quality

Support workforce communication, availability, and employee welfare

Assist with internal and external communications as required

Information Technology/Administration

Maintain data backups, system redundancies, and recovery capabilities

Support restoration of IT systems and digital assets

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, departments, and operations across all countries where DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business.

It covers all physical and digital assets, including offices, project sites, information systems, data storage, and remote work environments, and addresses disruptions arising from natural, technological, cyber, or other adverse events.

Terms and Definitions

Business ContinuityThe capability of an organization to continue delivery of products or services at acceptable predefined levels following a disruptive incident.

Disaster RecoveryThe process of restoring information technology systems, data, and infrastructure after a disruption.

Business Impact Analysis (BIA)An assessment that identifies critical business functions and evaluates the impact of disruptions over time.

Recovery Time Objective (RTO)The targeted duration of time within which a business process or system must be restored after a disruption.

Recovery Point Objective (RPO)The maximum acceptable amount of data loss measured in time.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to maintaining effective business continuity and disaster recovery capabilities.

Preparedness and Planning

DS Home Computing Ltd shall establish and maintain business continuity and disaster recovery plans appropriate to the nature, size, and complexity of its operations.

Risk Assessment and Business Impact Analysis

Periodic risk assessments and BIAs shall be conducted to identify vulnerabilities and prioritize critical functions

Risks and impacts shall be documented and reviewed to support informed decision-making

Continuity and Recovery Strategies

Strategies may include alternative work arrangements, remote access, secondary locations, and supplier redundancy

Information systems shall be supported by data backups and appropriate system redundancies

Incident Response and Communication

Clear roles and communication protocols shall be established to support timely response

Internal and external communication plans shall be maintained to provide consistent messaging during disruptions

Testing, Training, and Exercises

Business continuity and disaster recovery plans shall be tested periodically through simulations or tabletop exercises

Training and awareness shall be provided to relevant personnel

Monitoring, Review, and Improvement

BCDR plans and procedures shall be reviewed and updated as required

Lessons learned from incidents and exercises shall be incorporated into continuous improvement efforts

Training

Business continuity and disaster recovery awareness training shall be provided:

During onboarding for relevant roles

Periodically thereafter, based on risk and responsibility
