Corporate Travel & Expense Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-GOV-POL-009Corporate Travel & Expense Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing recognizes that business travel is a necessary component of operations across Canada and the United States. As a service-based organization operating in diverse project environments, travel must be conducted in a manner that reflects the Company’s commitment to safety, fiscal responsibility, ethical conduct, and environmental stewardship.

This policy establishes clear expectations for employees and provides direction to external travel service providers to ensure that all travel arrangements are consistent, cost-effective, and aligned with corporate governance and ESG objectives.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Establish standardized requirements for business travel and expense management

Ensure consistent and fair application of travel practices across the organization

Promote responsible use of company funds

Align travel practices with ESG principles, including environmental and ethical considerations

Provide clear direction to the Company’s designated travel agent for booking and coordination

Roles & Responsibilities

Finance/HR

Maintain per diem rates and travel guidance

Review and audit expense submissions

Provide clarification and enforcement of policy requirements

Management/Project Managers

Review and approve all travel requests prior to booking

Ensure travel is necessary, justified, and cost-effective

Monitor compliance with this policy across teams

Approve any exceptions in advance

Designated Travel Agent

Book all travel strictly in accordance with this policy

Prioritize cost-effective and practical travel options

Ensure compliance with:

Economy airfare requirements

Standard accommodation requirements

Ground transportation expectations

Escalate any deviations or unclear requests for approval prior to booking

Maintain consistency across all bookings

Field Employees

Follow per diem requirements where applicable

Manage accommodation and meals within assigned allowances

Ensure compliance with project-specific travel or accommodation requirements

Coordinate with their Lead or Project Manager for travel logistics when required

Office-Based Employees

Coordinate all travel through the designated travel agent

Utilize corporate credit cards for all eligible expenses

Follow reimbursement and expense reporting requirements

Scope

This policy applies to:

All DS Home Computing employees (full-time and part-time)

Contractors travelling on behalf of DS Home Computing

Any individual authorized to incur travel-related expenses for company business

This policy applies to all business travel conducted within Canada and the United States, and internationally where applicable.

Terms and Definitions

Business Travel

Travel undertaken for the purpose of conducting company business, including site visits, client meetings, training, and other work-related activities authorized by DS Home Computing.

Designated Travel Agent

The external travel service provider authorized by DS Home Computing to arrange all business travel, including flights, accommodations, and ground transportation, in accordance with this policy.

Economy Class Travel

The standard class of air travel that provides the lowest logical fare available at the time of booking, excluding premium economy, business class, or first-class options.

Per Diem

A fixed daily allowance provided to field employees to cover meals and accommodation expenses. The per diem replaces the need for individual expense reimbursement for these items.

Reasonable and Cost-Effective

Expenses that are appropriate to the business purpose, reflect good judgment, and avoid unnecessary or excessive cost. This includes selecting standard services and avoiding luxury or premium options unless pre-approved.

Corporate Credit Card

A company-issued payment method that must be used for all eligible business travel expenses unless otherwise authorized.

Non-Reimbursable Expenses

Costs incurred that are not eligible for reimbursement under this policy, including personal expenses, luxury upgrades, fines, and non-business-related charges.

Client Entertainment

Meals or events involving current or prospective clients for the purpose of conducting or supporting business relationships.

Exception Approval

Formal, documented authorization provided by management to deviate from standard policy requirements prior to incurring the expense.

Procedure

Booking Through the Company Travel Agent

All business travel must be arranged exclusively through the Company's designated travel agent. Employees should not book travel independently through personal accounts, online portals, or other third-party services unless expressly authorized by management.

Travel Agent Scope of Services (Clarification)

The designated travel agent is responsible for coordinating and booking flights and rental vehicles. The travel agent is not responsible for booking accommodation under normal operating conditions.

Accommodation arrangements are managed directly by employees or coordinated internally through Leads or Project Managers. On rare occasions, the travel agent may be requested to assist with accommodation bookings where required due to logistical, operational, or timing constraints. In these cases, the request must be clearly communicated and approved prior to booking.

How to Book

Contact the travel agent with your travel dates, destination, and purpose of trip.

Provide your manager's name for any bookings that may require approval.

Allow sufficient lead time: a minimum of 5 business days for domestic travel and 10 business days for international travel is strongly recommended.

Confirm all itinerary details with the travel agent before finalizing.

Last-Minute Travel

In exceptional circumstances where travel must be arranged at short notice, employees should notify their manager and contact the travel agent directly. Any additional costs incurred due to late booking should be explained in the expense report.

Travel Approval

All travel must receive manager approval before being booked. Employees should submit a travel request to their direct manager outlining the purpose of the trip, destination, estimated dates, and expected business benefit. The travel agent will not process bookings without confirmation that approval has been obtained.

Air Travel

Class of Travel

All employees, regardless of seniority, are required to book economy class for both domestic and international flights. Business or premium class is not permitted without prior written approval from a senior leader or the CEO.

Flight Selection Guidelines

While there are no hard spending caps, employees are expected to use good judgment and select the most cost-effective option that meets their travel needs. As a general guideline:

Book the lowest available economy fare that allows for reasonable flexibility (e.g., changes without excessive fees).

Avoid unnecessarily long layovers or indirect routes when a direct option is comparably priced.

Book as early as possible to take advantage of lower fares.

Avoid peak travel times where significantly cheaper alternatives are available.

Loyalty Programs

Employees may retain personal frequent flyer miles earned from business travel. However, loyalty program preferences must not influence flight selection if a more cost-effective option is available.

Accommodation

Employees should select hotels that are comfortable, conveniently located for their business purpose, and reasonably priced.

General Guidelines

Book standard rooms; suites or upgrades are not reimbursable unless provided complimentarily.

Where possible, choose hotels with included breakfast to reduce meal expenses.

The travel agent can provide guidance on suitable options for each destination when requested.

Extended Stays

For trips exceeding 5 nights, employees should explore serviced apartment options which may offer better value.

Ground Transportation

Taxis & Rideshare

Taxis and rideshare services (e.g., Uber, Lyft) are appropriate for travel to/from airports and between meetings. Employees should select standard options and avoid premium or luxury tiers.

Rental Cars

Car rentals should only be booked when necessary (e.g., when public transport is impractical or unavailable). The travel agent will arrange this.

Employees should:

Select compact or mid-size vehicles as the default option.

Decline unnecessary add-ons such as GPS (use a mobile phone instead) or premium insurance beyond what the Company card provides.

Return the vehicle with a full tank of fuel.

DS Home Computing-Supplied Rental Vehicles

When DS Home Computing is directly supplying a rental vehicle for an employee's use, the vehicle must be economy class. Upgrades to a larger, premium, or specialty vehicle class are not permitted without prior written approval from the employee's manager. Requests for an exception should be submitted before the rental is arranged, with a clear business justification.

Personal Vehicles

Where employees use their personal vehicle for business travel, mileage will be reimbursed at the applicable government-prescribed rate for the jurisdiction in which the travel occurs. Employees should confirm the current applicable rate with Finance before travel.

Meals & Client Entertainment

Office & Travelling Employees: Meal Guidelines

For employees travelling on business who are not on a field per diem arrangement, meals are covered by the Company. Where a corporate credit card has been issued, it must be used. Where a corporate credit card has not been issued, employees may use personal funds and submit for reimbursement.

Employees are expected to exercise reasonable judgment and select appropriate dining options for the city and context. As general guidance:

Breakfast: modest and functional; hotel breakfast or equivalent is appropriate.

Lunch: informal dining suitable for a business setting.

Dinner: reasonable dining, including a beverage if desired.

Alcohol consumed as part of a business dinner is reimbursable in moderation. Excessive bar tabs or room service alcohol are not covered.

Field Employees: Per Diem

Employees working in the field are provided a daily per diem allowance. This per diem is intended to cover all meal costs for the duration of the field assignment. Field employees are responsible for managing their own meal expenditures within this allowance; no separate meal reimbursements or corporate card charges for meals are permitted while on per diem.

The per diem also covers accommodation costs for field employees. Field employees are responsible for sourcing and booking their own hotels or other accommodation within the per diem amount. Accommodation that exceeds the per diem is the employee's own responsibility unless a specific exception has been pre-approved by a manager.

Per diem rates are set by Finance and may vary by region or assignment type. Employees should confirm their applicable rate with Finance or HR before the assignment begins.

Per diem is paid for each full day in the field. Partial days (travel days) may be subject to a reduced rate; confirm with Finance.

Receipts are not required for per diem-covered expenses, as the allowance is provided in lieu of expense reimbursement for meals and accommodation.

Client Entertainment

Meals or entertainment involving clients or prospective clients may involve higher spend and must be approved by a manager before the event where possible. Employees should:

Clearly note the names and organizations of all attendees in the expense report.

Describe the business purpose of the entertainment.

Ensure the venue and spend are appropriate for the relationship and context.

Payment & Company Credit Card

All eligible travel expenses should be charged to the Company-issued corporate credit card where one has been issued. Use of the corporate credit card is mandatory for employees who have been provided one.

Where a corporate credit card has not been issued, employees may use personal payment methods and submit expenses for reimbursement in accordance with this policy.

Expense Reporting

Employees must submit an expense report within 15 business days of returning from travel.

All receipts must be retained and attached to the expense report.

Expenses must include a clear description of the business purpose.

The Finance team will review all submissions and follow up on any queries.

Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not covered by the Company:

Personal entertainment (movies, spa, gym, minibar items unrelated to business).

Fines or penalties (e.g., traffic violations, parking tickets).

Upgrades beyond standard class without prior approval.

Travel for personal companions.

Lost or stolen personal property.
