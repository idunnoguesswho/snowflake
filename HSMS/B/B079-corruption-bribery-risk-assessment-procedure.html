Corruption & Bribery Risk Assessment Procedure

ESG-GOV-PROC-001Corruption & Bribery Risk Assessment Procedure

Introduction

DS Home Computing is committed to conducting its business with integrity, transparency, and accountability across all operations. Ethical conduct and compliance with applicable anti-corruption and anti-bribery laws are fundamental to maintaining the trust of our employees, clients, business partners, and stakeholders.

As part of DS Home Computing’ Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) framework, the organization recognizes that corruption and bribery risks may arise through business activities, third-party relationships, geographic exposure, and interactions with public or regulatory officials. Proactively identifying and managing these risks is essential to protecting DS Home Computing’ reputation, ensuring legal compliance, and supporting responsible business practices.

This document establishes a structured approach for assessing corruption and bribery risks and supports DS Home Computing’ broader commitment to ethical governance, continuous improvement, and effective risk management in alignment with the Ethics Policy (ESG-POL-002) and the Anti-Bribery & Corruption Policy (ESG-POL-007).

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to define a formal and consistent process for identifying, assessing, and mitigating corruption and bribery risks across DS Home Computing Ltd. This process ensures that potential risks are evaluated based on likelihood and impact, appropriate controls are applied, and mitigation actions are identified and monitored.

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Provide oversight of the corruption and bribery risk assessment process

Review assessment outcomes and approve mitigation actions

Support continuous improvement of governance controls

HR / Safety / ESG Coordinator

Lead and coordinate corruption and bribery risk assessments

Maintain risk assessment records, risk registers, and action plans

Monitor completion of mitigation actions and report status to management

Supervisors

Participate in identifying risks relevant to their projects or functional areas

Implement assigned controls and mitigation actions

Workers

Comply with applicable ethics and anti-bribery policies

Report suspected or actual bribery, corruption, or ethical concerns through established reporting channels

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd operations, projects, and business activities in both Canada and the United States. It applies to all employees, contractors, subcontractors, consultants, agents, and other third parties acting on behalf of DS Home Computing.

Terms and Definitions

Bribery: The offering, giving, receiving, or soliciting of anything of value to improperly influence a business decision or gain an unfair advantage.

Corruption: The abuse of entrusted power for private gain, including bribery, kickbacks, facilitation payments, and improper influence.

Conflict of Interest: A situation in which personal, financial, or other interests may interfere with an individual’s ability to act objectively in the best interest of DS Home Computing.

Facilitation Payment: A payment made to expedite or secure the performance of a routine government action. Facilitation payments are prohibited under DS Home Computing policy.

Third Party: Any individual or entity acting on behalf of DS Home Computing, including agents, consultants, subcontractors, suppliers, or intermediaries.

Risk Assessment: A structured process used to identify potential risks, evaluate their likelihood and impact, and determine appropriate mitigation measures.

Procedures

Risk Assessment Frequency

Corruption and bribery risk assessments shall be conducted:

At least annually, and

When significant changes occur, including:

Entry into new jurisdictions or geographic regions

Engagement of new third-party agents or subcontractors

Significant regulatory or legislative changes

Organizational or management changes

Material changes to business activities or client relationships

Risk Identification

Risks are identified across business activities and relationships, including but not limited to:

Gifts, hospitality, and business courtesies

Conflicts of interest

Third-party agents, suppliers, and subcontractors

Government and regulatory interactions

International operations

Financial record-keeping and payment controls

Facilitation payments

Hiring, recruitment, and nepotism risks

Risk Evaluation

Identified risks are evaluated based on:

Likelihood of occurrence (Low / Medium / High)

Impact if the risk were to occur (Low / Medium / High)

Existing controls are reviewed to determine the residual risk level.

Risk Register

The results of the assessment are documented in a corruption and bribery risk register, which includes:

Risk area and description

Likelihood and impact ratings

Existing controls

Residual risk rating

Required mitigation actions

Assigned owner and review date

Mitigation and Action Planning

Where risks are identified as medium or high, appropriate mitigation actions are defined, assigned, and tracked to completion. Actions may include procedural improvements, additional controls, contract amendments, or training enhancements.

Monitoring and Review

Progress on mitigation actions is monitored by the HR / Safety / ESG Coordinator and reviewed by management as part of regular ESG, compliance, or management review activities.

Training

Training related to ethics, anti-bribery, and corruption awareness is provided:

During new employee orientation

Periodically to existing employees and contractors, as required

When significant changes to policies or risk exposure occur

Training supports awareness of corruption and bribery risks, reporting expectations, and compliance obligations.
