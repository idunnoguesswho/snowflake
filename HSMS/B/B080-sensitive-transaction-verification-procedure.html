Sensitive Transaction Verification Procedure

ESG-GOV-PROC-002Sensitive Transaction Verification Procedure

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to conducting business ethically and transparently and recognizes that certain business transactions may present an elevated risk of unethical conduct, bribery, or corruption if not properly controlled.

Sensitive transactions—including gifts, hospitality, travel arrangements, facilitation payments, and other benefits—are common in business environments but may, if misused, create actual or perceived conflicts of interest or improper influence. As part of DS Home Computing’ Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) framework, a verification and approval process is established to ensure that sensitive transactions are reviewed, approved, and documented appropriately.

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to establish a formal verification and approval process for sensitive transactions to prevent bribery, corruption, conflicts of interest, and unethical conduct. This process ensures transparency, accountability, and compliance with applicable laws and DS Home Computing policies.

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Provide oversight of the sensitive transaction verification process

Review and approve high-risk or exceptional transactions

HR / Safety / ESG Coordinator

Maintain oversight of the verification process

Retain records of approved sensitive transactions

Provide guidance on interpretation of this procedure

Supervisors

Review and approve applicable sensitive transactions prior to commitment or payment

Ensure transactions comply with DS Home Computing policies

Workers

Disclose and seek approval for sensitive transactions as required

Comply with approval requirements and reporting expectations

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and third parties acting on behalf of DS Home Computing and covers all business activities in Canada and the United States.

Terms and Definitions

Sensitive Transaction: A business transaction that presents an increased risk of ethical, bribery, or corruption concerns. Sensitive transactions may include, but are not limited to:

Gifts and business courtesies

Meals, entertainment, and hospitality

Travel or accommodation arrangements

Facilitation payments

Charitable donations or sponsorships

Payments involving government or regulatory officials

Facilitation Payment: A payment made to expedite or secure the performance of a routine administrative action. Facilitation payments are prohibited under DS Home Computing policy.

Verification: The process of reviewing a sensitive transaction to confirm its legitimacy, compliance with policy, and appropriate authorization.

Procedures

Identification of Sensitive Transactions

Employees and contractors must identify and disclose any transaction that could reasonably be considered sensitive prior to commitment or payment.

Verification and Approval

All sensitive transactions must be reviewed to confirm:

The business purpose is legitimate

The transaction complies with DS Home Computing policies

The transaction is reasonable, proportionate, and not excessive

No conflict of interest exists

Approval requirements:

Low-risk transactions: Manager approval

Medium-risk transactions: Senior management approval

High-risk transactions (government interaction, international, or exceptional value): Senior management and ESG review

Prohibited Transactions

Facilitation payments and any transaction intended to improperly influence a business decision are strictly prohibited.

Documentation

Approved sensitive transactions shall be documented using a log or approval record, including:

Description of the transaction

Value and date

Business purpose

Approval authority

Training

Awareness of sensitive transaction requirements is provided:

Through periodic ethics and anti-corruption training

When policy or risk exposure changes occur
