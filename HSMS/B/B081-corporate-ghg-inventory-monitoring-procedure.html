Corporate GHG Inventory & Monitoring Procedure

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-GOV-PROC-003Corporate GHG Inventory & Monitoring Procedure

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to measuring, monitoring, and managing its greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions in a consistent, transparent, and credible manner. Establishing a corporate-level GHG inventory enables DS Home Computing Ltd to track emissions performance, assess reduction opportunities, and support climate-related targets and disclosures.

This procedure defines the process used to collect, calculate, store, and review GHG emissions data in accordance with recognized GHG accounting standards.

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to:

Establish a corporate-level GHG inventory for DS Home Computing Ltd

Ensure emissions data is collected and calculated consistently year over year

Align emissions accounting with the GHG Protocol and ISO 14064-1

Support ESG reporting, client disclosures, and continuous improvement

Roles & Responsibilities

Senior Management

Provide oversight of corporate GHG monitoring

Review annual emissions performance

ESG / Sustainability Function

Maintain the corporate GHG inventory

Ensure alignment with recognized accounting standards

Review data completeness and accuracy

Finance / Operations / Fleet Support

Provide activity data (fuel, energy, travel, procurement)

Support verification of source data

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd operations across jurisdictions and covers emissions within the following boundaries:

Scope 1: Direct emissions (company vehicles, generators, equipment)

Scope 2: Purchased electricity and energy at leased offices

Scope 3: Indirect emissions including business travel, commuting, procurement, and waste (where data is available)

The inventory reflects emissions sources within DS Home Computing Ltd’s operational control or reasonable influence.

Terms and Definitions

GHG InventoryA quantified list of greenhouse gas emissions sources and totals for a defined reporting period.

Operational BoundaryThe emissions sources included based on operational control.

Activity DataQuantitative data used to calculate emissions (e.g., fuel litres, kWh, kilometres travelled).

Emission FactorA coefficient used to convert activity data into GHG emissions (tCO₂e).

Procedure

Reporting Period

The corporate GHG inventory is compiled annually

The baseline year is 2025, unless revised through governance approval

Data Collection

Activity data may include:

Fuel consumption records

Utility invoices for electricity and natural gas

Business travel records

Waste disposal data

Procurement and logistics data (where available)

Data sources are documented within the inventory register.

Emissions Calculation

Emissions are calculated using recognized emission factors from:

Environment and Climate Change Canada

U.S. EPA

GHG Protocol databases

Calculations are expressed in tonnes of CO₂ equivalent (tCO₂e)

Inventory Management

The corporate GHG inventory is maintained in a controlled register

Data is reviewed for completeness and reasonableness

Supporting records are retained in accordance with record retention requirements

Review and Improvement

Emissions trends are reviewed annually

Methodology updates or data gaps are documented

Improvements are incorporated into future reporting cycles

Training

Personnel responsible for emissions data collection and reporting shall receive role-specific instruction on:

Data inputs

Calculation methodology

Inventory maintenance requirements

Review

This procedure shall be reviewed every three (3) years, or sooner if:

GHG accounting standards change

Reporting boundaries are updated

Significant operational changes occur
