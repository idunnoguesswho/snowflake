Materiality Assessment & ESG Topic Prioritization

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-GOV-PROC-004 Materiality Assessment & ESG Topic Prioritization

Introduction

DS Home Computing recognizes that not all Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) topics carry equal relevance or impact on its operations, stakeholders, or risk profile. A materiality assessment is used to identify and prioritize ESG topics that are most relevant to DS Home Computing’ business activities and stakeholder expectations.

This assessment supports focused ESG management, reporting, and continuous improvement.

Purpose

This procedure applies to:

All DS Home Computing business operations

Corporate, office, and field activities

Employees, contractors, and key suppliers

Operations in both Canada and the United States

Roles and Responsibilities

Senior Management

Endorse the material ESG priorities

Ensure alignment with business strategy and risk management

ESG / HSE / Quality Function

Conduct the materiality assessment

Maintain documentation and scoring records

Review and update the assessment annually

Project & Operational Leadership

Provide operational insight into risks and impacts

Support implementation of controls related to material topics

Methodology Overview

DS Home Computing uses a qualitative, internal materiality assessment methodology appropriate to the size, risk profile, and maturity of the organization.

The assessment is informed by:

Regulatory requirements (Canada and US)

Industry risk profile (commissioning, electrical, instrumentation)

Client expectations and prequalification requirements

Internal risk assessments and performance data

ESG frameworks and guidance (e.g., GRI, EcoVadis)

No external stakeholder surveys are conducted at this stage.

Identification of ESG Topics

An initial list of ESG topics is developed based on:

Applicable legislation and standards

Client and auditor expectations

Internal risk registers and incident trends

Existing policies and procedures

Topics are grouped under:

Environmental

Social

Governance

Materiality Scoring Criteria

Each ESG topic is evaluated against two criteria:

a) Business ImpactAssesses the potential impact on:

Health and safety

Legal and regulatory compliance

Operational continuity

Financial performance

Reputation and client confidence

b) Stakeholder ImportanceAssesses relevance to:

Clients and project owners

Employees

Regulators and certification bodies

Supply chain partners

Each criterion is scored on a 1–5 scale.

Determination of Material Topics

The combined score is used to determine materiality:

High Materiality: Core ESG priorities requiring KPIs, controls, and reporting

Medium Materiality: Managed and monitored through existing programs

Lower Materiality: Monitored and reviewed periodically

Material topics are those that score highest and represent the most significant risks or impacts to DS Home Computing and its stakeholders.

Material ESG Topics (Current Assessment)

High Materiality Topics

Occupational Health and Safety

Regulatory Compliance

Training and Competency

Business Ethics and Anti-Bribery

Human Rights and Labour Practices

GHG Emissions and Energy Use

Data Protection and Cybersecurity

Medium Materiality Topics

Waste Management

Supplier ESG Performance

Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

Environmental Protection and Spill Prevention

Community Engagement

Lower materiality topics are monitored through existing management systems.

Integration With ESG Program

Material ESG topics are:

Reflected in DS Home Computing ESG policies and procedures

Incorporated into risk assessments and management systems

Supported by KPIs where appropriate

Included in ESG and sustainability reporting

Review and Continuous Improvement

This materiality assessment is reviewed:

Annually, or

When significant business or regulatory changes occur

Updates are documented and approved through standard document control processes.
