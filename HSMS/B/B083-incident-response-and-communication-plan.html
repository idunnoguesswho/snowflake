Incident Response and Communication Plan

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-IT-PLAN-001 Incident Response and Communication Plan

Purpose

This plan establishes a structured approach for responding to incidents and communicating effectively with employees, clients, regulators, and other stakeholders.

Scope

Applies to incidents including:

Health and safety emergencies

Environmental incidents

Cybersecurity events

Business disruptions

Reputational or legal incidents

Incident Classification

Incidents may be classified as:

Minor: Limited impact, managed locally

Moderate: Requires management involvement

Major: Significant impact, external notification required

Roles & Responsibilities

Incident Commander

Overall control of incident response

Decision-making authority

Response Team

Implement response actions

Provide situation updates

Communications Lead

Manage internal and external communications

Ensure consistent and accurate messaging

Incident Response Process

Detection & Reporting

Incident identified and reported immediately

Assessment

Determine severity and required response

Containment

Control the incident and prevent escalation

Response

Implement corrective actions

Recovery

Restore operations

Review

Conduct post-incident review and improvement

Communication Principles

Timely and factual

Clear and consistent messaging

Respect confidentiality and legal requirements

Use designated communication channels only

Stakeholder Communication

May include:

Employees and contractors

Clients and partners

Regulatory authorities

Emergency services

Community stakeholders (if applicable)

Post-Incident Review

Root cause analysis conducted

Lessons learned documented

Action items tracked to completion
