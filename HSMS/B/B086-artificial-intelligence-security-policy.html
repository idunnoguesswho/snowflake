ESG-IT-POL-003 Artificial Intelligence Security Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes that Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies can enhance operational efficiency, data analysis, and decision-making when used responsibly. At the same time, AI introduces unique risks related to data protection, privacy, cybersecurity, and ethical use.

This policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to the secure, ethical, and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence systems, ensuring that AI technologies are deployed in a manner that protects information assets, respects privacy, and aligns with applicable laws and ESG principles.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Establish governance expectations for the secure use of Artificial Intelligence

Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data used in AI systems

Ensure compliance with applicable data protection and privacy regulations

Reduce cybersecurity, legal, and ethical risks associated with AI technologies

Roles & Responsibilities

Senior Management

Provide oversight and resources for AI security and governance

Ensure AI use aligns with organizational values, risk tolerance, and regulatory requirements

Information Technology / Cybersecurity Function

Support the secure deployment, monitoring, and protection of AI systems

Implement appropriate technical safeguards and access controls

Safety, Quality & Sustainability / ESG Function

Provide governance oversight and policy alignment

Support audits, reviews, and continuous improvement related to AI security

Human Resources

Support awareness and training related to responsible AI use

Workers and Contractors

Use AI tools responsibly and in accordance with this policy

Report suspected security incidents, misuse, or concerns involving AI systems

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and third-party service providers who develop, deploy, access, or use Artificial Intelligence systems on behalf of DS Home Computing Ltd.

It applies to all AI technologies, including but not limited to:

Machine learning systems

Large language models (LLMs)

Automated decision-support tools

AI systems that process, store, or transmit company, employee, or client data

Terms and Definitions

Artificial Intelligence (AI)Systems or technologies designed to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, including learning, reasoning, and pattern recognition.

Large Language Models (LLMs)AI systems trained on large volumes of text data to understand, generate, or analyze human language.

ConfidentialityThe protection of information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Data ProtectionSafeguards implemented to prevent unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration of data.

AI Security IncidentAny event that compromises, or has the potential to compromise, the security, integrity, or lawful use of an AI system or its data.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to the secure and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence technologies.

Responsible Use of AI

AI technologies shall be used to support legitimate business activities

AI systems shall not be used in a manner that violates laws, contractual obligations, or ethical standards

Data Protection and Privacy

Data used in AI systems shall be handled in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy laws

Personal or client data shall be anonymized or minimized where feasible

Sensitive data shall not be used in AI systems unless appropriate safeguards are in place

Security Controls

Appropriate technical and organizational controls shall be implemented to protect AI systems

Controls may include encryption, access controls, authentication measures, and audit logging

Access to AI systems shall be restricted to authorized personnel

Monitoring and Auditability

AI systems shall be monitored for unusual activity, misuse, or security threats

Records or logs may be maintained to support traceability and incident investigation

Incident Reporting and Response

Suspected or confirmed AI security incidents shall be reported promptly through established incident reporting channels

Incidents shall be managed in alignment with DS Home Computing Ltd’s Cyber Incident Management Program

Corrective actions shall be implemented to prevent recurrence

Compliance and Continuous Improvement

AI security practices shall be reviewed periodically

Improvements shall be implemented based on audits, incidents, regulatory changes, or emerging risks

Training

AI security and responsible use awareness training shall be provided:

To personnel who use or manage AI systems

During onboarding or role changes where applicable

Periodically thereafter, based on risk and operational needs

Review

This policy shall be reviewed every three (3) years, or sooner if required due to:

Regulatory or legislative changes

Significant changes in AI usage or technology

Security incidents or audit findings
