Cyber Access Control and Account Monitoring Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-IT-POL-004Cyber Access Control and Account Monitoring Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd operates in a digital environment where access to systems, applications, and data must be carefully controlled to protect organizational assets, sensitive information, and stakeholder trust. Effective access control and account monitoring are essential components of information security and risk management.

This policy establishes governance requirements for managing user access and monitoring accounts to reduce the risk of unauthorized access, misuse, or cyber incidents across all jurisdictions in which DS Home Computing Ltd operates.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Establish consistent access control requirements across DS Home Computing Ltd

Ensure user access is authorized, appropriate, and regularly reviewed

Reduce cybersecurity risks related to inactive, unauthorized, or compromised accounts

Support compliance with applicable data protection, privacy, and cybersecurity regulations

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Ensure access control requirements are implemented within their areas of responsibility

Approve access requests based on role and business need

Support compliance and corrective actions where required

Human Resources

Coordinate user account creation, modification, and deactivation based on employment status

Notify appropriate functions of onboarding, role changes, or terminations

Support access reviews and training requirements

Information Technology / Cybersecurity

Implement and maintain technical access controls and monitoring mechanisms

Support account provisioning, disabling, and security controls

Monitor account activity and investigate anomalies

Safety, Quality, and ESG Functions

Support governance oversight and policy compliance

Assist with audits, access reviews, and corrective actions

Workers and Contractors

Use company systems responsibly and in accordance with this policy

Protect account credentials and report access issues or suspicious activity promptly

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, vendors, and third-party users who have access to DS Home Computing Ltd information systems, applications, data, or digital assets.

It applies to both logical (digital) access and, where applicable, system-related physical access controls.

Terms and Definitions

Access ControlMeasures used to ensure that only authorized individuals can access systems, data, or resources.

Inactive AccountA user account that has not been accessed within a defined period, as established by DS Home Computing Ltd.

Account LockoutA security control that restricts access after a defined number of unsuccessful login attempts.

Session TimeoutThe automatic termination or locking of a system session after a period of inactivity.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to maintaining effective access control and account monitoring practices to protect information assets.

Account Provisioning and Authorization

User access shall be granted based on job role, responsibilities, and business need

Access approvals shall be documented and authorized appropriately

Account Modification and Deactivation

User access shall be modified or removed promptly when roles change or employment ends

Accounts not associated with authorized users shall be disabled

Inactive Accounts

User accounts that remain inactive beyond a defined period shall be reviewed and disabled where appropriate

Periodic reviews shall be conducted to identify inactive or unnecessary accounts

Access Review and Maintenance

Access lists shall be maintained and reviewed periodically

Reviews shall verify that access remains appropriate, authorized, and aligned with job duties

Security Controls

Systems shall implement reasonable security controls, including session timeouts and account lockout mechanisms

Controls shall be configured based on risk, system sensitivity, and regulatory requirements

Monitoring and Logging

Account activity may be monitored to detect unauthorized or suspicious behavior

Logs and monitoring records shall be retained in accordance with legal and business requirements

Suspected security incidents shall be investigated promptly

Training

Access control and cybersecurity awareness training shall be provided:

During onboarding for users requiring system access

When roles or access requirements change

Periodically, based on organizational risk and system usage
