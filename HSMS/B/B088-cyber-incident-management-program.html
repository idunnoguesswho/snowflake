Cyber Incident Management Program

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-IT-POL-005Cyber Incident Management Program

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd operates in an increasingly digital environment where cybersecurity incidents present a persistent risk to information security, business operations, and stakeholder trust. Cyber incidents may include data breaches, unauthorized access, system compromises, malware attacks, or service disruptions.

This Cyber Incident Management Program establishes a structured, coordinated approach for identifying, responding to, monitoring, and reviewing cybersecurity incidents to minimize impact, support business continuity, and maintain regulatory compliance.

Purpose

The purpose of this program is to:

Provide a consistent framework for managing cybersecurity incidents

Minimize disruption to business operations and services

Protect the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets

Support regulatory, contractual, and stakeholder obligations

Promote continuous improvement in cybersecurity resilience

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Provide oversight and authority for cyber incident response

Ensure appropriate resources and support are available

Review significant incidents and corrective actions

Incident Response Team

Coordinate identification, response, containment, and recovery activities

Assess incident severity and business impact

Maintain documentation and incident records

Operations/ IT Support

Conduct technical analysis, containment, and recovery activities

Support system monitoring, restoration, and validation

Human Resources/Safety/Quality/ESG Coordinator

Support internal and external communications

Coordinate employee notifications and awareness activities

Support post-incident review and improvement actions

Workers and Contractors

Promptly report suspected or confirmed cyber incidents

Comply with cybersecurity policies and response instructions

Participate in required training and awareness activities

Scope

This program applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, third-party vendors, and service providers who interact with DS Home Computing Ltd information systems, networks, or data.

It covers all cybersecurity incidents, including but not limited to:

Data breaches

Malware or ransomware attacks

Unauthorized access attempts

Denial-of-service attacks

System or network compromises

Terms and Definitions

Cybersecurity IncidentAny event that compromises, or has the potential to compromise, the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of information systems or data.

Incident Response Team (IRT)A designated group responsible for coordinating the response to cybersecurity incidents.

Incident ClassificationThe process of categorizing incidents based on severity, impact, and urgency.

ContainmentActions taken to limit the scope and impact of a cybersecurity incident.

Post-Incident ReviewA structured evaluation conducted after an incident to identify lessons learned and improvement opportunities.

Program Requirements

Incident Identification and Reporting

Cyber incidents may be identified through system monitoring tools or user reporting

All suspected or confirmed incidents must be reported promptly

Secure and confidential reporting mechanisms shall be available

Incident Classification and Prioritization

Incidents shall be assessed based on severity, scope, and potential impact

Response actions shall be prioritized based on criticality and regulatory obligations

Incident Response and Containment

The IRT shall be notified upon incident confirmation

Containment measures shall be implemented to prevent further impact

Response actions shall be documented throughout the incident lifecycle

Monitoring and Communication

Affected systems shall be monitored to track incident progression

Stakeholders shall be updated as appropriate based on incident severity

Communications shall be factual, timely, and coordinated

Reporting and Documentation

All actions, decisions, and timelines shall be recorded

Regulatory and contractual reporting obligations shall be fulfilled where required

Post-Incident Review and Improvement

A post-incident review shall be conducted following significant incidents

Lessons learned shall be documented and used to improve controls, training, or processes

Policies and procedures shall be updated where necessary

Training

Cyber incident management training shall include:

Role-specific training for Incident Response Team members

Periodic simulated incident exercises

General cybersecurity awareness training for all personnel

Training frequency shall be based on role, risk, and organizational needs.

Regulatory or legal changes
