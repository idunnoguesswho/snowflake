Media Protection Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-IT-POL-006Media Protection Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd manages sensitive information across a variety of digital and non-digital media, including electronic systems, cloud platforms, physical documents, and removable storage devices. Improper handling, storage, or disposal of media can expose the organization to cybersecurity, privacy, regulatory, and reputational risks.

This policy establishes governance expectations for protecting media throughout its lifecycle, ensuring that information assets are handled securely, responsibly, and in compliance with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Protect sensitive and confidential information stored on digital and non-digital media

Ensure media is accessed only by authorized individuals

Define expectations for media labelling, storage, transport, and disposal

Support compliance with data protection, privacy, and cybersecurity requirement

Roles & Responsibilities

Management / Supervisors

Ensure personnel understand and comply with this policy

Address non-compliance promptly and appropriately

Support secure handling practices within their areas of responsibility

Human Resources / Safety / Quality / ESG

Oversee implementation and compliance with this policy

Support training, awareness, and monitoring activities

Maintain records related to media protection where applicable

Information Technology / Cybersecurity

Support technical controls for digital media protection

Provide guidance on encryption, access controls, and secure storage

Workers and Contractors

Handle media responsibly in accordance with this policy

Protect media from unauthorized access, loss, or damage

Report suspected media loss, misuse, or security concerns promptly

Scope

This policy applies to all digital and non-digital media owned, managed, or accessed by DS Home Computing Ltd, including but not limited to:

Physical documents and printed records

Electronic files and databases

Removable storage devices (e.g., USB drives, external hard drives)

Cloud-based storage and collaboration platforms

It applies to all employees, contractors, and third parties with access to DS Home Computing Ltd information.

Terms and Definitions

MediaPhysical or electronic materials used to store, process, or convey information.

Digital MediaInformation stored electronically, including cloud systems, hard drives, and removable storage devices.

Non-Digital MediaPhysical information carriers such as printed documents, paper files, photographs, or tapes.

Authorized IndividualsPersons granted permission to access specific media based on role and business need.

ClassificationThe categorization of information based on sensitivity (e.g., Confidential, Internal, Public).

SanitizationThe process of permanently removing or destroying data to prevent recovery.

Procedure

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to protecting media throughout its lifecycle, from creation to disposal.

Access Control

Access to media shall be restricted to authorized individuals only

Sensitive or classified media shall be subject to additional controls where appropriate

Media Classification and Labelling

Media shall be labelled or otherwise identified based on its classification

Classification standards shall be applied consistently to support secure handling

Secure Storage

Non-digital media shall be stored in secure, controlled locations

Digital media shall be protected using appropriate security measures, such as encryption and access controls

Media Transport

Media transported outside controlled environments shall be secured against loss or unauthorized access

Sensitive digital media shall be encrypted where feasible

Movement of sensitive media may be tracked or logged where appropriate

Media Disposal and Sanitization

Digital media shall be sanitized prior to disposal, reuse, or transfer

Non-digital media shall be securely destroyed to prevent information recovery

Disposal activities shall be performed in accordance with applicable standards and regulations

Incident Reporting

Suspected loss, theft, or compromise of media shall be reported promptly

Media-related incidents shall be managed in alignment with DS Home Computing Ltd’s security incident reporting and cyber incident management processes

Training

Media protection awareness training shall be provided:

During onboarding for new employees and contractors

To personnel who handle sensitive or classified media

Periodically, based on risk, role, and operational needs
