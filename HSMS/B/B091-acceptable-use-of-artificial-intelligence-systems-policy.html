ESG-IT-POL-008Acceptable Use of Artificial Intelligence Systems Policy

Introduction

AI systems are increasingly used for productivity, drafting, analytics, and reporting. This Policy establishes acceptable use standards to ensure AI is used securely, ethically, and in alignment with corporate governance.

Purpose

To define:

Permitted AI uses

Prohibited activities

Data handling restrictions

Employee responsibilities

Roles & Responsibilities

Executive Leadership

Approves enterprise AI platforms

ESG Coordinator

Maintains approved AI list

Reviews acceptable use compliance

Information Technology/Administration

Controls access to enterprise AI tools

Monitors unauthorized installations

Managers

Ensure staff comply with use restictions

Employees and Contractors

Only use approved AI tools

Protect confidential data

Verify outputs

Scope

Applies to all personnel using AI tools in any capacity related to company operations.

Terms and Definitions

Public AI Tool:AI platform accessible via internet without enterprise data protection agreement.

Enterprise AI Tool:AI system approved through formal risk assessment and contract review.

Confidential Data:Client information, HR records, safety investigations, contracts, proprietary procedures.

Policy

Permitted Use

Drafting internal documents (non-confidential)

Productivity assistance

Brainstorming

General research (verified)

Prohibited Use

Uploading confidential client data

Uploading HR records

Generating falsified compliance data

Using AI for autonomous safety decisions

Using AI to issue regulatory certifications

Data Classification Compliance

All AI inputs must comply with Data Protection Policy.

Training

Responsible Ai training
