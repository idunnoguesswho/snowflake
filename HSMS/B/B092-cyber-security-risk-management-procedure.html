ESG-IT-PROC-001 Cyber Security Risk Management Procedure

Introduction

Cybersecurity risks present a significant threat to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of DS Home Computing Ltd information assets. As DS Home Computing Ltd operates primarily within a cloud-based environment and permits the use of personal devices, cybersecurity risk management focuses on access controls, cloud security, threat monitoring, and user compliance rather than direct endpoint ownership.

This procedure establishes a structured and proactive approach to identifying, assessing, controlling, and reviewing cybersecurity risks to support business continuity, regulatory compliance, and the protection of organizational data.

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to:

Identify and assess cybersecurity risks affecting DS Home Computing Ltd

Implement appropriate controls to mitigate identified risks

Monitor cybersecurity threats and control effectiveness

Support compliance with applicable cybersecurity, privacy, and data protection requirements

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Ensure cybersecurity risk management practices are implemented and maintained

Review cybersecurity risk assessments and approve mitigation strategies

Allocate resources for cybersecurity controls and training

Human Resources / Safety / Quality / ESG

Coordinate access changes related to onboarding, role changes, and terminations

Support compliance monitoring and awareness activities

Escalate cybersecurity concerns as required

Information Technology / Cybersecurity Function

Monitor cloud-based systems and threat alerts

Conduct cybersecurity risk assessments and reviews

Implement and maintain security configurations and controls

Support incident investigation and remediation

Workers and Contractors

Comply with cybersecurity policies and procedures

Report suspected or actual cybersecurity threats promptly

Complete required cybersecurity training

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and third parties with access to DS Home Computing Ltd digital infrastructure, including cloud-based systems, applications, and data.

Terms and Definitions

Access ControlControls that restrict access to systems, data, or resources based on authorization.

Cybersecurity RiskThe potential for unauthorized access, data loss, disruption, or compromise of digital assets.

Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)A security mechanism requiring two or more forms of authentication.

PhishingA cyberattack involving deceptive communications intended to obtain sensitive information.

Data Loss Prevention (DLP)Controls designed to prevent unauthorized disclosure or loss of sensitive data.

Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)Access control based on job roles and responsibilities.

Threat IntelligenceInformation used to identify, assess, and respond to cybersecurity threats.

Procedure

Risk Identification

Cybersecurity risks shall be identified through:

Continuous system and cloud monitoring

Employee reporting of suspicious activity

Periodic internal audits and reviews

Common risk categories include:

Unauthorized access or credential compromise

Phishing and social engineering attacks

Data breaches or data leakage

Malware or ransomware affecting cloud services

Regulatory and compliance risks

Risk Assessment

Identified risks shall be evaluated based on:

Likelihood: Low, Medium, or High

Impact: Low, Medium, or High

Risk assessments shall consider potential operational disruption, data loss, regulatory exposure, and reputational impact.

Where available, cloud security analytics and secure score tools may be used to support assessments.

Risk Control Measures

Access Control and Authentication

Multi-factor authentication shall be enforced for cloud-based systems

Role-based access shall be applied to limit access to sensitive data

User access shall be reviewed periodically

Personal Device Considerations

Where personal devices are used:

Users are expected to maintain up-to-date antivirus protection

Secure network connections shall be used when accessing company systems

Use of unsecured public networks without appropriate protection is discouraged

Threat Monitoring and Incident Response

Cloud-based threat monitoring tools shall be used to identify anomalies

High-risk alerts shall be reviewed within a reasonable timeframe

Suspected incidents shall be managed in accordance with the Cyber Incident Management Program

Email and Phishing Protection

Anti-phishing and malicious email filtering controls shall be implemented

Periodic phishing awareness activities may be conducted

Suspicious communications shall be reported promptly

Data Protection and Compliance

Data loss prevention controls shall be implemented where feasible

Sensitive data shall be protected through encryption and access restrictions

Compliance with applicable data protection legislation shall be monitored

Risk Review and Continuous Improvement

Cybersecurity risks and incidents shall be reviewed periodically

Lessons learned shall inform control improvements and training updates

Security posture metrics may be reviewed to support continuous improvement

Training

Cybersecurity training shall include:

Onboarding cybersecurity awareness training

Periodic refresher training on emerging threats

Targeted training for roles with elevated access or responsibilities
