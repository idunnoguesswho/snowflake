Security Incident Reporting Procedure

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use

ESG-IT-PROC-002Security Incident Reporting Procedure

Introduction

Cybersecurity threats continue to evolve, increasing the risk of unauthorized access, data breaches, and system disruptions. Timely identification and reporting of security incidents are essential to minimizing impacts, protecting company information, and maintaining business continuity.

This Security Incident Reporting Procedure establishes a clear and consistent process for identifying, reporting, responding to, and reviewing security incidents affecting DS Home Computing Ltd systems, data, or accounts.

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to:

Provide a clear and accessible method for reporting security incidents

Ensure timely response, containment, and remediation of threats

Reduce the impact of cybersecurity incidents on operations and data

Support compliance with cybersecurity, data protection, and ESG requirements

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Review reported security incidents and determine appropriate response actions

Ensure reporting and response requirements are followed

Support remediation activities and corrective actions

Human Resources / Safety / Quality / ESG

Support employee awareness of reporting requirements

Maintain records of reported incidents and outcomes

Review incident trends to support continuous improvement

Information Technology / Cybersecurity Function

Assess reported incidents and determine severity

Implement containment, mitigation, and recovery actions

Support investigations and technical remediation

Workers and Contractors

Report suspected or actual security incidents immediately

Follow instructions provided during incident response

Cooperate with investigations and corrective actions

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, and third parties who access or use DS Home Computing Ltd information systems, applications, or data.

It covers the reporting and handling of security incidents related to:

Digital systems and cloud environments

User accounts and credentials

Company data and information assets

Terms and Definitions

Security IncidentAny event that compromises, or has the potential to compromise, the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of DS Home Computing Ltd systems, data, or accounts.

PhishingA fraudulent attempt to obtain sensitive information by impersonating a trusted source.

Unauthorized AccessAccess to systems or data by an individual without proper authorization.

MalwareMalicious software designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to systems.

Data BreachThe accidental or intentional exposure of confidential or sensitive data.

Incident ResponseActions taken to identify, contain, mitigate, and recover from a security incident.

RemediationCorrective actions implemented to resolve an incident and prevent recurrence.

Procedure

Reportable Security Incidents

The following incidents must be reported immediately:

Phishing or suspicious emails

Unauthorized or suspicious account access

Data breaches or unintended data disclosure

Malware or ransomware indicators

Lost or stolen devices with access to company systems

Suspicious system behavior or unexplained changes

Violations of cybersecurity policies or access controls

Incident Identification

If suspicious activity is detected, users shall stop interacting with the affected system where possible

No attempts shall be made to investigate beyond basic containment actions

Incident Reporting

Security incidents shall be reported as soon as practicable using one or more of the following approved channels:

Internal incident reporting form or platform

Direct notification to management or designated cybersecurity contacts

Approved internal communication channels

Reporting should include, where available:

Date and time of the incident

Description of the issue

Affected systems, accounts, or data

Screenshots or supporting evidence

Immediate actions taken

Containment Actions

Where applicable, users may be instructed to:

Log out of affected accounts

Change passwords or enable multi-factor authentication

Disconnect affected devices from networks

Secure or report lost or stolen devices

Incident Review and Follow-Up

Reported incidents shall be reviewed to determine severity and root cause

Corrective and preventive actions shall be identified and implemented

Additional training may be required where incidents result from human error

Escalation

High-risk or unresolved incidents shall be escalated to senior management

Incidents with legal, regulatory, or client impact shall be managed in coordination with applicable functions

Training

Security incident reporting and awareness training shall be provided:

During onboarding

Periodically as part of cybersecurity awareness initiatives

Following significant incidents or emerging threats
