IT-PROC-003Data Backup and Verification Procedure

Introduction

Data is a critical asset for DS Home Computing Ltd, and its availability, integrity, and security are essential to business continuity and regulatory compliance. Loss, corruption, or unauthorized access to data can disrupt operations and expose the organization to financial, legal, and reputational risk.

This procedure establishes a structured approach for manually backing up critical business data, verifying backup integrity, and confirming data restoration capability using approved cloud storage solutions.

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to:

Protect critical business data from loss, corruption, or cyber threats

Define consistent requirements for manual data backups

Ensure backups are verifiable, accessible, and restorable

Support business continuity, cybersecurity, and data protection obligations

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Ensure backup and verification requirements are implemented

Review backup performance and address identified issues

Approve archive or restoration requests where required

Backup Coordinator (Designated Role)

Perform scheduled manual backups

Transfer backup files to approved cloud storage

Verify backup completeness and integrity

Maintain backup and verification records

Workers Handling Critical Data

Ensure files are stored in designated backup locations prior to scheduled backups

Report missing, corrupted, or inaccessible files promptly

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees and contractors responsible for managing, storing, or backing up critical business data.

It covers:

Manual backup of critical data

Secure cloud storage of backups

Backup verification and restoration testing

Terms and Definitions

BackupA copy of critical data stored separately to protect against loss, corruption, or cyber threats.

Backup CoordinatorThe designated role responsible for executing and verifying manual backups.

Data IntegrityThe accuracy, completeness, and consistency of data throughout its lifecycle.

Data RestorationThe process of retrieving and recovering backed-up data following data loss or corruption.

EncryptionThe process of converting data into a secure format to prevent unauthorized access.

Manual BackupA non-automated process in which data is manually saved, organized, and transferred to secure storage.

Verification CheckA review performed to confirm backup files are complete, accessible, and usable.

Procedure

Backup Frequency

Weekly Backups:Critical business data shall be backed up weekly at the end of the workweek.

Monthly Full Backup:A full backup of essential documents and records shall be completed monthly.

Data Backup Process

Identify Critical Data

Critical data may include, but is not limited to:

Financial records

Project documentation

Compliance and regulatory records

Employee and client data

Prepare Data for Backup

Ensure files are current and stored in designated backup folders

Remove duplicate or unnecessary files where appropriate

Transfer to Cloud Storage

Backup files shall be uploaded to an approved cloud storage location within the DS Home Computing Ltd environment

At present, Google Drive is used as the primary cloud storage platform for manual backups

File naming conventions shall be applied to support traceability (e.g., Project ID – Client – File Name)

Confirm Backup Completion

Backup completion shall be verified by confirming file upload and accessibility

Backup details shall be recorded, including date, file size, and verification status

Backup Verification

Routine Verification

Following weekly backups, random files shall be opened to confirm successful transfer

Following monthly backups, a full integrity check shall be completed

Restoration Testing

Quarterly restoration testing shall be conducted by selecting sample files and confirming successful retrieval and usability

Any restoration failures shall trigger investigation and corrective action

Security Controls

Access to backup storage shall be restricted to authorized personnel

Sensitive or confidential files shall be encrypted prior to upload where feasible

Backup records shall be reviewed periodically for anomalies or gaps

Training

Initial Training

Personnel responsible for handling or backing up critical data shall receive training on:

Manual backup procedures

Secure cloud storage practices

Verification and restoration testing

Data protection and encryption requirements

Ongoing Training and Awareness

Periodic refresher training shall be provided

Awareness activities may address emerging cyber threats affecting data backups

Restoration Testing Participation

Backup coordinators and authorized personnel shall participate in quarterly restoration testing to maintain readiness
