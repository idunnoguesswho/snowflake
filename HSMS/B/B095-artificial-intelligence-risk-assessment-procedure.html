IT-PROC-004Artificial Intelligence Risk Assessment Procedure

Introduction

AI systems introduce operational, cybersecurity, regulatory, and ethical risks. A structured risk evaluation process is required prior to implementation.

Purpose

To ensure AI tools are:

Risk assessed

Properly approved

Documented

Monitored

Roles & Responsibilities

Executive Leadership

Approves High-Risk AI systems

Reviews risk summaries

ESG Coordinator

Initiates and documents AI Risk Assessment

Assigns risk rating

Maintains records

Information Technology/Administration

Conducts cybersecurity review

Evaluates vendor security controls

Department Managers

Defines intended use

Identifies operational dependencies

Implements mitigation measures

Employees and Contractors

Do not deploy unapproved AI tools

Follow approved use parameters

Human Resources

Reviews AI tools impacting employment or workforce evaluation

Scope

This Policy applies to:

New AI tools

AI-enabled features

AI integrations into reporting, safety, HR, or analytics

Expansion of AI use cases

Terms and Definitions

AI Risk Assessment:Formal evaluation of AI-related risks across operational, cybersecurity, regulatory, and ethical domains.

Low Risk AI:Productivity assistance with no material decision-making impact.

Medium Risk AI:Decision-support systems affecting internal processes.

High Risk AI:Systems impacting safety, employment, financial reporting, ESG disclosures, or regulatory compliance.

Mitigation Measures:Controls implemented to reduce AI-related risk.

Procedure

Trigger Events

Risk Assessment required when:

Implementing new AI tool

Enabling AI in existing software

Expanding use into regulated functions

Risk Evaluation Categories

Data Sensitivity

Operational Reliance

ESG & Ethical Risk

Cybersecurity Exposure

Regulatory Impact

Risk Rating

Low

Medium

High

High Risk → Executive approval required.

Documentation

The following must be retained:

AI Risk Assessment Form

Vendor documentation

Approval record

Review schedule
