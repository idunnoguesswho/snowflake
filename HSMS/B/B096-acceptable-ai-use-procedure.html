ESG-IT-PROC-005Acceptable AI Use Procedure

Introduction

Artificial Intelligence technologies are increasingly integrated into enterprise software platforms, productivity applications, analytics tools, and reporting systems. While these technologies provide operational efficiencies and enhanced analytical capabilities, their use introduces risks related to data protection, regulatory compliance, cybersecurity exposure, bias, and reputational integrity.

This Procedure establishes the operational controls governing the day-to-day use of Artificial Intelligence systems within DS Home Computing Ltd. It ensures that AI systems are used in a controlled, secure, ethical, and auditable manner consistent with corporate governance obligations.

Purpose

The purpose of this Procedure is to define the operational requirements that must be followed when using Artificial Intelligence systems in support of company activities. This Procedure supports the Artificial Intelligence Governance & Responsible Use Policy (ESG-IT-POL-007) and ensures that all AI use remains compliant with corporate data protection standards, ethical commitments, and regulatory obligations.

Roles & Responsibilities

Executive Leadership

Approves final ESG and regulatory disclosures.

Maintains accountability for the integrity of corporate reporting.

ESG Coordinator

Oversees AI-assisted reporting controls.

Ensures independent verification of AI-generated content.

Confirms emission factors and regulatory references.

Retains verification documentation.

Disclose use of AI assistance internally.

Independently verify all quantitative and regulatory information.

Document verification activities.

Information Technology/Administration

Ensures AI systems used in reporting have been risk assessed.

Confirms secure handling of reporting data.

Department Managers

Ensure AI outputs within their function are reviewed and validated.

Confirm that no unverified AI content is externally distributed.

Employees and Contractors

Do not deploy unapproved AI tools

Follow approved use parameters

Human Resources

Reviews AI tools impacting employment or workforce evaluation

Scope

This Procedure applies to all employees, contractors, consultants, and temporary personnel who utilize AI-enabled tools in any capacity related to company operations. This includes the use of generative AI systems, predictive analytics platforms, automated drafting tools, embedded AI features within SaaS applications, and any other technology that utilizes algorithmic decision-making or content generation.

This Procedure applies to both Canadian and United States operations.

Terms and Definitions

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to software systems capable of generating predictions, classifications, recommendations, or content using machine learning models or algorithmic automation.

AI-Assisted Output refers to any document, report, communication, dataset, analysis, or recommendation generated in whole or in part through the use of AI systems.

Confidential Information includes client data, proprietary engineering information, safety investigation reports, employment records, contractual documentation, financial information, and any information classified under the Data Protection Policy (ESG-IT-POL-002).

Human-in-the-Loop refers to a governance control requiring competent human review, validation, and approval before AI-generated outputs are relied upon or externally distributed.

Procedure

Identification of AI-Assisted Reporting

Where Artificial Intelligence tools are used in drafting or analyzing reporting content, the individual preparing the document shall internally identify that AI assistance has been utilized. This identification is required to ensure enhanced verification controls are applied.

AI assistance may include narrative drafting, summarization, data organization, or analytical suggestions.

Verification of Quantitative Data

All quantitative information generated or organized using AI tools must be reconciled against primary data sources. This includes, but is not limited to:

Greenhouse gas emission calculations

Safety statistics

Workforce metrics

Financial figures

Performance indicators

AI-generated calculations shall not replace controlled internal calculation methodologies unless independently validated and approved.

Validation of Emission Factors and Methodologies

When AI tools are used to assist in greenhouse gas reporting or environmental disclosures, emission factors must be confirmed against official, jurisdictionally appropriate sources. The reporting methodology must align with established corporate procedures and regulatory guidance.

Under no circumstances shall emission factors or regulatory methodologies be accepted solely on the basis of AI-generated recommendations without manual confirmation.

Regulatory Citation Confirmation

All regulatory references, legislative citations, or compliance claims generated with AI assistance must be manually verified against current legislation or authoritative regulatory publications. Outdated, fabricated, or unverifiable citations shall not be included in any formal reporting document.

Narrative Interpretation Review

AI-generated summaries of regulatory requirements, ESG frameworks, or safety standards must be reviewed by competent personnel to ensure accuracy and contextual appropriateness. Where interpretation uncertainty exists, clarification shall be obtained from qualified internal or external subject matter experts.

Approval Prior to External Distribution

No AI-assisted ESG or regulatory report shall be externally distributed until it has undergone documented human review and approval in accordance with corporate governance requirements.

Documentation and Record Retention

Verification evidence shall be retained in accordance with document control procedures. This may include annotated drafts, emission factor confirmations, regulatory cross-check documentation, or completed AI Output Verification Checklists.

Training

Personnel involved in ESG or regulatory reporting shall receive training on:

AI risk awareness

Verification requirements

Regulatory citation validation

Data reconciliation procedures
