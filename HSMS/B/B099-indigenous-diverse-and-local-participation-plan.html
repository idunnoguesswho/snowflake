Indigenous, Diverse, and Local Participation Plan

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-SOC-PLAN-001Indigenous, Diverse, and Local Participation Plan

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes the historical and systemic barriers faced by Indigenous Peoples and other underrepresented groups and is committed to fostering meaningful participation through employment, procurement, and community engagement.

This plan outlines DS Home Computing Ltd’s approach to Indigenous, diverse, and local participation across its operations, supporting responsible growth, respectful engagement, and alignment with internationally recognized human rights and reconciliation principles.

Purpose

The purpose of this plan is to:

Promote equitable access to employment and subcontracting opportunities

Support Indigenous, diverse, and local participation in DS Home Computing Ltd projects

Encourage respectful engagement with Indigenous communities

Strengthen social value creation across regions of operation

Roles & Responsibilities

Senior Management

Provide oversight and commitment to participation objectives

Support allocation of resources for implementation

Project Management / Site Leadership

Promote inclusive hiring and subcontracting practices at the project level

Support community engagement activities where applicable

Human Resources / Safety / Quality / ESG

Coordinate implementation and tracking of participation initiatives

Support training, awareness, and reporting

Monitor performance indicators and trends

Suppliers and Subcontractors

Support inclusive hiring and subcontracting practices where feasible

Comply with DS Home Computing Ltd participation and human rights expectations

Scope

This plan applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd operations, projects, and activities where opportunities exist to engage Indigenous, diverse, or local workers, suppliers, or communities.

It applies to employees, contractors, subcontractors, and suppliers operating on behalf of DS Home Computing Group.

Terms and Definitions

Indigenous PeoplesDistinct cultural groups with historical ties to specific territories and recognized rights under international frameworks such as UNDRIP.

Local ParticipationEngagement of individuals, businesses, or communities located near DS Home Computing Ltd project areas.

Diverse SuppliersBusinesses owned or operated by individuals from underrepresented or equity-seeking groups.

Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC)The principle that Indigenous communities have the right to give or withhold consent to activities that may affect their lands or rights.

Procedure

Commitment to Indigenous and Diverse Inclusion

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to:

Providing equitable employment and subcontracting opportunities

Respecting Indigenous rights and cultural heritage

Engaging respectfully with communities affected by company activities

Supporting reconciliation as an ongoing process

Employment and Workforce Inclusion

DS Home Computing Ltd operates in a highly specialized technical environment. Where project requirements allow, the company seeks to support workforce inclusion through:

Fair and merit-based hiring practices

Apprenticeship, mentorship, and entry-level opportunities where feasible

Sharing job postings through diverse and Indigenous employment channels

Broad public job advertising to promote accessibility

Subcontracting and Procurement Opportunities

DS Home Computing Ltd seeks to:

Identify Indigenous, diverse, and local suppliers for goods and services

Promote transparency and fairness in subcontracting selection

Encourage subcontractors to support inclusive hiring practices

Where appropriate, supplier information may be tracked to support participation reporting.

Cultural Awareness and Training

DS Home Computing Ltd promotes cultural awareness through:

Training on Indigenous rights and reconciliation principles

Awareness of UNDRIP and respectful engagement practices

Encouraging a workplace culture that values diversity, respect, and inclusion

Community Engagement and Communication

Where operations occur near Indigenous or local communities, DS Home Computing Ltd may:

Share employment and subcontracting opportunities through local channels

Participate in community outreach or engagement initiatives

Maintain open and respectful communication

Performance Tracking and Continuous Improvement

DS Home Computing Ltd may track and review:

Indigenous and local workforce participation (where disclosed)

Engagement with Indigenous or local suppliers

Participation in cultural awareness training

Performance trends and lessons learned shall inform ongoing improvement of participation practices.

Training

Training related to Indigenous and diverse participation may include:

Cultural awareness and human rights training

Project-specific engagement expectations

Periodic refresher or awareness initiatives
