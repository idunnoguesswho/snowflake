ESG-SOC-POL-001 Human Rights Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to respecting and upholding internationally recognized human rights throughout its operations, workforce, and supply chain. This commitment is fundamental to how DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business and reflects the organization’s values of dignity, respect, safety, and fairness.

This policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s expectations for human rights conduct across all jurisdictions in which it operates and supports responsible, ethical, and sustainable business practices.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Affirm DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to human rights

Establish clear expectations for ethical and respectful conduct

Prevent human rights abuses, including discrimination, harassment, forced labour, and child labour

Provide mechanisms for reporting and addressing human rights concerns

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Demonstrate leadership and accountability for human rights commitments

Ensure appropriate resources are available for implementation and oversight

Review significant human rights risks and incidents

Human Resources/Safety/ESG Coordinator

Coordinate human rights training and awareness

Manage grievance mechanisms and investigations

Monitor human rights performance and trends

Supervisors

Promote a respectful and inclusive work environment

Monitor for potential human rights concerns

Ensure compliance with this policy at the operational level

Workers

Act in a manner that respects the rights and dignity of others

Participate in required training

Report suspected or actual human rights violations

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, temporary workers, and business partners across all operations, projects, and countries where DS Home Computing Ltd operates.

Terms and Definitions

Human RightsThe basic rights and freedoms inherent to all individuals, including the right to work in safe conditions, be free from discrimination, and live without exploitation or abuse.

Child LabourThe employment of individuals below the legal minimum working age or in work that is harmful to their physical, mental, or moral development.

Forced LabourAny work or service extracted from an individual under threat, coercion, or penalty and for which the individual has not offered themselves voluntarily.

DiscriminationUnfair or prejudicial treatment of individuals based on protected characteristics such as race, gender, age, religion, disability, or sexual orientation.

HarassmentUnwelcome conduct that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to respecting human rights and preventing adverse impacts through the following principles:

Respect and Dignity

DS Home Computing Ltd promotes a workplace culture founded on respect, dignity, and inclusion. Discrimination, harassment, and abusive behaviour are not tolerated.

Safe and Secure Work Environment

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to providing a safe and healthy work environment free from violence, intimidation, and exploitation.

Prohibition of Forced and Child Labour

DS Home Computing Ltd maintains zero tolerance for forced labour, child labour, or human trafficking in any form, including within its supply chain.

Fair Treatment and Equal Opportunity

Employment-related decisions are based on merit, qualifications, and business needs, without discrimination.

Monitoring and Due Diligence

DS Home Computing Ltd will monitor compliance with this policy through internal reviews, audits, and feedback mechanisms, and will take corrective action where required.

Reporting and Grievance Mechanisms

Individuals are encouraged to report human rights concerns without fear of retaliation. Reports may be made through supervisors, Human Resources, or designated reporting mechanisms.

Training

Human rights awareness training shall be provided as part of onboarding and periodically thereafter, as appropriate to role and risk.
