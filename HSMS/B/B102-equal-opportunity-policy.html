ESG-SOC-POL-003 Equal Opportunity Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to providing a fair, respectful, and inclusive work environment where all individuals are treated with dignity and afforded equal opportunity. Equal opportunity is a fundamental element of responsible business conduct and supports a diverse, high-performing workforce.

This policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to equal opportunity and non-discrimination across all operations and jurisdictions in which the organization operates.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Promote equal opportunity in employment and business practices

Prevent discrimination, harassment, and unequal treatment

Ensure compliance with applicable employment and human rights legislation

Support a respectful and inclusive workplace culture

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Establishing and endorsing DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to equal opportunity and non-discrimination.

Ensuring adequate resources, systems, and oversight are in place to support implementation of this Policy.

Demonstrate leadership and commitment to equal opportunity principles

Ensure fair employment practices are implemented and maintained

Address discrimination concerns and support corrective action

Human Resources/ ESG Coordinator

Implementing and maintaining this Policy.

Ensuring compliance with applicable equal opportunity and anti-discrimination legislation.

Monitoring employment practices to identify and address potential discriminatory barriers.

Providing training, guidance, and education related to equal opportunity and inclusivity.

Investigating reported concerns in a fair, timely, and confidential manner.

Supervisors

Applying this Policy consistently in day-to-day supervision, work assignments, and operational decisions.

Ensuring employment-related decisions within their control are based on qualifications, performance, and business requirements.

Promoting a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free work environment.

Identifying, addressing, and escalating potential discriminatory behaviours or barriers.

Promptly reporting concerns, complaints, or observed violations to Human Resources.

Workers

Understanding and respecting diversity within DS Home Computing Ltd and among stakeholders.

Treating others with professionalism, dignity, and respect.

Comply with this policy and applicable laws.

Valuing diverse perspectives, backgrounds, and experiences.

Promptly reporting any suspected or observed discrimination or harassment in accordance with this Policy.

Scope

This Policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, temporary workers, self-employed persons, job applicants, and any individuals conducting business on behalf of DS Home Computing Ltd.

Equal opportunity principles apply to all aspects of the employment relationship, including but not limited to:

Recruitment and hiring

Employment and assignment of work

Training and professional development

Promotion, advancement, and relocation

Compensation, benefits, and payroll administration

Workplace conditions and work environment

Termination of employment

Terms and Definitions

Equal Employment Opportunity (EEO)

The practice of providing fair and unbiased access to employment and advancement based on merit, qualifications, and job-related criteria.

Discrimination

Unfair or unequal treatment of an individual or group based on protected characteristics such as race, colour, religion, gender, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, age, national or ethnic origin, disability, genetic information, marital or family status, veteran status, or any other characteristic protected by law.

Harassment

Unwelcome conduct based on a protected characteristic that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment.

Protected Characteristics

Characteristics protected by law, including but not limited to race, colour, religion, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, pregnancy, age, national or ethnic origin, disability, genetic information, veteran status, marital or family status, or any other characteristic protected under applicable legislation.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to equal opportunity and prohibits discrimination and harassment in any form.

Equal Opportunity Commitment

Employment-related decisions shall be based on qualifications, skills, experience, and business needs, without discrimination.

Prohibition of Discrimination and Harassment

Discrimination or harassment based on protected characteristics is not tolerated in any aspect of employment or business engagement.

Fair Employment Practices

Equal opportunity principles apply to:

Recruitment and hiring

Training and development

Compensation and benefits

Promotion, transfer, and relocation

Performance management

Termination of employment

Reporting and Non-Retaliation

Individuals are encouraged to report discrimination or harassment concerns

Reports may be made through management, Human Resources, ESG, or designated reporting mechanisms

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited

Investigation and Corrective Action

Reported concerns will be assessed promptly and fairly

Appropriate corrective actions will be taken where violations are confirmed

Training

Equal opportunity and anti-discrimination awareness training shall be provided:

During onboarding

Periodically thereafter, as appropriate
