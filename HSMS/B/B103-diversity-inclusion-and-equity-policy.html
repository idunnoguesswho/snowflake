ESG-SOC-POL-004 Diversity, Inclusion, and Equity Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes that diversity, inclusion, and equity are fundamental to building a strong, innovative, and high-performing organization. A workplace that values differences and ensures fair access to opportunities supports employee well-being, ethical business practices, and long-term organizational success.

This policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to fostering a diverse workforce, cultivating an inclusive culture, and promoting equity across all operations and jurisdictions in which the organization operates.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Promote diversity in all its forms

Foster an inclusive workplace where individuals feel valued and respected

Ensure equity in access to opportunities, resources, and advancement

Prevent discrimination and inequitable treatment

Support compliance with applicable employment and human rights legislation

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Demonstrate leadership and accountability for diversity, inclusion, and equity commitments

Integrate diversity and inclusion considerations into business and workforce decisions

Support initiatives that promote fair and inclusive practices

Human Resources/ ESG Coordinator

Oversee implementation and monitoring of this policy

Provide guidance, training, and awareness programs

Manage reports, investigations, and corrective actions

Supervisors

Promote inclusive behaviours within teams

Ensure fair treatment in recruitment, training, and daily work activities

Address and escalate concerns related to discrimination or inequity

Workers

Treat others with dignity, respect, and professionalism

Contribute to an inclusive and respectful work environment

Report discrimination, harassment, or inequitable practices in good faith

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, temporary workers, vendors, and business partners across all operations, projects, and countries where DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business.

The principles of diversity, inclusion, and equity apply to all aspects of employment and engagement, including recruitment, hiring, training, professional development, promotion, compensation, and termination.

Terms and Definitions

DiversityThe presence of differences within a given setting, including but not limited to race, ethnicity, gender, age, sexual orientation, gender identity or expression, disability, socioeconomic background, cultural background, and other individual characteristics.

InclusionThe intentional and ongoing effort to ensure that all individuals feel welcomed, respected, supported, and able to participate fully in the workplace.

EquityThe fair treatment, access, opportunity, and advancement for all individuals, while recognizing and addressing historical and systemic barriers.

DiscriminationUnfair or unequal treatment of individuals based on protected characteristics under applicable laws.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to advancing diversity, inclusion, and equity across all aspects of its operations.

Commitment to Diversity

DS Home Computing Ltd values the unique perspectives and experiences that individuals bring to the organization and seeks to cultivate a workforce that reflects the diversity of the communities in which it operates.

Inclusive Workplace Culture

DS Home Computing Ltd promotes an inclusive environment where individuals feel respected, heard, and supported. Open dialogue, collaboration, and mutual respect are encouraged.

Equity in Employment Practices

Employment decisions shall be based on merit, qualifications, performance, and business needs. DS Home Computing Ltd will actively identify and address systemic barriers that may limit fair access to opportunities.

Prohibition of Discrimination

Discrimination or harassment based on protected characteristics is not tolerated in any aspect of employment or business engagement.

Reporting and Non-Retaliation

Individuals are encouraged to report concerns related to discrimination, harassment, or inequitable practices

Reports may be made through management, Human Resources, ESG, or designated reporting mechanisms

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited

Accountability

Failure to comply with this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment or contractual relationships.

Training

Diversity, inclusion, and equity awareness training shall be provided:

During onboarding

Periodically thereafter, as appropriate

Training may include inclusive leadership, cultural awareness, respectful workplace practices, and bias awareness.
