ESG-SOC-POL-005 Indigenous Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd acknowledges the unique rights, cultures, traditions, and contributions of Indigenous Peoples and recognizes the importance of respectful, meaningful, and ongoing engagement with Indigenous communities. Responsible engagement with Indigenous Peoples is an essential element of ethical business conduct, environmental stewardship, and social responsibility.

This policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to respecting Indigenous rights and fostering positive, collaborative relationships with Indigenous communities in all jurisdictions where the organization operates.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Affirm DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to respecting the rights and interests of Indigenous Peoples

Promote respectful, transparent, and meaningful engagement with Indigenous communities

Support ethical, sustainable, and responsible business practices

Ensure compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and internationally recognized standards related to Indigenous rights

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Demonstrate leadership and accountability for Indigenous engagement commitments

Ensure resources and oversight are in place to support respectful engagement

Review significant Indigenous-related risks or concerns

Safety, Quality & ESG Coordinator

Provide guidance and oversight on Indigenous engagement matters

Support monitoring, reporting, and continuous improvement

Coordinate grievance mechanisms where applicable

Supervisors

Promote respectful engagement practices at the project level

Ensure Indigenous considerations are integrated into planning and execution

Escalate concerns or issues promptly

Workers

Act respectfully and in a culturally sensitive manner

Comply with this policy and related requirements

Participate in required training and awareness initiatives

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, business partners, and activities across all projects, operations, and countries where DS Home Computing Ltd conducts business.

It applies to all interactions with Indigenous Peoples and communities, including project planning, employment and training initiatives, environmental stewardship activities, and community engagement.

Terms and Definitions

Indigenous PeoplesDistinct cultural groups who have historical continuity with pre-colonial societies and who maintain unique social, cultural, economic, and political characteristics.

Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC)The principle that Indigenous Peoples have the right to give or withhold consent to projects that may affect their lands, resources, or rights, based on adequate information provided in advance and without coercion.

Meaningful EngagementEngagement that is timely, transparent, inclusive, culturally appropriate, and conducted in good faith.

Cultural HeritageTangible and intangible heritage, including traditions, languages, knowledge systems, sacred sites, and cultural practices.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to respectful and responsible engagement with Indigenous Peoples.

Respect for Indigenous Rights

DS Home Computing Ltd recognizes and respects the inherent rights of Indigenous Peoples, including rights to land, resources, culture, self-determination, and heritage. Engagement shall be conducted in accordance with applicable laws and internationally recognized standards.

Engagement, Consultation, and Collaboration

DS Home Computing Ltd seeks to engage Indigenous communities through meaningful consultation, dialogue, and collaboration throughout the lifecycle of applicable projects. Engagement processes shall promote transparency, mutual respect, and shared understanding.

Employment, Training, and Capacity Building

DS Home Computing Ltd supports equitable access to employment, training, and development opportunities for Indigenous Peoples and seeks to increase Indigenous participation within its workforce where feasible.

Cultural Awareness and Respect

DS Home Computing Ltd promotes cultural sensitivity and respect by fostering awareness and understanding of Indigenous cultures, traditions, and perspectives. Cultural awareness training may be provided where appropriate.

Environmental Stewardship

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to minimizing environmental impacts on Indigenous lands and resources. Where applicable, the organization will collaborate with Indigenous communities on environmental monitoring, mitigation, and stewardship initiatives.

Compliance, Monitoring, and Accountability

DS Home Computing Ltd will comply with all applicable laws, regulations, agreements, and commitments related to Indigenous rights. Mechanisms will be in place to report concerns, address grievances, and support continuous improvement.

Reporting and Non-Retaliation

Individuals are encouraged to report concerns related to Indigenous engagement or rights

Reports may be made through management, ESG, or designated reporting mechanisms

Retaliation against individuals who report concerns in good faith is strictly prohibited

Training

Indigenous awareness and cultural sensitivity training shall be provided:

During onboarding where relevant

Periodically thereafter, based on role, project, and risk
