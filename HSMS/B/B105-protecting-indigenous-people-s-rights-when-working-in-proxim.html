ESG-SOC-POL-006 Protecting Indigenous People’s Rights When Working in Proximity to Indigenous Communities

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to respecting, protecting, and upholding the rights of Indigenous Peoples in all jurisdictions where it operates. Indigenous communities have distinct cultures, traditions, governance systems, and relationships to land, water, and resources that must be acknowledged and respected.

This policy establishes DS Home Computing Ltd’s expectations for conducting work responsibly and ethically when operating in proximity to Indigenous communities, ensuring engagement practices are respectful, lawful, and aligned with internationally recognized human rights standards.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Affirm DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to respecting Indigenous rights

Ensure compliance with applicable Indigenous rights legislation and international standards

Promote respectful, transparent, and meaningful engagement with Indigenous communities

Prevent adverse social, cultural, environmental, or economic impacts

Align Indigenous engagement practices with DS Home Computing Ltd’s ESG framework

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Provide oversight and accountability for compliance with this policy

Ensure adequate resources are available for engagement, training, and risk management

Support alignment with client, regulatory, and community expectations

Supervisors and Project Leads

Apply this policy during project planning and execution

Ensure respectful conduct and adherence to engagement requirements

Escalate risks, concerns, or grievances appropriately

Workers and Contractors

Conduct themselves respectfully when working near Indigenous communities

Comply with training, site protocols, and cultural expectations

Report concerns, incidents, or grievances promptly

ESG / Safety / Human Resources Functions

Support policy implementation and monitoring

Maintain records related to Indigenous engagement and grievances

Support continuous improvement and reporting

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and third parties involved in projects or activities conducted in proximity to Indigenous communities.

It applies to all jurisdictions where DS Home Computing Ltd operates and to any activity that may affect Indigenous Peoples, including impacts to land, water, resources, cultural heritage, or community well-being.

Terms and Definitions

Indigenous PeoplesDistinct peoples and communities with historical continuity to pre-colonial societies, possessing unique cultural, social, and governance systems.

Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC)The principle that Indigenous Peoples have the right to give or withhold consent to projects that may affect their lands, resources, or rights, based on full and transparent information provided in advance.

Cultural HeritageTangible and intangible attributes inherited from past generations, including sacred sites, traditions, knowledge, and cultural practices.

Grievance MechanismA formal process through which concerns or complaints may be raised, investigated, and resolved fairly and transparently.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to respecting Indigenous rights and conducting activities responsibly when operating in proximity to Indigenous communities.

Respect for Indigenous Rights

Indigenous Peoples shall be treated with dignity, respect, and fairness

Indigenous rights shall be respected in accordance with applicable laws and international standards, including UNDRIP

Indigenous communities shall be recognized as rights-holders, not stakeholders

Legal and Regulatory Compliance

All applicable federal, state/provincial, and local Indigenous rights legislation shall be complied with

Client-specific Indigenous engagement requirements shall be followed where applicable

Engagement and Consultation

Engagement with Indigenous communities shall be initiated as early as practicable

Engagement shall be conducted in a culturally appropriate, transparent, and respectful manner

Where applicable, DS Home Computing Ltd shall support efforts to obtain Free, Prior, and Informed Consent through client-led or regulatory processes

Cultural Awareness and Sensitivity

Cultural protocols, customs, and community expectations shall be respected

Sites of cultural, historical, or spiritual significance shall be identified and protected

Workers shall follow site-specific rules and cultural guidance

Environmental and Social Stewardship

Potential environmental and social impacts shall be considered during planning and execution

Where applicable, DS Home Computing Ltd shall support mitigation measures identified through assessments or engagement

Socio-Economic Participation

Indigenous employment, training, and business participation shall be supported where feasible

Procurement and subcontracting decisions shall remain fair, transparent, and capability-based

Grievance and Issue Resolution

Indigenous community concerns may be raised through established DS Home Computing Ltd grievance and reporting mechanisms

Grievances shall be addressed promptly, fairly, and transparently

Retaliation for raising concerns is prohibited

Training

Indigenous rights and cultural awareness training shall be provided:

When required by clients, regulators, or project conditions

To personnel working on or supporting projects near Indigenous communities

As part of onboarding or role-specific training where applicable

Training needs shall be reviewed periodically and updated based on operational risk and project scope.
