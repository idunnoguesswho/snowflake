ESG-SOC-POL-008 Working Conditions & Employment Practices Policy

Introduction

DS Home Computing is committed to fostering fair, respectful, and compliant working conditions for all employees. The company recognizes that positive working conditions support employee well-being, operational effectiveness, and long-term business sustainability. This policy outlines DS Home Computing’ commitment to lawful employment practices and responsible management of working conditions across all operations.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish DS Home Computing’ commitments and expectations related to working conditions, including working hours, employee well-being, remuneration practices, and respectful treatment in the workplace.

Roles and Responsibilities

Senior Management

Ensure compliance with applicable labour and employment legislation

Communicate and implement this policy across the organization

Address concerns related to working conditions in a timely and appropriate manner

Workers

Comply with company policies and procedures

Raise concerns related to working conditions in good faith through appropriate channels

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing employees across all office and field operations in Canada and the United States.

Terms and Definitions

Working ConditionsThe conditions under which employees perform their work, including working hours, rest periods, remuneration practices, benefits, workplace environment, and respectful treatment.

RemunerationCompensation provided to employees for work performed, including wages, salaries, and applicable benefits, in accordance with legal requirements and company practices.

Policy

Fair Employment Practices

DS Home Computing is committed to complying with all applicable labour and employment laws, including requirements related to minimum wage, working hours, overtime, and statutory benefits.

Working Hours and Rest

DS Home Computing respects applicable regulations related to working hours, rest periods, and time off. Work schedules are managed in consideration of operational requirements and employee well-being.

Employee Well-being and Work-Life Balance

DS Home Computing promotes a respectful and supportive work environment and encourages reasonable work-life balance where operationally feasible. Employees are encouraged to communicate workload or well-being concerns to management.

Remuneration and Benefits

DS Home Computing provides remuneration and benefits in accordance with applicable legal requirements and company practices. Compensation and benefits are reviewed periodically to ensure fairness and compliance.

Respectful Workplace

DS Home Computing maintains a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and retaliation. Concerns related to working conditions may be raised without fear of reprisal and will be addressed through established processes.

Training

Training related to working conditions, respectful workplace expectations, and applicable employment practices is provided through onboarding, policy communication, and ongoing awareness as required.
