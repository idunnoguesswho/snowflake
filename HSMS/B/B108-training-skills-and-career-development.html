ESG-SOC-POL-010 Training, Skills and Career Development

Introduction

DS Home Computing recognizes that effective training, skills development, and career growth are essential to maintaining a competent, engaged, and high-performing workforce. Supporting employee development contributes to operational excellence, employee retention, and long-term organizational success. This policy outlines DS Home Computing’ commitment to providing equitable access to training and career development opportunities aligned with business needs.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to establish DS Home Computing’ commitments and expectations related to training, skills development, and career management. This includes supporting employees throughout their employment lifecycle by providing access to learning opportunities that enhance competence, performance, and professional growth.

Roles and Responsibilities

Management

Support and promote training and development initiatives

Ensure training programs align with organizational objectives

Encourage internal career development and skill progression

Supervisors

Identify skill gaps and training needs within their teams

Support employees in applying newly acquired skills

Provide feedback related to performance and development

Human Resources/ESG/Safety Coordinator

Design and coordinate training and development programs

Monitor participation and effectiveness of training initiatives

Support equitable access to career development opportunities

Workers

Participate in required and role-specific training

Communicate development goals and training needs

Apply acquired skills to their roles and responsibilities

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing employees across all departments, projects, and locations in Canada and the United States. It covers training and development activities throughout the employee lifecycle, including onboarding, skills development, performance management, and career progression.

Terms and Definitions

Career DevelopmentThe process of acquiring skills, experience, and competencies that support professional growth and progression within the organization.

TrainingStructured learning activities designed to develop job-related knowledge, skills, and competencies.

Professional DevelopmentOngoing learning opportunities that enhance an employee’s effectiveness, expertise, or career progression.

Policy

Recruitment and Onboarding

Job descriptions define required competencies and skills

Skills and experience are considered during recruitment

Onboarding includes orientation to DS Home Computing values, expectations, and development opportunities

Training

Mandatory and role-specific training is provided as required

Training may include safety, technical, leadership, and compliance-related topics

Learning resources may include internal training, external courses, and approved e-learning platforms

Career Management and Mobility

Internal job opportunities may be communicated to support career progression

Skill development and job rotation may be used to broaden experience where operationally appropriate

Informal mentorship and supervisory guidance support career growth

Professional Development

Employees may be supported in obtaining role-relevant certifications or credentials

Access to workshops, courses, or industry events may be provided based on role and business needs

Training Objectives and Targets

DS Home Computing establishes the following objectives related to training and career development:

Support employee competence and skills development

Encourage continuous learning and professional growth

Where practicable, quantitative targets may be used to monitor training participation, including:

Targeting high participation in annual training programs

Tracking completion of mandatory and role-specific training

Training

Training related to this policy is implemented through:

New employee onboarding

Role-specific and project-specific training

Ongoing skills and competency development
