Human Rights Impact Assessment

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use

ESG-SOC-PROC-001Human Rights Impact Assessment

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to respecting internationally recognized human rights across all areas of its operations and business relationships. As part of this commitment, DS Home Computing Ltd has established a Human Rights Impact Assessment (HRIA) process to systematically identify, assess, and address actual and potential human rights risks associated with its activities.

This procedure supports DS Home Computing Ltd’s Human Rights, Ethics, Diversity and Inclusion, Indigenous, and Modern Slavery commitments and ensures that human rights considerations are integrated into project planning, operational decision-making, and supply chain management. The HRIA process is aligned with the United Nations Guiding Principles on Business and Human Rights and forms part of DS Home Computing Ltd’s broader ESG and risk management framework.

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to define a consistent, risk-based approach for conducting Human Rights Impact Assessments across DS Home Computing Ltd operations. This process is intended to:

Identify actual and potential human rights impacts

Prevent or mitigate adverse human rights impacts

Support responsible decision-making across projects and business relationships

Promote continuous improvement and accountability

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Provide oversight and support for the HRIA process

Approve mitigation measures and allocate necessary resources

Ensure integration of human rights considerations into business planning

Safety / ESG Coordinator

 Lead and coordinate the HRIA process

 Maintain HRIA documentation and records

 Monitor implementation of mitigation actions

 Support reporting and audit requirements

Human Resources

Support labour-related assessments and training initiatives

Oversee grievance mechanisms and corrective actions

Monitor workforce-related human rights risks

Supervisors

Support site-level risk identification and data collection

Implement required mitigation actions within projects

Promote awareness of human rights expectations

Workers

Comply with applicable human rights requirements

Participate in engagement activities when required

Report concerns or potential violations through established channels

Scope

This procedure applies to:

All DS Home Computing Ltd operations and activities globally

Employees, contractors, subcontractors, and temporary workers

Business partners, suppliers, and other third parties directly linked to DS Home Computing Ltd operations

The HRIA process applies to impacts that DS Home Computing Ltd may:

Cause

Contribute to

Be directly linked to through its business relationships

Terms and Definitions

Human RightsRights inherent to all human beings, regardless of nationality, place of residence, sex, ethnicity, religion, disability, or any other status. These rights include civil, political, economic, social, and cultural rights as recognized under international human rights instruments.

Human Rights Impact Assessment (HRIA)A systematic process used by DS Home Computing Ltd to identify, assess, prevent, mitigate, and address actual and potential adverse human rights impacts connected to its operations, projects, and business relationships.

Actual Human Rights ImpactA negative effect on the human rights of individuals or groups that has already occurred as a result of DS Home Computing Ltd activities or business relationships.

Potential Human Rights ImpactA negative effect on human rights that could reasonably occur in the future based on the nature of operations, location, workforce practices, or business relationships.

CauseA situation where DS Home Computing Ltd’s actions alone result in a human rights impact.

Contribute ToA situation where DS Home Computing Ltd’s actions, together with the actions of other parties, lead to a human rights impact.

Directly LinkedA situation where a human rights impact is connected to DS Home Computing Ltd’s operations, products, or services through a business relationship, even if DS Home Computing Ltd did not cause or contribute to the impact.

SeverityThe seriousness of a human rights impact, assessed based on:

Scale (gravity of harm),

Scope (number of individuals affected), and

Irremediability (extent to which harm can be reversed).

LikelihoodThe probability that a potential human rights impact will occur.

Rights-HolderAn individual or group whose human rights may be affected by DS Home Computing Ltd activities, including workers, contractors, subcontractors, community members, and Indigenous peoples.

StakeholderAny individual or group that may be affected by, or can affect, DS Home Computing Ltd operations, including employees, contractors, suppliers, communities, Indigenous groups, clients, and regulators.

Business RelationshipAny relationship DS Home Computing Ltd has with third parties connected to its operations, including suppliers, subcontractors, labour providers, joint venture partners, and clients.

MitigationActions taken to reduce the likelihood or severity of a potential or actual adverse human rights impact.

RemediationActions taken to address and remedy actual adverse human rights impacts that have occurred, including corrective actions and grievance resolution.

Grievance MechanismA formal or informal process through which workers, communities, or other stakeholders can raise concerns or complaints related to human rights impacts and receive a response.

Indigenous PeoplesDistinct cultural groups with historical continuity to lands and territories prior to colonization, possessing collective rights recognized under international standards such as the United Nations Declaration on the Rights of Indigenous Peoples (UNDRIP).

Free, Prior, and Informed Consent (FPIC)The principle that Indigenous peoples have the right to give or withhold consent to projects that may affect their lands, territories, resources, or rights, based on timely, transparent, and culturally appropriate information.

Procedures

Screening and Triggering

An HRIA shall be initiated when one or more of the following apply:

Entry into a new country, region, or operating environment

Commencement of a new project or contract

Engagement of new suppliers, subcontractors, or labour providers

Work conducted in proximity to Indigenous communities

Significant changes to operations, workforce, or supply chains

Identification of recurring grievances or incidents

Context and Risk Identification

Relevant information is collected to understand the operating context, including:

Applicable local, national, and international human rights requirements

Workforce composition and labour practices

Supply chain structure and sourcing practices

Community and Indigenous considerations

Historical grievances, incidents, or audit findings

Human rights topics considered may include, but are not limited to:

Forced labour, child labour, and human trafficking

Discrimination, harassment, and equal opportunity

Occupational health and safety

Freedom of association and fair working conditions

Indigenous rights and cultural protection

Ethical conduct, privacy, and security

Stakeholder Engagement

Where appropriate, DS Home Computing Ltd will engage relevant stakeholders to inform the assessment, which may include:

Workers and supervisors

Indigenous communities or representatives

Business partners or suppliers

Local stakeholders impacted by operations

Engagement is conducted in a respectful, transparent, and culturally appropriate manner.

Impact Assessment and Prioritization

Identified actual and potential impacts are evaluated based on:

Severity (scale, scope, and potential irreversibility of harm)

Likelihood of occurrence

Vulnerability of affected individuals or groups

Impacts are prioritized based on their potential impact on people, rather than solely on business risk.

Mitigation and Action Planning

For prioritized risks, DS Home Computing Ltd will define and implement appropriate actions to:

Prevent adverse impacts where possible

Mitigate impacts where prevention is not feasible

Remediate impacts where harm has occurred

Actions may include policy updates, operational controls, training, contractual requirements, supplier engagement, or enhanced monitoring.

Responsibilities, timelines, and resources are assigned as part of the action plan.

Implementation

Approved mitigation measures are implemented by the responsible functions and project teams. Implementation progress is tracked and documented.

Monitoring and Effectiveness

DS Home Computing Ltd will monitor the effectiveness of mitigation measures through:

Key performance indicators and qualitative feedback

Incident and grievance trend analysis

Periodic reviews and internal audits

Where controls are found to be ineffective, corrective actions will be identified and implemented.

Reporting and Integration

HRIA outcomes are:

Communicated internally to management as appropriate

Integrated into ESG reporting, audits, and risk management processes

Used to inform continuous improvement of policies, procedures, and controls

Records and Documentation

Records related to the HRIA process may include:

Screening and scoping records

Risk assessments and impact evaluations

Stakeholder engagement summaries

Action plans and corrective actions

Monitoring and review records

Records are retained in accordance with DS Home Computing Ltd document control requirements.
