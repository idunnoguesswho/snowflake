Handling Local Community Grievances Related to Company Projects

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-SOC-PROC-002Handling Local Community Grievances Related to Company Projects

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to maintaining respectful and constructive relationships with the communities in which it operates. Recognizing that company activities may impact local communities, DS Home Computing Ltd has established a formal grievance mechanism to ensure concerns are addressed in a fair, timely, and transparent manner.

This policy supports DS Home Computing Ltd’s commitment to responsible operations, community trust, and alignment with internationally recognized human rights and social responsibility standards.

Purpose

The purpose of this policy is to:

Provide a clear and accessible process for local communities to raise concerns or complaints

Ensure grievances are addressed promptly, fairly, and respectfully

Promote open communication and transparency with community stakeholders

Support DS Home Computing Ltd’s social license to operate

Roles & Responsibilities

Senior Management

Provide oversight and accountability for grievance management

Ensure adequate resources are available to support grievance resolution

Review escalated or high-risk grievances

Project Management / Site Leadership

Implement grievance handling requirements at project sites

Ensure workers are aware of grievance processes

Support investigations and corrective actions

Safety, Quality, ESG, and Human Resources Functions

Coordinate the grievance process and maintain records

Ensure confidentiality, impartiality, and consistency

Monitor trends, report outcomes, and support continuous improvement

Workers and Contractors

Conduct themselves respectfully when interacting with community members

Direct community concerns to appropriate grievance channels

Participate in grievance-related training

Scope

This policy applies to all DS Home Computing Ltd employees, contractors, subcontractors, and third parties involved in projects or activities that may impact local communities.

It covers grievances raised by community members related to:

Company operations or activities

Project impacts

Conduct of DS Home Computing Ltd personnel or contractors

Terms and Definitions

GrievanceA concern, complaint, or issue raised by a community member related to DS Home Computing Ltd activities or personnel.

Community MemberAn individual or group residing in or representing a community affected by DS Home Computing Ltd operations.

Grievance MechanismA formal process through which grievances may be raised, investigated, resolved, and tracked.

Corrective ActionMeasures implemented to address the cause of a grievance and prevent recurrence.

Policy

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to providing an effective grievance mechanism that is accessible, transparent, and respectful.

Grievance Submission

Grievances may be submitted verbally or in writing

Submission channels may include on-site personnel, designated company contacts, or published communication channels

Confidentiality shall be respected unless disclosure is required by law

Grievance Recording and Acknowledgement

All grievances shall be documented and recorded in a grievance register

Receipt of grievances shall be acknowledged within a reasonable timeframe

Records shall include details of the concern, actions taken, and outcomes

Assessment and Investigation

Grievances shall be assessed to determine severity, impact, and required response

Investigations shall be conducted impartially and may involve site visits, interviews, or document reviews

Resolution and Communication

Appropriate corrective actions shall be identified and implemented

Complainants shall be informed of outcomes and actions taken

Feedback may be sought to confirm resolution satisfaction

Escalation

Unresolved or high-risk grievances shall be escalated to senior management

External mediation may be considered where internal resolution is not feasible and where appropriate

Monitoring and Reporting

Grievances and outcomes shall be monitored for trends and recurring issues

Aggregated grievance data may be reported internally or externally while maintaining confidentiality

Training

Training on grievance handling and respectful community engagement shall be provided:

During onboarding where roles involve community interaction

To project and site leadership

Periodically, based on operational risk and project scope

Information on grievance mechanisms shall be communicated to communities through appropriate channels, such as site signage or project communications.
