ESG-SUP-POL-001 Supplier Code of Conduct

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd is committed to conducting business responsibly, ethically, and sustainably. Suppliers and contractors play a critical role in supporting DS Home Computing Ltd’s operations and reputation. As such, DS Home Computing Ltd expects its suppliers to uphold standards consistent with its values and ESG commitments.

This Supplier Code of Conduct outlines the minimum ethical, environmental, social, and performance expectations for suppliers, contractors, subcontractors, and other third parties doing business with DS Home Computing Ltd.

Purpose

The purpose of this Supplier Code of Conduct is to:

Define clear expectations for supplier behaviour and performance

Promote ethical business practices and legal compliance

Support environmental protection and social responsibility

Align supplier conduct with DS Home Computing Ltd’s ESG framework

Roles & Responsibilities

Management

Communicate this Supplier Code of Conduct to suppliers and contractors

Integrate supplier expectations into procurement and contracting processes

Monitor compliance and address identified risks or concerns

Suppliers and Contractors

Comply with this Supplier Code of Conduct and all applicable laws

Ensure their employees, subcontractors, and agents adhere to these standards

Cooperate with DS Home Computing Ltd in monitoring, audits, or corrective actions

Scope

This Code applies to all suppliers, contractors, subcontractors, consultants, and other third parties providing goods or services to DS Home Computing Ltd, regardless of location or jurisdiction.

Compliance with this Code is a condition of doing business with DS Home Computing Ltd

Terms and Definitions

SupplierAny organization or individual that provides goods or services to DS Home Computing Ltd.

Contractor / SubcontractorA third party engaged by DS Home Computing Ltd or a supplier to perform work or provide services.

Ethical ConductBusiness behaviour characterized by honesty, integrity, transparency, and accountability.

Corrective ActionMeasures taken to address and prevent recurrence of non-compliance.

Code of Conduct Requirements

Ethical Conduct

Suppliers shall:

Conduct business with honesty, integrity, and transparency

Comply with all applicable local, national, and international laws and regulations

Avoid conflicts of interest and disclose any actual or potential conflicts

Prohibit bribery, corruption, fraud, and unethical business practices

Environmental Responsibility

Suppliers shall:

Comply with applicable environmental laws and permit requirements

Minimize environmental impacts through resource conservation and waste reduction

Implement pollution prevention measures and responsible hazardous material management

Social Responsibility and Labour Practices

Suppliers shall:

Respect internationally recognized human rights

Prohibit child labour, forced labour, and human trafficking

Provide fair wages, reasonable working hours, and safe working conditions

Prohibit discrimination, harassment, and abuse

Health and Safety

Suppliers shall:

Provide a safe and healthy working environment

Comply with applicable occupational health and safety laws and standards

Implement controls to identify and mitigate workplace hazards

Community and Indigenous Considerations

Suppliers shall:

Engage respectfully with local and Indigenous communities where applicable

Avoid actions that negatively impact community well-being

Support socio-economic development where feasible

Quality and Performance

Suppliers shall:

Deliver goods and services that meet contractual and quality requirements

Implement quality management practices and continuous improvement processes

Address performance issues in a timely and cooperative manner

Monitoring, Reporting, and Corrective Action

Suppliers shall monitor compliance with this Code

Suspected or actual violations shall be reported promptly to DS Home Computing Ltd

Corrective actions shall be implemented where non-compliance is identified

Failure to comply with this Code may result in corrective action, suspension, or termination of the business relationship.

Training

Suppliers may be required to:

Acknowledge this Supplier Code of Conduct

Provide training or awareness to personnel responsible for compliance

Participate in refresher training where applicable
