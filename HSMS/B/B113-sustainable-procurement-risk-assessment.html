Sustainable Procurement Risk Assessment

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

ESG-SUP-RA-001_2025 Sustainable Procurement Risk Assessment

Reporting Period: January 1- December 31 2025

Geographic Scope: Canada and United States

Introduction

DS Home Computing Ltd recognises that responsible procurement is a critical component of its environmental, social, and governance (ESG) commitments. As a commissioning and technical services company operating across Canada and the United States, DS Home Computing relies on a network of suppliers, subcontractors, and service providers to support its operations. The sustainability practices of these third parties can influence the company’s overall risk profile and impact.

This Sustainable Procurement Risk Assessment outlines DS Home Computing Ltd’s structured and proportionate approach to identifying, evaluating, and managing potential sustainability risks within its supply chain. The assessment focuses on risks related to labour and human rights, health and safety, environmental impact, ethical conduct, and community considerations, and supports informed supplier selection and oversight.

Given the service-based nature of DS Home Computing Ltd’s operations and its limited procurement of raw materials or manufactured goods, this assessment is designed to be risk-based and scalable. It prioritises higher-risk supplier categories while avoiding unnecessary burden on low-risk suppliers, ensuring alignment with stakeholder expectations, client requirements, and recognised ESG best practices.

This risk assessment is implemented in accordance with the DS Home Computing Ltd Sustainable Procurement Policy (ESG-SUP-POL-002) and is supported by the Supplier Code of Conduct (ESG-SUP-POL-001), which establishes minimum sustainability and ethical expectations for suppliers.

Purpose

The purpose of this Sustainable Procurement Risk Assessment is to identify, evaluate, and manage potential adverse sustainability impacts within DS Home Computing Ltd’s supply chain.

This assessment supports responsible sourcing by identifying supplier types, purchasing categories, and geographic factors that may expose the company to environmental, labour, human rights, ethical, or health and safety risks, and by determining whether enhanced due diligence or additional controls are required.

Scope

This risk assessment applies to:

Subcontractors and labour providers

Equipment and service rental providers

Professional and technical service suppliers

Office, administrative, and support services

Due to the service-based nature of DS Home Computing Ltd’s operations, procurement of raw materials and manufactured goods is limited. This assessment therefore focuses on services, labour, and third-party operational support.

Risk Assessment Methodology

DS Home Computing Ltd applies a qualitative, risk-based screening approach aligned with recognized ESG and sustainable procurement principles.

Supplier sustainability risk is evaluated based on:

Nature of goods or services provided

Geographic location of supplier operations

Labour intensity and subcontracting practices

Environmental impact of services

Visibility into supplier compliance practices

Risks are categorized as Low, Medium, or High, triggering proportionate due diligence actions.

Sustainability Risk Categories

The following sustainability risk categories are considered during supplier assessment:

Labour and human rights (forced labour, child labour, fair treatment)

Health and safety practices

Environmental impact (emissions, waste, energy use)

Ethics and compliance (bribery, corruption, data protection)

Indigenous and community impacts (where applicable)

Integration with Supplier Onboarding

This risk assessment is conducted:

Prior to onboarding new suppliers where practicable

During supplier pre-qualification

When supplier scope or geographic exposure changes

When client or regulatory requirements dictate enhanced review

Results are documented and retained as part of supplier record

Monitoring and Review

Sustainability risks within the supply chain are reviewed:

Annually as part of ESG program review

When new supplier categories are introduced

Following significant operational or regulatory changes

Where sustainability practices are not aligned with expectations, additional monitoring or corrective actions may be implemented.

Conclusion

This Sustainable Procurement Risk Assessment demonstrates DS Home Computing Ltd’s structured and proportionate approach to identifying and managing sustainability risks within its supply chain. The assessment supports responsible sourcing decisions and aligns with stakeholder, client, and ESG expectations.
