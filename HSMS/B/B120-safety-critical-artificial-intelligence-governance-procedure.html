OPS-PROC-002Safety-Critical Artificial Intelligence Governance Procedure

Introduction

The integration of Artificial Intelligence into safety-related systems introduces elevated operational, legal, and ethical risk. AI systems that influence hazard identification, risk assessment, incident analysis, or safety decision-making have the potential to materially impact worker health and safety outcomes.

Given the life-safety implications of such systems, enhanced governance, validation, oversight, and monitoring controls are required beyond standard AI usage requirements.

Purpose

This Procedure establishes mandatory controls for the development, deployment, validation, monitoring, and oversight of Artificial Intelligence systems used in safety-related applications.

The objective is to ensure that AI systems:

Do not replace competent person judgment

Do not autonomously issue safety directives

Are validated prior to operational reliance

Are continuously monitored for accuracy and reliability

Do not introduce new hazards

Roles & Responsibilities

Executive Leadership

Approves deployment of safety-critical AI systems.

Reviews safety performance impact assessments.

Maintains ultimate accountability for risk exposure.

ESG Coordinator

Ensures AI system aligns with corporate safety and ESG commitments.

Reviews risk assessments and validation documentation.

Monitors governance compliance.

Information Technology/Administration

Reviews cybersecurity and data protection risks.

Ensures access controls and system integrity.

Department Managers

Oversees AI system performance.

Ensures required validation testing is conducted.

Ensures human oversight mechanisms remain active.

Competent Persons/Supervisors

Retain final authority over safety decisions.

Review AI-generated recommendations.

Override AI outputs where necessary.

Employees and Contractors

Report discrepancies between AI recommendations and observed conditions.

Follow established safety procedures, not AI outputs alone.

Scope

This Procedure applies to any Artificial Intelligence system that:

Assists in hazard identification

Generates risk ratings

Analyzes safety data trends

Supports Job Hazard Assessments

Produces safety recommendations

Influences work planning

Monitors worker behavior

Performs predictive safety analytics

This includes internally developed AI platforms and third-party AI-enabled safety software.

Terms and Definitions

Safety-Critical AI System:An AI system whose outputs may influence worker health and safety decisions, hazard controls, or operational risk mitigation.

Competent Person:An individual capable of identifying existing and predictable hazards and authorized to take corrective measures.

Human Override:A control mechanism ensuring that AI-generated recommendations may be modified, rejected, or overridden by qualified personnel.

Validation Testing:Structured evaluation of AI system outputs against known historical safety data or controlled scenarios to confirm reliability and accuracy.

Procedure

Pre-Deployment Risk Assessment

Prior to implementation of any safety-critical AI system, a formal AI Risk Assessment shall be conducted in accordance with ESG-IT-PROC-004, with additional evaluation of:

Potential failure modes

Impact of incorrect recommendations

Risk of over-reliance

Human override capability

Legal and regulatory exposure

No safety-critical AI system shall be deployed without documented Executive approval.

Validation & Testing Requirements

Before operational use, safety-critical AI systems shall undergo structured validation testing. Testing shall include:

Comparison against historical safety events

Controlled scenario analysis

Verification of risk scoring logic

Stress testing under abnormal data inputs

Validation results shall be documented and retained.

Human Oversight & Override

Safety-critical AI systems shall function strictly as decision-support tools. Final safety determinations shall remain the responsibility of competent personnel.

AI systems shall not:

Issue binding safety directives

Authorize work activities

Replace hazard assessments

Substitute for required supervisory review

Human override capability must be maintained at all times.

Monitoring & Performance Review

Following deployment, safety-critical AI systems shall be periodically reviewed for:

Accuracy of recommendations

False positives / false negatives

Near-miss correlation

Alignment with actual safety outcomes

Documented review shall occur at least annually or following any significant safety incident.

Incident Escalation

If a safety incident occurs where AI-generated guidance was a contributing factor, the incident shall trigger:

Immediate review of AI system performance

Suspension of system use if required

Revalidation prior to continued use

AI-related safety incidents shall be logged in the AI Incident Log (ESG-IT-REG-003).

Documentation & Records

The following records shall be retained:

AI Risk Assessment

Validation testing documentation

Executive approval documentation

Monitoring review summaries

AI Incident Log entries

Training

Personnel interacting with safety-critical AI systems shall receive training on:

Limitations of AI systems

Human oversight responsibilities

Override protocols

Reporting discrepancies
