Purchase Order Process for Flights & Rental Vehicles

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

QA-PRO-5.1.2Purchase Order Process for Flight & Rental Vehicles

Purpose

To establish a clear and standardized process for requesting, approving, issuing, and processing purchase orders (POs) for flights and rental vehicles. This procedure ensures efficient coordination, timely bookings, accurate project accounting, and consistent documentation.

Scope

This procedure applies to all Leads, Project Managers (PMs), Operations Managers (OMs), and administrative staff involved in arranging flights and rental vehicles for employees traveling on behalf of DS Home Computing for both Canadian and U.S. projects.

Roles & Responsibilities

Leads / Project Managers / Operations Managers

Initiate the request for all flight and rental vehicle bookings.

Provide all required information at the time of request.

Confirm budget and cost-coding details (project number, task number, billable status).

Third-Party Travel Liaison

Provide flight options, availability, and pricing.

Confirm booking only after the PO number is issued.

Ensure timely booking to avoid price increases or delays.

US Project Administrator

Issues POs for flights and rental vehicles for U.S.-based employees or contractors.

Ensures POs are created the same day or next business day after the flight option is selected.

Accounts Payable

Issues POs for Canadian-based employees or contractors.

Processes PO receipts, coding, accounts payable entries, and invoice payments.

Files documentation and ensures accurate project allocation.

Definitions

Project Number: Unique identifier for the project associated with the travel.Task Number: Sub-code indicating the specific task within the project.Billable / Non-Billable: Indicates whether the travel cost is chargeable to a client or an internal expense.Acumatica: DS Home Computing' accounting and project management software used for PO creation, cost allocation, and expense tracking.

Procedures

Initiating the Request

Leads/PMs/OMs must submit travel requests via email to the Third-Party Travel Liaison (Gord).The email must include:

Project Number

Task Number

Billable or Non-Billable status

Full name of the employee traveling

Estimated duration of the rental vehicle (if required)

CC: U.S. Project Admin and Accounts Payable.

Receiving Flight Options

The Third-Party Travel Liaison will:

Provide flight options including pricing, departure/arrival times, and carrier information.

Provide vehicle rental options if applicable.

Respond promptly to minimize booking delays.

PM or responsible party must confirm:

The selected option fits the project budget.

Flight or vehicle details meet scheduling needs.

Issuing the Purchase Order

U.S.-Based Travelers

The U.S. Project Admin issues the PO for the full quoted amount.

The PO must be issued same day or next business day once the travel option is selected.

Canada-Based Travelers

Accounts Payable issues the PO.

All POs must:

Be created for the quoted amount.

Be coded to the correct project and task in Acumatica.

Include vendor information for the Third-Party Travel Liaison.

Booking Process

Once the PO number is issued:

The Third-Party Travel Liaison completes the booking using the PO.

Confirmation details are sent to the traveler and the requesting PM.

Note: DS Home Computing is discontinuing the Excel PO Tracker for flight bookings; Acumatica will be the system of record.

Accounts Payable Process

Upon receiving invoices, Accounts Payable will:

Complete the PO receipt in Acumatica.

Perform accounts payable entry and assign costs to the correct project.

Process payment to the vendor.

File all supporting documentation in accordance with Record Retention procedures.

Frequency and Review Period

This procedure will be reviewed annually or earlier if changes occur in:

Travel process

Accounting workflows

Project requirements

Software systems
