Creating a Field Ticket

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

QA-PRO-5.1.3Creating a Field Ticket

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to define the standardized process for creating, managing, reviewing, and issuing Field Tickets in Acumatica to ensure accurate capture of labour, LOA, and equipment usage.This process supports quality objectives by reducing common data-entry errors, improving transparency for clients and internal stakeholders, and ensuring consistency between field records, payroll, and invoicing.

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing projects in Canada and the United States where labour, LOA, mileage, or truck usage is recorded through Acumatica.It covers:

Daily and weekly Field Ticket creation

Data entry requirements

Use of copy functionality for weekly records

Editing and validation of entries

Reporting and client sign-off

This procedure replaces the legacy weekly LEM process as Field Tickets become the primary method of recordkeeping.

Roles & Responsibilities

Field Leads / Supervisors

Create and maintain daily or weekly Field Tickets in Acumatica

Ensure all crew members working during the period are fully populated

Verify daily hours, LOA, and truck/mileage entries

Save entries regularly and correct errors promptly

Generate Field Ticket reports for client review and signature when required

Project Managers (PMs)

Provide clarification on scope, coding, and project task selection

Review Field Tickets as needed for completeness and alignment with project scope

Finance / Payroll

Use Field Tickets as the primary source for payroll and invoicing

Identify discrepancies and request corrections where required

Quality / Administration

Ensure the procedure is followed consistently

Support continual improvement of the Field Ticket process

Maintain this procedure and review it periodically

Definitions

Field Ticket: A daily or weekly record in Acumatica that captures labour hours, LOA, and applicable expenses by date and individual.

LOA (Living Out Allowance): Daily allowance applied per eligible worker, entered per day worked.

LEM: Legacy Labour, Equipment, and Materials report previously used; being phased out in favour of Field Tickets.

Copy Transaction Details: An Acumatica function that duplicates labour and expense line items across a selected date range to support weekly Field Tickets.

Procedures

General Principles

Field Tickets are used to improve accuracy, reduce common errors, and provide clear daily breakdowns for clients.

Entries must reflect actual daily work, not weekly totals.

All data entered must be traceable, reviewable, and auditable.

Creating a Field Ticket (Daily or Weekly)

Log in to Acumatica.

Navigate to Time & Expenses → New Field Ticket.

Select the appropriate:

Date (Sunday for full-week tickets or first day on site for mid-week starts)

Project number

Project task

Scope

Enter a brief description of work performed.

Save the Field Ticket.

Labour and LOA Entry

In the Data Entry section, add rows for each worker.

Ensure:

Hours entered are per day, not per week

LOA is entered daily, where applicable

Repeat for all crew members working during the period.

All crew expected to work during the week must be fully populated before proceeding.

Truck and Mileage Entry

Scroll to the Employee and Subcontractor Expense Entry section.

Add rows for:

Daily truck charges

Mileage, where applicable

Confirm entries match the specific workday.

Do not enter trucks or mileage in the labour section.

Weekly Field Tickets – Copy Function

Once Day 1 is fully populated, open the three-dot (⋯) menu.

Select Copy Transaction Details.

Enter:

From Date: first working day on site

To Date: last working day (must not exceed the Valid End Date)

Ensure Include dates in range is selected.

Click OK and allow the system time to populate entries.

Review and edit daily hours or LOA as required.

Editing and Validation

Hours and LOA can be edited by double-clicking the relevant field.

Missing personnel or entries may be added manually at any time prior to submission.

Field Leads must confirm:

All personnel are accounted for

All applicable days are populated

Entries align with actual work performed

Reporting and Client Sign-Off

Open the three-dot (⋯) menu.

Select Unpriced / Ungrouped report.

Export to PDF or print directly.

Provide the Field Ticket to the client for review and signature, if required.

Retain signed copies in the project records.
