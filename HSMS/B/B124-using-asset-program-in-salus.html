Using Asset Program in Salus

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

QA-PRO-5.2.3 Using Asset Program in Salus

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to establish a standardized method for managing DS Home Computing-owned assets within Salus, including vehicles, trailers, tools, safety equipment, and specialized commissioning equipment.

This procedure ensures compliance with COR preventative maintenance requirements, supports safe operation, centralizes documentation, and enables accurate tracking of maintenance, inspections, fuel use, mileage/kilometres, and equipment location.

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing-owned assets used in Canada and the USA, including but not limited to:

Vehicles and trailers

Gas monitors and calibratable equipment

Rubber insulating gloves (Arc Flash gloves)

Tools and test instruments (e.g., flukes, meters, generators)

Any other asset requiring tracking, recertification, maintenance, or documentation

This procedure applies to Company Admin, Lead, and Worker user levels in Salus, with permissions defined below.

Roles & Responsibilities

Company Admin

Create and manage all assets within Salus

Assign and archive assets

Upload insurance, registration, calibration, certificates, and supporting documents

Review maintenance cases and verify completion

Ensure assets are removed from service when unsafe or expired

Build and maintain custom forms (logbooks, maintenance uploads, fuel tracking)

Leads

Update asset information as required (mileage, notes, site assignment)

Create logbook entries and maintenance cases

Upload maintenance documents

Send reminders for required maintenance

Assign assets to workers or sites

Ensure compliance with inspection and calibration schedules

Workers

Complete vehicle inspections and assign the correct asset

Submit maintenance or logbook forms as required

Report deficiencies or damage immediately

Record fuel, mileage, or maintenance details using assigned forms

Definitions

Asset: Any DS Home Computing-owned equipment tracked in Salus (vehicles, tools, PPE requiring certification, etc.)

Maintenance Case: A structured record of preventative or corrective maintenance, including pending, in-progress, and completed statuses.

Logbook: A digital record for mileage, usage notes, maintenance details, and attachments.

Preventative Maintenance: Scheduled maintenance required to ensure assets remain in safe working condition and compliant with COR, OSHA, and CSA standards.

Procedures

Accessing the Asset Tab (Lead & Company Admin Only)

Log into Salus.

Select Assets from the left-side navigation menu.

Review the following tabs as needed:

Active Assets

Archived Assets

Maintenance Records

Types (asset categories)

Note: Workers cannot access the Asset tab but can still submit inspections and asset-linked forms.

Creating Asset Types (Company Admin Only)

Select Types.

Click New Type.

Enter the category (e.g., Vehicles, Trailers, Gas Monitors, Rubber Gloves).

Save.

Asset types help organize equipment and ensure consistency in maintenance records.

Creating or Updating an Asset

Navigate to Active Assets.

Select Add New Asset (Company Admin) or choose an existing asset to update.

Enter or update:

Asset name and type

Assigned user

Vehicle details (make, model, VIN)

Current mileage/kilometres

Notes or condition details

Upload applicable documentation (insurance, registration, calibration).

Save changes.

Using the Logbook (Lead & Company Admin)

Open the asset profile.

Select Logbook.

Enter details such as:

Mileage/kilometres

Site location

Notes (e.g., “Recertified,” “Calibrated April 2025”)

Add files (calibration certificates, photos, invoices).

Save entry.

Logbooks provide an ongoing record of use, condition, and history.

Creating and Managing Maintenance Cases

Open the asset profile.

Select Maintenance → Create Maintenance Case.

Select a status:

Pending – Maintenance required

In Progress – Asset at shop or temporarily unavailable

Completed – Maintenance finished

Add:

Notes

Cost details

Assigned worker

Attachments (repairs, recertification, calibration)

Change the status as the maintenance progresses.

Mark the asset out of service if required.

Sending Maintenance Reminders

From within a maintenance case, select Send Reminder.

Choose the date and recipient.

Enter message (e.g., “Please take gloves for recertification”).

Send email.

A copy of the reminder is stored in the case record.

Uploading and Managing Documents

Open the asset profile.

Select Documents.

Upload files such as:

Insurance

Registration

Calibration certificates

Maintenance invoices

Documents may be downloaded or emailed to workers as needed.

Assigning Assets to Workers or Sites

Open the asset.

Select Assign.

Choose worker or site location.

Save assignment.

Worker-Level Processes (Without Asset Tab Access)

Workers may use forms to support asset tracking:

Vehicle inspections → Auto-attached to assigned asset

Logbook forms → Mileage, notes, fuel

Maintenance forms → Upload repairs (windshield, tires, oil changes, etc.)

Fuel tracking forms → Litres/gallons as required

These serve as manual workarounds when workers cannot access the Asset tab directly.

Vehicles and Trailers

For vehicles and trailers, ensure:

Make/model/VIN are updated

Assigned worker is accurate

Mileage is updated during inspections

Oil changes and repairs are logged under Maintenance

Photos are uploaded as necessary

All inspections automatically attach to the asset.

Frequency and Review Period

Logbook entries should be updated as required based on usage.

Maintenance cases must be opened immediately when maintenance is required.

Asset documentation should be reviewed monthly by the Safety/QA Team.

Vehicle and trailer details must be verified semi-annually.

This procedure will be reviewed annually or as regulatory requirements change.
