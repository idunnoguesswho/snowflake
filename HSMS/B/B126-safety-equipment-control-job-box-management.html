Safety Equipment Control & Job Box Management

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use
🔍

QA-PRO-5.2.5Safety Equipment Control & Job Box Management

Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to establish a standardized system for the distribution, control, tracking, and recovery of safety equipment used on DS Home Computing projects.

This procedure introduces the Safety Job Box program, which centralizes safety equipment management at the project level to improve accountability, reduce unnecessary purchasing, ensure inspection compliance, and maintain control of life-critical safety equipment.

Scope

This procedure applies to all DS Home Computing operations where safety equipment or personal protective equipment (PPE) is issued for project work.

It applies to:

Field technicians

Site Leads

Resource Coordinators

Warehouse / shop personnel

Project management staff

The procedure governs all safety equipment issued to project sites, including equipment transported in Safety Job Boxes.

Roles & Responsibilities

Warehouse / Shop

Preparing Safety Job Boxes prior to project mobilization

Verifying equipment inventory before shipment

Inspecting returned equipment after project completion

Managing long-term storage of safety equipment

Resource Coordinator

Coordinating Safety Job Box shipment to project locations

Assigning equipment to projects

Maintaining tracking records where applicable

Site Lead

Receiving the Safety Job Box at the project site

Maintaining control of equipment while on site

Issuing equipment to workers when required

Ensuring controlled safety equipment is returned at project completion

Workers

Proper use and care of issued equipment

Returning equipment to the Site Lead when no longer required

Management

Supporting implementation of this program

Ensuring compliance with equipment accountability requirements

Definitions

Safety Job BoxA project-specific container that holds required safety equipment and consumable PPE for a project site.

Controlled Safety EquipmentSafety equipment that must be returned, tracked, inspected, and maintained due to its critical safety function.

Examples include:

Fall protection harnesses

Arc flash suits

Gas monitors

Specialty electrical PPE

Consumable PPEPersonal protective equipment intended for single use or short-term use that is not expected to be returned.

Examples include:

Safety glasses

Hearing protection

Gloves

Employee-Issued PPEClothing or equipment issued directly to an employee for personal use during employment.

Examples include:

FR pants

FR shirts

Chain of CustodyThe documented transfer of responsibility for equipment between personnel throughout a project lifecycle.

Gas MonitorA portable safety device used to detect hazardous gas concentrations in the work environment.

Procedures

Safety Job Box Program

DS Home Computing will utilize project-specific Safety Job Boxes to distribute and control safety equipment.

Each project will receive a prepared job box containing necessary safety equipment and PPE required for the scope of work. The job box will remain on site for the duration of the project and will be returned to the warehouse upon project completion.

Safety Equipment Classification

Safety equipment is categorized into three primary groups.

Employee-Issued PPE

Items issued directly to individual employees:

FR pants

FR shirts

Standard PPE clothing

These items are not expected to be returned.

Consumable PPE

Items used and replaced regularly:

Safety glasses

Hearing protection

Gloves

These items are supplied within the Safety Job Box but are not tracked as returnable assets.

Controlled Safety Equipment

Critical safety equipment that must be tracked and returned:

Fall protection harnesses

Arc flash suits

Gas monitors

Specialty electrical PPE

These items remain property of DS Home Computing and must be returned after project completion.

Equipment Accountability Process

The following chain of custody will be used for all Safety Job Box equipment:

Warehouse / Shop→ Resource Coordinator→ Site Lead→ Worker→ Site Lead→ Resource Coordinator→ Warehouse

This process ensures that responsibility for equipment is clearly defined at every stage of the project.

Equipment Inspection and Maintenance

Controlled safety equipment must be inspected according to manufacturer recommendations and applicable safety standards.

Returned equipment will be inspected prior to being redeployed to another project.

Any damaged or expired equipment will be removed from service.

Gas Monitor Standardization

Gas monitors are considered life-critical safety equipment and must be reliable, serviceable, and capable of supporting calibration programs.

DS Home Computing will prioritize the use of serviceable gas monitors within the Safety Job Box program.

Serviceable monitors allow:

Sensor replacement

Battery replacement or recharge

Full calibration capability

Extended service life through maintenance

This supports long-term equipment management and improved operational reliability.

Benefits

Implementation of the Safety Job Box program provides several operational improvements:

Reduced unnecessary PPE purchasing

Improved accountability for safety equipment

Better inspection and maintenance control

Faster project mobilization

Standardization of safety equipment across projects
