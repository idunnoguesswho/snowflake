Job Tasks
1
Accept driving assignment / pre-trip fitness check
PRE: 16 H POST: 6 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Fatigue, illness, medication effects, impairment, stress, or distraction affecting judgment and reaction time.
Controls
  • AWorker confirms fit for work before driving and reports fatigue, illness, medication concerns, or impairment before operating a vehicle.
  • AComplete fatigue self-assessment when travel is long distance, after-hours, early morning, adverse weather, or fatigue concern is identified.
  • ASupervisor to delay, reassign, add rest break, or arrange alternate driver if worker is not fit to drive.
  • ANo alcohol, unauthorized drugs, or impairing substances before or during driving.
Required Forms: C.049 Fatigue Self-Assessment · C.033 when required
2
Pre-use vehicle inspection / driver authorization
PRE: 9 H POST: 6 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Breakdown, tire failure, equipment defect, expired documents, missing emergency equipment, or unsafe vehicle condition.
Controls
  • ADriver must be authorized and hold a valid licence for the vehicle class.
  • AComplete documented vehicle pre-use inspection before first use of the day or when taking over a vehicle.
  • AConfirm registration, insurance, first aid kit, fire extinguisher, roadside kit, lights, tires, fluid leaks, mirrors, wipers, brakes, horn, and seatbelts.
  • ARemove vehicle from service and report defects that could affect safe operation.
Required Forms: C.050 Driver Auth/Vehicle Pre-Use · C.020/C.010 if defect caused incident/near miss
3
360 before driving / vehicle movement
PRE: 16 H POST: 4 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Striking people, property, parked vehicles, animals, equipment, low objects, or trailer hazards while pulling away or backing.
Controls
  • AComplete a 360 walkaround before moving the vehicle after parking, loading, unloading, stopping at site, or changing drivers.
  • ACheck all sides, blind spots, ground hazards, overhead hazards, trailer connection, wheel chocks, load security, and clearance.
  • AUse spotter when available for backing, congested sites, tight parking, or trailer movement.
  • ABack into parking spaces when practicable to reduce future backing risk.
Required Forms: C.052 360 Before Driving Checklist
4
Plan journey / long distance, winter, remote, or unfamiliar travel
PRE: 12 H POST: 6 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Route delays, severe weather, poor road conditions, loss of communication, fatigue, emergency response delays, or insufficient fuel.
Controls
  • AComplete a Journey Management Form for out-of-town, remote, winter/adverse weather, after-hours, long-distance, or client-site travel as required by policy.
  • AReview route, weather, road conditions, fuel stops, parking, communication method, emergency contacts, and expected arrival/check-in times.
  • ACarry emergency kit, first aid kit, warm clothing/blanket, phone/radio, charger, and seasonal equipment.
  • APostpone or reroute trip when risk cannot be controlled.
Required Forms: C.051 Journey Management Form
5
General driving
PRE: 16 H POST: 6 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Collision, traffic violation, distracted driving, wildlife, pedestrians, cyclists, other vehicles, or loss of control.
Controls
  • EUse seatbelts, headlights, mirrors, wipers, brakes, and vehicle safety systems.
  • AObey traffic laws, speed limits, seatbelt requirements, signs, and traffic controls.
  • ANo handheld phone use; stop in a safe location before calling, texting, programming GPS, or reading messages.
  • ADo not eat, handle paperwork, or complete forms while driving.
  • ADrive to conditions and increase following distance in poor weather, gravel, construction zones, or wildlife areas.
Required Forms: C.047 Cell Phone Acknowledgement · C.020/C.010 if incident/near miss
6
Driving for extended / long distances
PRE: 16 H POST: 6 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Fatigue, reduced attention, slower reaction time, microsleep, body strain, and reduced ability to respond to hazards.
Controls
  • AUse journey plan and scheduled check-ins for long travel.
  • ATake planned rest breaks, stretch, hydrate, and stop if drowsy.
  • AUse a second driver or overnight stop when needed.
  • ADo not continue driving when unable to stay alert; contact supervisor for direction.
Required Forms: C.049 Fatigue Self-Assessment · C.051 Journey Management
7
Driving with a trailer or loaded vehicle
PRE: 16 H POST: 4 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Jackknifing, tipping, trailer detachment, poor load distribution, extended stopping distance, striking objects on turns.
Controls
  • AConfirm hitch, chains, safety latch, lights, brakes, breakaway cable, tires, and load securement before travel and during stops.
  • AReduce speed, allow additional stopping distance, and take turns wider than normal.
  • AConfirm load weight and distribution are within vehicle/trailer limits.
  • AUse spotter and 360 walkaround before backing or repositioning.
Required Forms: C.002 Vehicle Inspection · C.049 · C.050 · C.052
8
Post-trip / vehicle return
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Unreported vehicle damage or defects, missing equipment, fuel/mileage record gaps, or delayed corrective action.
Controls
  • AReport vehicle damage, defects, incidents, near misses, or emergency equipment use immediately.
  • ARecord mileage/fuel/expenses as required.
  • ARemove garbage, return equipment, and restock safety supplies.
  • ASupervisor reviews defect reports and ensures repair or removal from service.
Required Forms: C.050 · C.020 · C.010 · C.005 if corrective action required
