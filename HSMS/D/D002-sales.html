Job Tasks
1
Customer / site visit planning
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Poor understanding of site hazards, access restrictions, visitor rules, weather, working alone, or travel requirements.
Controls
  • AConfirm site contact, address, parking, PPE, visitor orientation, emergency meeting point, and communication method before travel.
  • AComplete journey plan when travel is remote, out of town, after-hours, winter/adverse weather, or unfamiliar.
  • AReview respectful workplace/visitor requirements before entering client/customer areas.
Required Forms: C.051 Journey Management · C.039 when hosting visitors
2
Customer interaction / sales call
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Disrespectful behaviour, harassment, violence, intimidation, or conflict with customer, worker, or public.
Controls
  • AMaintain respectful communication and stop interaction if behaviour becomes unsafe.
  • AUse public/visible meeting areas where practicable; do not enter isolated areas if uncomfortable.
  • AReport harassment, violence, threats, or concerning behaviour to supervisor/manager.
  • AFollow non-retaliation and complaint process.
Required Forms: C.028 Violence/Harassment · C.038 Non-Retaliation · C.020 if incident
3
Travel to / from customer or site
PRE: 16 H POST: 6 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Collision, breakdown, fatigue, poor road/weather conditions, and distracted driving.
Controls
  • AFollow D.001 Driving controls, B.047–B.050, and complete required travel/vehicle forms.
  • AConduct 360 before moving vehicle and documented inspection where required.
  • ANo handheld cell phone use while driving; pull over safely before calls/texts.
Required Forms: C.049 · C.050 · C.051 · C.052
4
Handling customer information, quotes, photos, and documents
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Loss of confidential information, privacy breach, unauthorized photos, or inappropriate use of company devices/social media.
Controls
  • ACollect only information required for legitimate business purposes.
  • AStore customer/client information in approved company locations.
  • ADo not post customer, client, jobsite, or worker information/photos online without authorization.
  • AUse company-approved devices and access controls.
Required Forms: C.029 Privacy · C.031 Internet Use Acknowledgement · C.046 BYOD Agreement
5
Office/computer work for quotes and follow-up
PRE: 6 M POST: 4 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Ergonomic strain, eye strain, stress from deadlines, and repetitive motion injuries.
Controls
  • AMaintain ergonomic setup, take micro-breaks, stretch, rotate tasks, and manage workload deadlines.
  • ARequest assistance or schedule adjustment when workload creates fatigue or stress concern.
  • AReport discomfort early.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment · C.044 if performance/workload follow-up
6
Cash / expense / cash advance handling
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Loss of funds, inaccurate records, robbery risk, or unauthorized advance/expense.
Controls
  • AUse approved cash advance process before funds are issued.
  • AKeep receipts and submit reconciliation by required date.
  • ESecure cash and avoid carrying unnecessary amounts.
  • AReport lost/stolen funds immediately.
Required Forms: C.030 Cash Advance · C.007/C.008 if used
