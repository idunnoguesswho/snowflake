Job Tasks
1
Daily supervision / work planning
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Unclear expectations, missed hazards, incomplete forms, or workers starting without required controls.
Controls
  • AConfirm hazard assessment/FLHA and required C forms are complete before work begins.
  • AReview applicable B policies and D JSAs during planning and toolbox talks.
  • AAssign competent workers and verify training/authorization requirements.
  • AStop work if hazards or required controls are missing.
Required Forms: C.001 FLHA · C.003 Toolbox Meeting · C.021 Training Record · applicable C.028–C.052
2
Approving travel / field visits
PRE: 16 H POST: 6 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Uncontrolled driving risk, fatigue, weather exposure, remote work, or incomplete journey plan.
Controls
  • AReview need for travel, driver fitness, vehicle condition, weather, route, and check-in requirements.
  • ARequire journey management and fatigue self-assessment when triggers apply.
  • AVerify driver authorization, pre-use inspection, and 360 walkaround requirements.
  • APostpone travel when risk cannot be controlled.
Required Forms: C.049 · C.050 · C.051 · C.052
3
Train, mentor, or correct employees
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Conflict, harassment complaint, reprisal concern, unclear communication, or psychological stress.
Controls
  • ACommunicate expectations respectfully and privately.
  • AUse progressive discipline/non-retaliation process when required.
  • ADocument training, coaching, complaints, and corrective actions.
  • AEscalate violence, harassment, privacy, or impairment concerns immediately.
Required Forms: C.013 Discipline · C.021 Training Record · C.028 · C.038 · C.005 Corrective Action
4
Meetings with employees, clients, or visitors
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Disgruntled person, violence, harassment, privacy breach, or unsafe meeting environment.
Controls
  • EUse suitable meeting room with clear exit route and adequate lighting.
  • AKeep sensitive meetings confidential and limit attendance to those with need to know.
  • AUse visitor sign-in and escort rules for non-workers.
  • AEnd or relocate meeting if behaviour becomes unsafe.
Required Forms: C.028 · C.029 · C.039 Visitor Sign-In · C.020 if incident
5
Managing records and employee personal information
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Privacy breach, loss of records, unauthorized access, or improper disclosure.
Controls
  • AStore HR, safety, medical, payroll, and investigation records in approved secure locations.
  • AShare personal information only with authorized persons for legitimate business/legal purposes.
  • ADo not leave records visible in vehicles, public areas, or shared devices.
  • AReport suspected privacy incidents promptly.
Required Forms: C.029 Privacy · C.011 Document Revision
6
Manual handling / site support
PRE: 9 H POST: 3 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Back strain, overexertion, slips/trips/falls, or poor body mechanics.
Controls
  • EUse carts, dollies, or mechanical aids when available.
  • APlan lift, reduce load size, team lift when needed, keep load close, avoid twisting, and take breaks.
  • PUse gloves and CSA footwear as required.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment · C.020 if injury
