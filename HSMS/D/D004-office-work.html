Job Tasks
1
Daily visual inspection of office / work area
PRE: 4 M POST: 1 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Slip/trip/fall hazards, blocked exits, poor housekeeping, poor lighting, or unsafe equipment.
Controls
  • AInspect work areas, walkways, exits, cords, mats, lighting, and equipment.
  • ACorrect hazards immediately or report for corrective action.
  • AKeep exits, aisles, and emergency equipment clear.
Required Forms: C.023 Hazard Report · C.005 Corrective Action if needed
2
Computer / clerical work
PRE: 6 M POST: 4 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Ergonomic strain, repetitive motion, eye strain, fatigue, and stress.
Controls
  • AComplete ergonomic setup or assessment when discomfort or new workstation is identified.
  • AAdjust chair, monitor, keyboard, mouse, lighting, and work surface.
  • ATake micro-breaks and stretch; rotate tasks where practicable.
  • AReport discomfort early.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment · C.032 if remote work
3
Use of devices, internet, email, social media, and BYOD
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Privacy breach, cyberbullying, inappropriate content, malware, loss of company/client information, or reputational harm.
Controls
  • AUse company systems for business purposes and follow computer/social networking/internet policy.
  • AUse strong passwords and lock screens when unattended.
  • ADo not share confidential information, client photos, worker information, or workplace issues online.
  • ABYOD use requires authorization and compliance with security expectations.
Required Forms: C.031 Internet Use Acknowledgement · C.046 BYOD Agreement · C.029 if privacy incident
4
Cell phone use in workplace
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Distraction, reduced awareness, privacy breach, or inappropriate recording/photos.
Controls
  • AUse cell phones only where safe and appropriate.
  • ADo not use phone while driving or performing safety-sensitive tasks.
  • ADo not take photos/video/audio of workers, clients, visitors, or confidential materials without authorization.
Required Forms: C.047 Cell Phone Authorization · C.029 if privacy incident
5
Visitors, clients, or contractors in office
PRE: 8 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Violence, harassment, unauthorized access, injury to visitor, or confidentiality breach.
Controls
  • AVisitors sign in/out and are escorted or directed as required.
  • AReview visitor rules when entering work areas.
  • AKeep confidential information out of visitor view.
  • AReport disrespectful, threatening, or unsafe behaviour immediately.
Required Forms: C.039 Visitor Sign-In · C.028/C.020 if incident
6
Cash, petty cash, gifts, or entertainment
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Theft, robbery, conflict of interest, inaccurate records, unauthorized cash advance, or fraud concern.
Controls
  • ESecure funds and avoid unnecessary cash on site.
  • AUse approved cash advance, expense, and gift disclosure processes.
  • AKeep receipts and submit reconciliation by required date.
  • AReport loss, theft, or suspected misuse immediately.
Required Forms: C.030 Cash Advance · C.041 Gifts Disclosure · C.007/C.008 if used
7
Manual handling and storage
PRE: 9 H POST: 3 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Back injury, strain, falling file cabinets, overreaching, or dropped materials.
Controls
  • AStore heavy items low, open one file drawer at a time, use step ladder instead of chair, and avoid overreaching.
  • AUse carts, reduce load size, or ask for help with heavy boxes/water jugs.
  • AFollow back injury prevention controls.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment · C.020 if injury
8
Inclement weather / office closure decision
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Unsafe commute, delayed emergency response, slips/falls in parking areas, or staffing confusion.
Controls
  • AMonitor weather/road conditions and communicate delays, work-from-home, or closure decisions.
  • ADo not require unsafe travel when work can be delayed or completed remotely.
  • AKeep entrances/walkways treated and report hazards.
Required Forms: C.045 Inclement Weather Decision · C.032 if WFH used
