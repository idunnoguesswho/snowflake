Working from Home

Job Tasks
1
Approve and set up work-from-home arrangement
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Unapproved work location, unsuitable workspace, poor communication, privacy exposure, or worker isolation.
Controls
  • AComplete work-from-home agreement/checklist before regular remote work.
  • AConfirm emergency address, work hours, communication method, equipment, privacy expectations, and reporting process.
  • ASupervisor confirms worker knows check-in expectations and incident reporting process.
Required Forms: C.032 Work from Home Checklist
2
Home workstation / computer work
PRE: 6 M POST: 4 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Ergonomic strain, eye strain, repetitive motion, poor lighting, and fatigue.
Controls
  • AComplete ergonomic self-assessment and adjust workstation.
  • AUse suitable chair/work surface, neutral posture, screen height, keyboard/mouse position, lighting, and breaks.
  • AReport discomfort early and request equipment/support if needed.
Required Forms: C.032 WFH Checklist · C.048 Back Injury Assessment
3
Privacy, BYOD, and confidential documents at home
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Family/visitor access to confidential records, device loss, data breach, unauthorized printing/disposal, or insecure Wi-Fi.
Controls
  • AStore records securely and keep screens/documents out of view of others.
  • ALock devices when unattended and use approved storage locations.
  • ADo not use personal devices for company work unless BYOD authorization is in place.
  • AShred/securely dispose of confidential paper records.
  • AReport suspected privacy incident immediately.
Required Forms: C.029 Privacy · C.046 BYOD Agreement
4
Working alone / emergency response at home
PRE: 4 M POST: 1 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Delayed help after injury/illness, communication failure, or emergency at home office.
Controls
  • AKeep phone/communication device available during working hours.
  • AConfirm emergency address and contact method with supervisor.
  • AReport communication outages or inability to be reached.
  • ACall 911 for emergencies and notify supervisor as soon as safe.
Required Forms: C.032 WFH Checklist · C.020 if incident
5
Stress, fatigue, family interruptions, and workload management
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Mental fatigue, distraction, reduced quality, conflict, or extended working hours.
Controls
  • ASet work hours and boundaries with household members where practicable.
  • ATake breaks and report fatigue/stress concerns before errors or unsafe work occur.
  • AUse scheduling, workload review, and supervisor support when deadlines create stress.
  • AFollow respectful communication expectations for calls/messages.
Required Forms: C.049 Fatigue Self-Assessment · C.044 if workload/performance review needed
6
Home office fire / electrical safety
PRE: 8 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Fire, overloaded circuits, tripping cords, poor ventilation of electronics, smoke/CO alarm failure.
Controls
  • EUse surge protection and keep equipment ventilated.
  • AKeep cords in good condition and out of walkways.
  • ATest smoke/CO alarms monthly; maintain fire escape plan.
  • ADo not leave candles or heat sources unattended near work area.
Required Forms: C.032 WFH Checklist · C.020 if incident
