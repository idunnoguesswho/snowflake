Computer Repair

Emergency Contact
911 · Established at site/client location

Established at site or client location

Computer repair competency · Privacy/confidentiality · Electrical awareness · Respectful workplace · Defensive driving
Approval Required

Job Tasks
1
Client/site travel for computer repair
PRE: 16 H POST: 6 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Driving collision, fatigue, severe weather, breakdown, or unfamiliar location.
Controls
  • AFollow D.001 Driving and applicable fleet/journey/360 controls.
  • AComplete journey management when travel trigger applies.
  • AConduct 360 before moving vehicle and pre-use inspection when required.
Required Forms: C.049 · C.050 · C.051 · C.052
2
Client interaction / work in occupied space
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Harassment, violence, disrespectful behaviour, working alone, unauthorized access, or customer complaint.
Controls
  • AConfirm site contact and work area; maintain respectful communication.
  • AAvoid isolated work where practicable; maintain communication/check-in if working alone.
  • AStop work and leave area if behaviour becomes unsafe.
  • AReport incident or complaint promptly.
Required Forms: C.028 Violence/Harassment · C.039 Visitor Sign-In · C.020 if incident
3
Accessing computers, networks, and customer information
PRE: 12 H POST: 3 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Privacy breach, data loss, unauthorized access, malware exposure, or mishandling credentials.
Controls
  • AAccess only systems/data required to complete authorized work.
  • ADo not copy, photograph, disclose, or retain customer/employee personal information unless authorized.
  • AProtect passwords, credentials, files, photos, and devices from unauthorized access.
  • AReport suspected privacy/security incident immediately.
Required Forms: C.029 Privacy · C.031 Internet Use Acknowledgement · C.046 BYOD Agreement
4
Computer repair / troubleshooting equipment
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Electric shock, burns, cuts from sharp components, stored energy, or equipment damage.
Controls
  • EDisconnect power and isolate equipment before opening covers where required.
  • AFollow manufacturer instructions and safe job procedures.
  • AKeep liquids away from electrical equipment.
  • PUse eye protection/gloves when needed for sharp edges or cleaning.
Required Forms: C.001 FLHA if field hazard assessment required
5
Manual handling of computers, monitors, printers, and boxes
PRE: 9 H POST: 3 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Back strain, pinch points, dropped equipment, awkward posture, or overexertion.
Controls
  • AAssess weight and path before lifting.
  • AUse carts, team lift, reduce loads, keep load close, and avoid twisting.
  • ATake breaks for repetitive set-up/teardown.
  • PWear suitable footwear and gloves as required.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment
6
Cleaning electronics / using chemical products
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Chemical exposure, inhalation, skin/eye irritation, fire risk from aerosol/solvents.
Controls
  • AUse products as labelled and ensure ventilation.
  • AKeep SDS available for controlled products.
  • ADo not spray cleaners directly into energized equipment.
  • PUse gloves/eye protection as required.
Required Forms: C.020 if exposure/incident
