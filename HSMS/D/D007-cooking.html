Cooking

JSA Leader: Katie Snow, Manager · Revision 1
Revision focus: Updated to include fitness/fatigue, back injury prevention, chemical cleaning, and incident reporting controls.
PPE Required
Gloves, heat protection, eye protection, non-slip footwear as required
Emergency Contact
911
Emergency Meeting Point
To be determined at home office / kitchen area
Tools & Equipment
Knives, cutting boards, stove/oven, microwave, kettle, cleaning supplies, fire extinguisher, first aid kit
Training / Skills Required
Cooking competency · First aid · Fire response · WHMIS/label awareness as applicable
Approval Required
High risk work requires Supervisor/Management approval per risk matrix

Job Tasks
1
Pre-task fitness / kitchen preparation
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Fatigue, illness, distraction, poor housekeeping, blocked exits, or missing first aid/fire controls.
Controls
  • AWorker confirms fit for work and reports illness/fatigue before beginning.
  • AInspect kitchen for spills, clutter, blocked exits, working smoke alarms, and fire extinguisher location.
  • AWash hands and follow hygiene requirements.
Required Forms: C.049 if fatigue concern · C.020 if incident
2
Preparing ingredients / cutting
PRE: 9 H POST: 4 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Cuts, punctures, repetitive motion, or cross-contamination.
Controls
  • EUse stable cutting board and proper sharp knife for task.
  • AKeep fingers clear, cut away from body, and do not rush.
  • ASeparate raw/cooked foods and clean surfaces.
  • PCut-resistant glove if appropriate.
Required Forms: C.020 if injury
3
Using stove, oven, kettle, microwave, or hot surfaces
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Burns, scalds, fire, smoke, or unattended cooking.
Controls
  • ANever leave active cooking unattended.
  • ATurn pot handles inward and keep flammables away.
  • PUse oven mitts/heat protection.
  • EKeep fire extinguisher available and know emergency response.
Required Forms: C.020 if incident
4
Handling hot oil, boiling water, or heavy pots
PRE: 9 H POST: 4 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Splashes, burns, scalds, muscle strain, or dropped load.
Controls
  • EUse lids/splash guards where practicable.
  • AReduce container weight, move slowly, and ask for help with heavy/hot items.
  • AKeep pathway clear before moving hot items.
  • PUse heat-resistant gloves/apron as needed.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment · C.020 if injury
5
Cleaning kitchen and chemicals
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Slips, chemical exposure, burns from cleaners, or mixing incompatible products.
Controls
  • AClean spills immediately and use wet-floor warning when others are present.
  • ARead labels and never mix cleaning chemicals.
  • EVentilate area.
  • PWear gloves/eye protection as required.
Required Forms: C.020 if exposure/incident
6
Food storage and waste disposal
PRE: 4 M POST: 1 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Foodborne illness, pests, odours, biohazards, or lifting strain from garbage.
Controls
  • ARefrigerate promptly, check expiry dates, label/store food appropriately, and dispose of waste regularly.
  • AUse sealed bags and avoid overfilling.
  • PWear gloves for waste handling.
Required Forms: C.020 if illness/incident
