Cleaning

JSA Leader: Katie Snow, Manager · Revision 1
Revision focus: Updated to include fatigue/fitness, privacy/respectful workplace, chemical safety, biohazards, and back injury controls.
PPE Required
Gloves, eye protection, non-slip footwear, mask/respiratory protection if required by product/task
Emergency Contact
911
Emergency Meeting Point
To be determined at home office / work area
Tools & Equipment
Cleaning products, mop/bucket, vacuum, ladder/step stool, garbage bags, caution signs, first aid kit
Training / Skills Required
Cleaning procedures · Label/WHMIS awareness · First aid · Respectful workplace · Incident reporting
Approval Required
High risk work requires Supervisor/Management approval per risk matrix

Job Tasks
1
Pre-task inspection and fitness check
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Fatigue, illness, poor lighting, clutter, unknown chemicals, or working alone.
Controls
  • AConfirm worker is fit for work and understands task scope.
  • AInspect area for spills, obstacles, lighting, exits, and special hazards before starting.
  • AEstablish communication/check-in if working alone.
Required Forms: C.049 if fatigue · C.001 FLHA if needed
2
Using chemical cleaners
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Skin/eye irritation, inhalation, chemical reaction, spill, or improper storage.
Controls
  • ARead labels and follow product directions.
  • ADo not mix chemicals.
  • EVentilate area and keep containers closed when not in use.
  • PWear gloves and eye protection as required.
  • AStore chemicals securely and report spills.
Required Forms: C.020 if exposure/spill
3
Mopping floors / wet cleaning
PRE: 4 M POST: 1 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Slips, trips, falls, or public/worker exposure to wet floor.
Controls
  • EUse caution signs/barriers where others may enter.
  • AClean small sections and keep one dry path available.
  • PWear non-slip footwear.
  • ARemove signs only when floor is dry.
Required Forms: C.010 if near miss · C.020 if incident
4
Dusting high areas / reaching
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Falls from height, overreaching, shoulder/back strain, or falling objects.
Controls
  • EUse stable ladder/step stool appropriate to task.
  • AAvoid overreaching; reposition ladder and maintain three points of contact.
  • ADo not stand on chairs or unstable surfaces.
  • PEye protection as required.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment · C.020 if injury
5
Vacuuming / corded equipment
PRE: 4 M POST: 1 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Trips over cords, electric shock, noise, repetitive strain, or damaged equipment.
Controls
  • AInspect cord/equipment before use; remove defective equipment from service.
  • AKeep cords organized and out of walkways.
  • AUse proper posture and change hands/position.
  • PHearing protection if equipment/noise requires.
Required Forms: C.015 Equipment Inspection if issue · C.048 Back Injury Assessment
6
Trash, washroom, or biohazard clean-up
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Cuts, sharps, bodily fluids, biological exposure, odours, pests, or lifting injury.
Controls
  • ADo not reach blindly into bags or bins.
  • AUse sealed bags and avoid overfilling.
  • PWear gloves; use additional PPE for bodily fluids.
  • AReport sharps, blood/bodily fluids, or unusual waste to supervisor.
  • AUse proper lifting technique or reduce load size.
Required Forms: C.020 Incident Investigation · C.048 Back Injury Assessment
7
Interaction with workers, clients, or public while cleaning
PRE: 6 M POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Harassment, violence, disrespectful behaviour, privacy exposure, or unauthorized access to work areas.
Controls
  • AKeep communication respectful and report concerning behaviour.
  • ADo not enter private/restricted spaces without authorization.
  • AProtect confidential documents/items seen while cleaning.
  • AStop work and contact supervisor if unsafe.
Required Forms: C.028 Violence/Harassment · C.029 Privacy · C.039 if visitors involved
