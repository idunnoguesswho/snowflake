Lawn Maintenance

JSA Leader: Katie Snow, Manager · Revision 1
Revision focus: Updated to include fatigue/weather, equipment inspection, back injury prevention, public interaction, and fleet controls.
PPE Required
Eye protection, hearing protection, gloves, long pants, sturdy closed-toe footwear, weather/sun protection
Emergency Contact
911 · Supervisor contact
Emergency Meeting Point
To be determined at work area
Tools & Equipment
Lawn mower, trimmer/edger, rake, bags, approved fuel container/battery charger, first aid kit, phone
Training / Skills Required
Equipment operation · First aid · Heat/cold stress awareness · Back injury prevention · Driving/fleet controls if transporting equipment
Approval Required
High risk work requires Supervisor/Management approval per risk matrix

Job Tasks
1
Pre-task planning / weather and fitness check
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Fatigue, heat/cold stress, lightning, poor visibility, slippery ground, or working alone outdoors.
Controls
  • ACheck weather, daylight, ground conditions, and worker fitness before starting.
  • ADelay work during lightning, severe weather, excessive heat/cold, or unsafe ground conditions.
  • ATake breaks, hydrate, and use sun/weather protection.
  • AEstablish communication/check-in if working alone.
Required Forms: C.045 Inclement Weather · C.049 Fatigue Self-Assessment · C.001 FLHA if needed
2
Inspect equipment (mower, trimmer, fuel, cords/batteries)
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Cuts, electric shock, fuel leak, fire, thrown parts, or equipment failure.
Controls
  • AInspect guards, blades/line, wheels, handles, cords/batteries, fuel lines, and controls before use.
  • ARemove defective equipment from service and report.
  • AFollow manufacturer instructions.
  • PWear gloves during inspection/handling.
Required Forms: C.015 Equipment Inspection · C.020 if incident
3
Mowing lawn
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Flying debris, slips, rollover, contact with blade, noise, vibration, or pedestrian exposure.
Controls
  • AClear lawn of rocks, sticks, toys, hoses, and debris before mowing.
  • EKeep guards in place.
  • AKeep people/pets away from work area.
  • AAvoid steep slopes or mow across/with manufacturer guidance.
  • PEye protection, hearing protection, long pants, sturdy closed-toe footwear.
Required Forms: C.020 if incident
4
Trimming / edging
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Cuts, flying debris, noise, repetitive motion, and injury to bystanders.
Controls
  • EUse guards and correct line/blade.
  • AMaintain safe distance from people, windows, vehicles, and property.
  • AChange posture/hand position and take breaks.
  • PEye protection, hearing protection, gloves, long pants, sturdy footwear.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment · C.020 if incident
5
Raking, bagging, lifting, and disposing of yard waste
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Back strain, overexertion, blisters, allergens, pests, cuts, or trip hazards.
Controls
  • AUse ergonomic tools, switch sides, take breaks, and avoid twisting.
  • AKeep bags light; team lift or reduce load when needed.
  • AWatch for sharp objects, insects, and allergens.
  • PGloves; mask if allergy/dust risk.
Required Forms: C.048 Back Injury Assessment · C.020 if injury
6
Refueling or charging equipment
PRE: 9 H POST: 2 L ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Fire, explosion, fumes, burns, battery hazard, or environmental spill.
Controls
  • ETurn off engine and allow to cool before refueling.
  • ARefuel outdoors away from ignition sources; no smoking.
  • AUse approved fuel container and clean spills immediately.
  • ACharge batteries with approved charger in suitable area.
Required Forms: C.020 if spill/fire/incident
7
Travel / transport of lawn equipment
PRE: 12 H POST: 4 M ▾
Potential Hazards & Effects
Vehicle collision, unsecured load, fuel spill, fatigue, or 360 hazard before moving.
Controls
  • AFollow D.001 Driving, fleet, journey, and 360 controls.
  • ASecure equipment/fuel containers before travel.
  • AComplete required vehicle inspection, journey plan, fatigue check, and 360 walkaround when triggers apply.
Required Forms: C.049 · C.050 · C.051 · C.052
