E.004 · Emergency Response Plan

Severed or Crushed Appendage

DS Home Computing Ltd · SA03 SECOR · For internal use

HSMS Wiki › E Series › E.004
Source: A.001 Health and Safety Management System, Section 9 Emergency Preparedness.
📌

Purpose

Provide immediate response actions for this emergency condition. Workers shall follow the site lead or supervisor instructions and summon outside emergency assistance when required.

🏥

Emergency Facilities

Muster Point - Located across the road from the front entrance.

University of Alberta Hospital - 8440-112 Street, Edmonton, AB, T6G-2B7

Emergency equipment locations - See shop & office map for locations (11.9 & 11.10).

🧯

Emergency Equipment Reference

First aid kit: seek help from a certified first aid person.

⚠️

Emergency Response Procedure

Seek help from one of the Company’s first aid personnel

If the person’s injuries are deemed to be serious enough by the first aid responder, then call 911 and request that an ambulance be dispatched to the office

Notify the safety officer of the incident

📋

Reporting and Follow-Up

Notify the Safety Officer or site lead of the incident as soon as it is safe to do so.

Do not resume work until instructed by the site lead or supervisor.

Reassess the Field Level Hazard Assessment (F.L.H.A.) before resuming work when applicable.
