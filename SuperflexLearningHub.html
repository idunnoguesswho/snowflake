MEET SUPERFLEX

Superflex is the superhero living in YOUR brain. When Stuck Bots try to take over, Superflex has the power to fight back and keep your thinking flexible, calm, and kind.

  • Stay calm when things don't go your way
  • Try new ideas even when it's hard
  • Think about how others feel
  • Ask for help when you need it
  • STOP → THINK → TRY A NEW IDEA
🤖 The Stuck Bots (UnthinkaBots)
ROCK BRAIN
StuckBot
Rock Brain makes your thinking hard and stiff — like a rock! It sends signals that say "MY WAY ONLY!" and makes you not want to try new ideas or change your plan.
Rock Brain might make you...
  • Refuse to change your mind even when it's not working
  • Say "No! Only my idea!" to the group
  • Get stuck on doing things one way only
  • Forget to ask for help
⚡ Superflex T-FLEX Strategy: Solve another way • Pause or stop if asked • Follow the group plan • Self-talk: "It's okay, I can try their idea!"
GLASSMAN
ExplodaBot
Glassman is made of glass — and so are your reactions when this bot visits! Small problems feel HUGE. Glassman makes emotions too big for the situation.
Glassman might make you...
  • Yell or cry over small things
  • Slam things or stomp away
  • Quit when frustrated
  • Feel like everything is terrible
⚡ Superflex Calm Strategy: PAUSE → BREATHE → CHOOSE A CALM SOLUTION
Use Kool Q. Cumber's Cooling Cap: Deep breaths • Tighten/relax muscles • Ask for help
BRAIN EATER
DistractoBot
Brain Eater (D.O.F. — Destroyer of Fun!) munches on your attention. It makes you look around, play with things, and forget what you were supposed to be doing.
Brain Eater might make you...
  • Stop listening mid-sentence
  • Play with your eraser or fidget instead of working
  • Forget directions you just heard
  • Look around the room when you should focus
⚡ Focus Tron Strategy: LOOK → LISTEN → DO THE JOB
Focus Pocus • Take a brain break • Use a Distracter Shield • Move the distraction away
BODY SNATCHER
ImpulseBot
Body Snatcher tries to control your body and make you act before thinking! It moves your hands, feet, and mouth without asking your brain first.
Body Snatcher might make you...
  • Blurt out answers without raising your hand
  • Push ahead in line
  • Grab things without asking
  • Move around when you're supposed to be still
⚡ Superflex Body Strategy: STOP → THINK → MAKE A SAFE CHOICE
Hands in lap • Wait your turn • Think before acting
MEAN BEAN
MeanBot
Mean Bean is a sneaky, green MeanBot machine! It throws mean thoughts into your brain and tries to make those thoughts come out of your mouth as mean words or actions.
Mean Bean might make you...
  • Say hurtful things about someone's appearance
  • Gossip or tell mean stories
  • Call people names
  • Do mean things for no reason
⚡ Nice Light Strategy: Self-talk: "Everyone has mean thoughts — but I don't have to say them."
Activate Thought Catcher's Thought Net to snag those green mean thoughts!
WORRY WALL
WorryBot
Worry Wall builds a wall of worry in your brain. It makes small worries feel enormous and can trap your thinking so you can't move forward or enjoy the moment.
Worry Wall might make you...
  • Get stuck on "what ifs"
  • Avoid new situations because of fear
  • Feel like everything will go wrong
  • Have trouble sleeping or concentrating
⚡ Worry WiseWorm Strategy: Is it a Wee-Tiny Worry, a Wise Worry, or a WorryBot? • Worry Journal • Deep Worm Breath • Listen to brain and body

🧠 DBT Quick Skills
🛑 STOP Skill Distress
  • Stop what you're doing — freeze!
  • Take a step back — don't act yet
  • Observe — what am I feeling? What's happening?
  • Proceed mindfully — now act from your wise mind
💬 DEAR MAN Relationships
  • Describe the situation clearly
  • Express how you feel (I feel...)
  • Assert — ask for what you want
  • Reinforce — explain why it helps both of you
  • Mindful — stay focused on your goal
  • Appear confident — stand tall, speak clearly
  • Negotiate — be willing to give a little
💛 PLEASE Skills Wellness
  • Treat PhysicaL illness — see a doctor
  • Eating — balanced, not too much or too little
  • Avoid mood-Altering substances
  • Sleep — regular, balanced
  • Exercise — get your body moving every day
🎭 Opposite Action Emotion Reg
When emotions push you toward a harmful action — do the OPPOSITE.

😤 Angry? → Be gentle instead of attacking
😰 Scared? → Approach what you fear (safely)
😔 Sad/depressed? → Get active, reach out
😳 Ashamed? → Hold your head up, don't hide

Get your own URL in 5 minutes
This website is a single HTML file — no server, no database, no hosting fees. It runs 100% in the browser and works perfectly on iPhone. Choose any of these free hosting options:

⭐ GitHub Pages (Recommended)

  1. Go to github.com and create a free account
  2. Click New Repository → name it superflex-hub → Public
  3. Click Add file → Upload files
  4. Upload this HTML file, rename it to index.html
  5. Go to Settings → Pages → Source: main branch
  6. Your site is live at yourusername.github.io/superflex-hub 🎉

⚡ Netlify Drop (Fastest)

  1. Go to netlify.com/drop
  2. Drag and drop the HTML file onto the page
  3. Instantly get a URL like random-name.netlify.app
  4. Create a free account to keep the URL and customize it

📱 Cloudflare Pages

  1. Go to pages.cloudflare.com
  2. Create free account → Direct Upload
  3. Upload your HTML file
  4. Get yoursite.pages.dev URL
  5. Also works as a Progressive Web App (saveable to iPhone home screen!)
💡 Pro tip: After hosting, open the URL on your iPhone and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It will appear as a full-screen app icon — no App Store needed!
