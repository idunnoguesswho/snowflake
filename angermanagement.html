Anger Management

Back to Work

Big feelings are signals. The goal is to get safe, get calm, and choose the next right thing.

Use this page when anger starts climbing or after a hard moment. Start with the body, name the feeling, then repair what needs repair.

Stop Breathe Choose

Heat Check

What Level Is It?

1-2

Warm

I can talk. My body is tense, but I can still listen.

3-4

Hot

I need space. My words might come out sharp or fast.

5

Too Hot

I stop talking, move to a safe place, and use my calm-down plan.

Reset Plan

Five-Minute Calm Down

Step 1

Make Space

Put both feet on the floor, unclench hands, and step away from people or objects if needed.

Step 2

Cool The Body

Take five slow breaths, drink water, splash cool water, or press hands together for ten seconds.

Step 3

Use Words

Say: "I felt mad because ____. I need ____. I can try ____."
