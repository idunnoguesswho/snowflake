Life Skills -  Laundry Legend
Earn this badge by helping with laundry at home!
🎯 Goal:
Learn how to sort, wash, dry, and put away your clothes.
What You’ll Do (Steps):
Sort your laundry
Ask a grown-up to show you where the laundry goes.
Separate darks, lights, and towels.
Start the washing machine
Ask for help reading the settings.
Add clothes and detergent.
Press start!
Move laundry to the dryer
Wait until the washer is done.
Move the clothes to the dryer (or drying rack).
Start the drying process.
Fold the clothes
Match socks
Fold shirts, pants, and towels
Put everything away
Put your clothes in the right drawers or shelves.
✅ 
🧠 Helpful Hints:
You can do this with a grown-up the first time.
Use a checklist if it helps!
It’s okay to ask for help.
📸 Bonus Challenge:
Take a photo of your neatly folded clothes or sorted laundry baskets!
