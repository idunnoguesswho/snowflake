Who We Are

A family business built on trust

DS Home Computing Ltd is a family-owned and operated IT firm founded in 1996 in Edmonton, Alberta, with a branch office near Bruderheim, Alberta. For nearly 30 years we have delivered tailored computing solutions to individuals and businesses across the region.

Our guiding principle: exceptional customer service, driven by customer success. We are a one-stop shop for information technology — from strategic planning to hands-on hardware support — with face-to-face, real people who never leave you on hold for more than three minutes.

We believe technology should work for you, not the other way around.
